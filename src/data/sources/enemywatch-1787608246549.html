🇮🇳/🇮🇷 | India has bought an average of 540,000 barrels of Iranian oil per day over the past month: Planning chief:

Head of Iran’s Plan and Budget Organization Davoud Manzoor said India has purchased an average of 540,000 barrels of Iranian oil per day over the past month, arguing that the situation is not as bleak as some portray it.

Manzoor said the fact that no new oil cargoes have been loaded in recent weeks does not mean that Iran’s oil exports have stopped. He added that Iran has enough oil available for sale for at least the next two months.

Responding to concerns that the government would be unable to collect taxes, Manzoor said, “When have we ever collected taxes during the first two or three months of the fiscal year? Tax collection always begins from the fourth or fifth month, in the second half of the year.”

🇸🇦 | Saudi oil tankers reroute around Africa amid disruptions:

At least nine Saudi Arabian very large crude carriers (VLCCs) are currently sailing around Africa to load crude at Sidi Kerir in northern Egypt or through ship-to-ship transfers in the eastern Mediterranean. Five of these oil tankers, which had returned from East and South Asia, decided 23 days ago to take the route around Africa due to Yemen’s blockade against Saudi Arabia. However, they have yet to reach Gibraltar. Four other oil tankers arrived in the Sea of Oman 10 days ago to receive oil from the Strait of Hormuz through ship-to-ship transfers. However, due to the slow and limited flow of this process, they have now changed course and opted for the much longer route around Africa to reach their cargo.
