The United Nations Is on the Brink of Bankruptcy Due to the United States and China — The Wall Street Journal

The UN could run out of funds by mid-August.

The main financial challenges stem from delayed contributions from the United States and China, the organization’s two largest donors.

Washington’s outstanding debt exceeds $4 billion, while Beijing still owes approximately $455 million after recently paying $850 million.

UN Secretary-General António Guterres has warned that the organization is effectively engaged in a “race toward insolvency.” Around 3,000 positions have already been cut.

The situation is further aggravated by the UN’s inability to borrow money, while the majority of its expenditures are allocated to personnel costs.
