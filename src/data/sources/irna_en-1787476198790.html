📡 Iran doubles defense weapons production in one year: Defense Ministry spokesperson

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran has doubled its production of defense weapons and equipment over the past year, a military official says, emphasizing that strengthening national power is essential to confronting foreign powers seeking to undermine the country’s independence.


Speaking in an interview late on Friday, spokesperson for the Ministry of Defense and Armed Forces Logistics, Brigadier General Reza Talaei-Nik, said Iran’s defense industry has become a key pillar of national security and military preparedness. He described the rapid production and replacement of domestically manufactured weapons and ammunition as one of the industry’s main strengths.
​
According to the official, Iran’s defense production capacity has continued to operate alongside the use of weapons and equipment in the field, allowing supplies to be replenished rapidly when needed.
​
Talaei-Nik said defense weapons and equipment production doubled during the past year despite attacks on limited sections of Iran’s defense industry during the 12-day war.
​
He said the country’s ability to maintain a stable supply of weapons and military equipment was among the key factors contributing to its battlefield resilience. This capability, he added, has been supported by cooperation among the Armed Forces, the Ministry of Defense, knowledge-based companies, private-sector firms, and scientific and research centers.
​
Some strategic defense products and wartime supplies experienced more than threefold growth in production during the conflict, the spokesperson noted.
​
Talaei-Nik said Iran’s defense industry has expanded from producing only 31 basic products before the 1979 Islamic Revolution to manufacturing more than 1,000 advanced systems, weapons and ammunition today.
​
He also said that production costs for some Iranian defense systems are up to 20 times lower than those of comparable foreign products.
​
The spokesperson further said Iran managed to withstand the recent conflict despite the United States, the Israeli regime and their supporters having military budgets more than 100 times larger, attributing the outcome to domestic production capabilities and national resilience.

📄 Iran calls US new sanctions assertion of extraterritorial sovereignty over independent UN member states

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has criticized the United States’ latest economic sanctions against Iran, describing the measure as “an assertion of extraterritorial sovereignty over every independent Member State of the United Nations.”


In an X post on Saturday, Baqaei said, “The United States’ declaration of new economic sanctions on Iran is far more than continued unlawful ’economic warfare’ against a single country. It is an assertion of extraterritorial sovereignty over every independent Member State of the United Nations.”
​
He wrote, “No State may lawfully compel foreign banks, enterprises, or airports - each subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of its own sovereign - to renounce lawful commerce with a third State.”
​
According to Baqaei, “Such secondary sanctions find no foundation in international law. They violate the principle of sovereign equality enshrined in Article 2(1) of the UN Charter and breach the customary prohibition on intervention affirmed by the International Court of Justice in the Nicaragua case. Economic coercion designed to force a sovereign State to alter its lawful policy choices constitutes an outright internationally wrongful act.”
​
He further noted, “When combined with a naval blockade amounting to military aggression, these demands reduce the sovereignty of all other States to something provisional, conditional, and revocable at the whim of another power. Compliance purchases no immunity or respect; it merely concedes that one’s banks, enterprises, and airports operate only under a foreign licence.”
​
He warned, “The end result would be the complete erosion of sovereignty as the foundational basis of the UN-based inter-State system, and a recipe for an abysmal return to full-scale classic colonialism.”

📡 Iran, Russia near signing executive contract for Rasht-Astara railway: Envoy

📌 Moscow, IRNA – Iran and Russia are nearing the signing of an executive contract for the construction of the Rasht-Astara railway, a key project under the International North-South Transport Corridor, Iranian Ambassador to Russia Kazem Jalali says.


The most important joint project between Tehran and Moscow within the framework of the International North-South Corridor is the Rasht-Astara railway project, Jalali told the Moscow-based Vedomosti newspaper, carried by IRNA late on Friday, adding that the two sides had reached an agreement on its executive contract and were planning to sign it in the presence of officials from both countries.
​
Jalali said talks and practical measures were currently underway on several infrastructure and economic projects, including energy cooperation and the transfer of Russian gas to Iran.
​
He stressed that Iran-Russia cooperation was not limited to political ties, saying the two countries had specific transport, energy and infrastructure projects on their agenda.
​
The goal of the contacts and negotiations between the two countries is to ensure that joint projects, such as the construction of units 2 and 3 of the Bushehr nuclear power plant, do not stop and are pursued more rapidly after the regional situation is stabilized, he said.

🖥 Book of poems by martyred Leader of Islamic Revolution published

📌 Tehran, IRNA — A collection of poems by Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei has been released by the Islamic Revolution Publications on the 40th day marking the farewell ceremony for the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution.


Titled From Amin’s Poetry Notebook , the volume features a selection of the martyred Leader’s poems, prefaced by an introduction by Gholam-Ali Haddad-Adel, the head of the Academy of Persian Language and Literature.
​
According to the publisher’s public relations office, the collection combines the poems with calligraphy created by prominent Iranian visual artists.
​
The artworks were produced during a one-day workshop hosted and directed by Masoud Nejabati, the artistic director of the Islamic Revolution Publications, during the days of national mourning and farewell ceremonies.
​
The book includes works by several noted Iranian artists, including Hamid Ghorbanpour, Hossein Chaman-Khah, Mojtaba Majlesi, Mohammad Rouholamim, Hamed Maghrouri, Mohammad-Reza Chitsaz, Farhoud Moghadam, Majid Keshavarzi, Mahmoud Bazdar, Mehran Pendar, Alireza Hesaraki, Saeed Karimi, Hosseinreza Vanaki, and Hajar Salimi-Namin.
​
The book is available at bookstores across Iran and through the publisher’s online platform at https://khameneibook.ir/ .

🌍 Commemoration ceremony for martyrs of Minab school held in Nicaragua

📌 London, IRNA — A commemoration ceremony for the 168 martyred students of the Shajareh Tayyebeh elementary school in Minab, southern Iran, was held at the National Autonomous University of Nicaragua (UNAN), with the attendance of students, university professors, members of the National Assembly of Nicaragua, and the ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran.


The ceremony coincided with the 168th day since the attack on the Shajareh Tayyebeh elementary school in Minab.
​
More than 250 students, faculty members, and members of the Nicaragua–Iran Parliamentary Friendship Group participated in the event.
​
Ramin Zare’, the ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran to Nicaragua, detailed the dimensions of the US and Israeli regime’s military aggression against Iran’s territorial integrity, and said this incident is not merely a human tragedy, but a serious test for the credibility of international law and international humanitarian law.
​
The Minab incident was not simply an attack on a school building; it also constituted a violation of international legal frameworks and a failure of international organizations to fulfill their duty to protect civilians, he said.
​
“We are not seeking revenge; we are demanding accountability. We do not advocate lawlessness in return, but rather the enforcement of law and justice. We reject the normalization of aggression and instead call for adherence to the UN Charter and international humanitarian law.”
​
Genuine peace can only be realized when aggression incurs consequences, when crimes are not left unpunished, when the rights of victims are duly acknowledged, and when international legal norms are upheld universally and without bias.
