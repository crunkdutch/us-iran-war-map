🇮🇷 | IRGC:

The IRGC has released videos and images of the unmanned submarine.

According to information released by domestic news agencies, the unmanned submarine in question is the Dive-LD, manufactured by Anduril.

The unmanned submarine is 5.8 meters long and 1.2 meters in diameter, with an endurance of up to 10 days and a maximum operating depth of 6,000 meters.

Its modular design allows it to be rapidly reconfigured for a range of missions, including underwater intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance (ISR), mine countermeasures, anti-submarine warfare (ASW), and high-resolution seabed mapping. Given its deployment in the Strait of Hormuz, reconnaissance and mine countermeasures are likely among its primary missions.

The Dive-LD is classified as a Large Displacement Unmanned Undersea Vehicle (LDUUV). In 2024, the Pentagon selected the system under the Replicator program, which aims to mass-produce low-cost autonomous and unmanned systems. The U.S. Navy’s Unmanned Undersea Vehicle Squadron 1 (UUVRON-1) received the system in 2025.
