📡 PM Modi says India is ready to ‘expand and diversify’ trade relations with Iran

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi, emphasizing his country’s long-standing relations with Iran, has announced New Delhi’s readiness to expand and diversify trade ties with Tehran.


Modi made the remarks in a post on his X network, following a meeting with Iran’s President Masoud Pezeshkian, on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, on Monday.
​
Referring to his talks, Modi said he reaffirmed to Pezeshkian India’s “commitment to strengthening India’s long-standing friendship with Iran across diverse sectors”, especially diversifying the trade basket in the times to come.
​
According to the social media post, the Indian premier and the Iranian president also discussed the current situation in the region, with Modi ensuring “India will continue to support all efforts aimed at ensuring lasting peace.”
​
Indian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal also described the Modi–Pezeshkian meeting as productive and fruitful, saying the two leaders reviewed various aspects of bilateral relations and the latest developments in the region.
​
Jaiswal added that Modi, in his meeting with Pezeshkian, reiterated New Delhi’s position on the need to resolve all issues through dialogue and diplomacy.

🎙 Iran, Mongolia foreign ministers meet on sidelines of SCO Summit in Bishkek

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and his Mongolian counterpart Battsetseg Batmunkh met in the Kyrgyz capital of Bishkek on Monday on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) Summit.


During the meeting, the two top diplomats discussed recent developments in the region and the issues of mutual interest.
​
The 26th Summit of heads of state of the SCO kicked off in Bishkek on August 31 and will run through September 1 as Kyrgyzstan is the organization’s periodic chair in 2026.
​
This year’s summit slogan is “25 Years of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization: Together for Peace, Development, and Sustainable Prosperity.”
​
President Masoud Pezeshkian arrived in Bishkek on Monday to attend the Shanghai Cooperation Organization and Shanghai Plus Summit, where he is due to deliver a speech and hold bilateral meetings.
​
Upon his arrival, Pezeshkian was welcomed by Kyrgyzstan’s deputy cabinet chairman. His two-day visit will focus on the summit of heads of state from the Shanghai Cooperation Organization and Shanghai Plus, where he will speak and hold bilateral talks with a number of participating leaders and the presidents of Kyrgyzstan, Russia and Tajikistan on the sidelines of the summit.
​
At the invitation of the Kyrgyz president, the Iranian president will also attend the official opening ceremony of the sixth World Nomad Games during the trip.

⚡️ President believes ‘neither war nor peace’ situation not in Iran’s interest: Official

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The deputy chief of protocol at the Iranian president’s office says President Masoud Pezeshkian believes the current “neither war nor peace” situation benefits no one in the country, and he intends to seize any opportunity to move past the crisis and uncertainty.


President Pezishkian believes that if there is a window or path for genuine diplomacy amid the efforts being made by mediators, Iran should seize the opportunity and reach a more stable situation, Seyed Abbas Mousavi told IRNA.
​
Iran believes in diplomacy and is also fully prepared for it, but is clear that the other side shows no seriousness, Mousavi said. However, if Pezeshkian speaks of peace, it does not mean that all our problems with the Americans will be resolved by signing a memorandum or even a formal agreement, the official further added.
​
Regarding Iranians living abroad, the official said that most Iranians abroad – except for a specific minority – are patriotic, highly educated, and cultured individuals. They can return to their country with the support and guarantee of the president himself, stay as long as they wish, and travel freely, he added.
​
The issue of the return of Iranians abroad is being pursued seriously and decisively by Pezeshkian, so that the country can benefit from their capacities and expertise, Mousavi said.
​
On whether President Pezeshkian will attend the upcoming UN General Assembly given current Iran-US tensions, Mousavi said, “We are still finalizing whether to participate, and if so, at what level and with how many delegates.”

🖥 Iran will deliver a decisive response to any new attack: Commander

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Commander of the Iranian Army’s Air Defense Force Brigadier General Alireza Elhami warns that the Armed Forces will deliver a decisive response to any new aggression by the enemy.


General Elhami made the remarks during a ceremony at the mausoleum of Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini in Tehran, marking the anniversary of the establishment of the Khatam al-Anbiya Air Defense Headquarters and honoring those martyred in imposed wars on Iran.
​
He said the Armed Forces had resisted a major military challenge during the recent 40-day war, attributing the outcome to domestic capabilities, military experience and national cohesion.
​
Elhami said more than 90 percent of the air-defense successes against manned and unmanned aircraft and various projectiles during the recent war were achieved using entirely Iranian-made products.
​
He also said recent battlefield experience had been incorporated into operational systems and equipment, while other lessons were being developed through military research and defense institutions.
​
Speaking to reporters, Elhami said Iran’s air-defense forces had recently unveiled several systems and capabilities developed during the 40-day war.
​
He said the systems were intended to convey a message of friendship, stability and peace to neighboring and friendly countries, while sending a message of “firmness and power” to those threatening Iran’s borders.
​
“We have a duty that whenever the enemy makes a mistake and steps beyond the path of reason and what it has promised, it will receive a forceful and decisive response from the armed forces,” Elhami said.
​
He added that any similar action in the future would again receive a decisive response.

The statement also quoted the Iranian president as expressing appreciation for Pakistan’s diplomatic efforts toward regional peace and security.
