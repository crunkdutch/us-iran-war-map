🔊 Iran will treat support for US economic pressure as ‘act of war’: Top security official

📌 Tehran, IRNA - Iran’s top security official warns that Tehran will regard any country’s participation in or support for America’s economic pressure campaign against the Iranian people as an act of war.


“If the economic war continues, not a single drop of oil will be exported٫ neither through the Strait of Hormuz nor from anywhere in the Persian Gulf,” Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, wrote on X on Sunday.
​
“Iran will regard any country’s participation in or support for America’s economic war against the Iranian people as an act of war,” he added.
​
The post echoed the warning Rezaei issued to the US and its regional allies the previous day.
​
In a televised interview on Saturday, Rezaei warned that any country joining the US economic war would be considered an “enemy” by Tehran.
​
Rezaei also reiterated that the Strait of Hormuz remains closed and will not reopen until the United States fulfills its commitments under a peace memorandum of understanding signed between Tehran and Washington in June, adding that Washington must act first.

🌍 Israeli airstrikes, artillery fire hit southern Lebanon

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Israeli warplanes, artillery units and ground forces have carried out attacks against several areas in southern Lebanon, Lebanese media reported.


According to Lebanon’s National News Agency, Israeli warplanes late on Sunday targeted a house in the Deir neighborhood of Nabatieh al-Fawqa.
​
The agency also reported intermittent artillery shelling of areas of Ali al-Taher and Kfar Reman.
​
Meanwhile, Israeli forces carried out an explosive demolition operation in the southern town of al-Taybeh, Lebanese media said. Israeli forces also set fire to several houses in the town of Haddatha, according to the reports.
​
Al Mayadeen reported that Israeli artillery shelled areas near al-Dabsha and Ali al-Taher heights as well as the town of Mansouri in southern Lebanon.
​
No immediate reports were available on possible casualties or the extent of material damage from the latest attacks.
​
Israel launched a wide-scale offensive against Lebanon on March 2, killing and wounding Lebanese civilians, displacing more than one million people and continuing to occupy parts of southern Lebanon.

🎙 Official: 2,400 infrastructure, economic projects unveiled in Iran’s Bushehr

📌 Bushehr, IRNA – As many as 2,400 infrastructure and economic projects have been launched in Iran’s southern Bushehr Province during Government Week, with a total investment of more than $13.06 billion, the provincial governor has announced.


Speaking at a ceremony marking Government Week projects in Bushehr County, Arsalan Zare said 205 economic projects account for over $13.06 billion of the investment and are expected to create employment for 6,900 people.
​
He said investment in this year’s Government Week projects has increased by 263 percent compared with last year, while employment opportunities have doubled.
​
Zare highlighted the provincial administration’s efforts to advance infrastructure development, complete unfinished projects and attract further investment, stressing that cooperation between government officials and local residents has helped accelerate development initiatives.
​
A total of 2,195 infrastructure projects, valued at $1.3 billion, are also ready for operation across Bushehr Province, he said.
​
The projects cover key sectors including education, housing, roads, ports, healthcare, agriculture and tourism, providing new infrastructure and public services for residents.
​
Zare expressed appreciation to President Masoud Pezeshkian, his administration and the people of Bushehr for their support and cooperation.
​
He also voiced hope that stronger coordination and joint efforts would help accelerate development and improve public services across the province.

📄 Official: 787 construction projects set to open in Iran’s Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari Province

📌 Shahr-e Kord, IRNA — The Deputy Governor of Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari Province, Ardeshir Azar, has announced that 787 construction projects worth 50 trillion rials have been prepared for inauguration in the province to mark the Government Week (August 24–28).


On the occasion of the Government Week, construction work will begin on 107 new projects worth 14 trillion rials across the province, Azar told reporters on Sunday.
​
Since the beginning of President Pezeshkian’s administration, 3,315 projects have been inaugurated in Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari, he added.
​
The total number of completed projects will rise to 4,130, with a total value of 235 trillion rials, he noted, he said, adding that during the same period, work has begun on 1,997 projects across the province, with a total value of 250 trillion rials.
​
The total number of projects underway will increase to 2,104, he stressed.
​
The province has 12 national projects, including the Shahr-e Kord–Sefid Dasht–Mobarakeh railway, he said, adding that 33.8 trillion rials were allocated to these projects last year.

📌 Shahr-e-Kord, IRNA – The governor of Borujen County in the southwestern Iranian Province of Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari has announced the inauguration and operation of 52 projects in the fields of infrastructure, tourism, education, industry, and agriculture, simultaneously with the beginning of the Government Week.


Masoud Zamani made the announcement at an inauguration ceremony for Borujen projects on Monday, held in the presence of the provincial governor’s deputy coordinator for economic affairs.

The province has, so far, inaugurated and operated 3,315 projects during the administration of President Masoud Pezeshkian, which will increase to 4,130 with the addition of this year’s Government Week projects.
