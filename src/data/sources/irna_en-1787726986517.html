🎙 Iran, Oman agree on temporary maritime corridor in Strait of Hormuz

📌 Tehran, IRNA – During a visit by Omani foreign minister to Iran on Tuesday, the two sides issued a joint statement declaring an agreement on establishing a temporary joint maritime corridor and implementing a joint project to clear mines from the Strait of Hormuz.


According to the joint statement, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and his Omani counterpart, Sayyid Badr Hamad Al-Busaidi, held ‘constructive consultations’ focused on the resumption of safe navigation through the Strait of Hormuz, while preserving the sovereignty and sovereign rights of both nations.
​
The consultations focused on the importance both countries attach to the resumption of safe navigation through the Strait of Hormuz while preserving their sovereignty and sovereign rights.
​
The two Ministers discussed a phased framework that could provide a practical and implementable basis for moving forward, in light of the current situation in the Strait resulting from the recent war and its tragic repercussions.
​
The proposed framework includes the establishment of a joint temporary navigational corridor through the Strait of Hormuz and an agreement to implement a joint project to clear the Strait of mines. Technical negotiations between the two sides would continue with a view to agreeing on a permanent navigational corridor and future administration of the Strait, as well as a mechanism for information-sharing, traffic management, and the provision of relevant navigational and security services.
​
In this context, both sides underscored the importance of convening joint discussions with regional states bordering the waters of the Persian Gulf. They further stressed the need to uphold applicable international law and respect the sovereign rights of the coastal states.

🎙 Ghalibaf: Iran, Iraq will play decisive role in emerging regional order as US hegemony fails

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has declared that Iran and Iraq are poised to play a decisive role in shaping a new regional order, emphasizing that the United States has suffered a definitive defeat in its military and political war against the Islamic Republic.


Speaking on Tuesday during a high-level meeting in Tehran with Faeq Zidan, the Head of Iraq’s Supreme Judicial Council, Ghalibaf highlighted the profound historical bonds between the two nations and the shifting geopolitical landscape of West Asia.
​
Ghalibaf asserted that the United States initiated its recent war against Iran with the explicit intent of overthrowing the Iranian political system and expanding American hegemony across the Islamic world.
​
However, he stated that this objective was thoroughly thwarted.
​
"Thanks to the blood of the martyrs, the determination of the valiant Iranian nation, and the efforts of the Resistance Front, they clearly failed, and the entire world has acknowledged this reality," Ghalibaf said.
​
Addressing the ongoing US campaign of economic pressure, the Iranian Parliament speaker dismissed American threats.
​
"America failed in its military and political war against Iran, and it will also fail in its economic actions," he declared.
​
Describing the current geopolitical climate as a "sensitive and historic" juncture, Qalibaf pointed to clear signs of a new regional order taking shape.
​
He specifically welcomed the impending withdrawal of US-led coalition forces from Iraq, calling it a "historical honor for the Iraqi government and people," and expressed hope that this exit would be complete, covering both Iraqi land and airspace.
​
He also warned of the broader implications of the recent war, noting that the Zionist regime’s strategic map involves dragging other regional countries into war.
​
"If Israel had succeeded in its war against Iran, it would have moved on to target other countries in the region," he cautioned.
​
To counter these threats, Qalibaf urged Islamic nations to set aside divisive issues.
​
Highlighting the unity displayed during the war on Gaza, he recalled the words of the late Lieutenant General Qasem Soleimani that "Shia and Sunni are not only brothers, but we would give our lives for the Sunni."
​
Zidan, for his part, conveyed a strong message of solidarity from the Iraqi government and people to Iran in the wake of the recent US-Israeli war of aggression.
​
He affirmed Baghdad’s readiness to deepen cooperation with Tehran, noting that strong Iran-Iraq relations create a foundation for broader security and cooperation agreements across the region.
​
"Cooperation, agreement, and coalition-building among regional countries is the only way out of insecurity, and I believe this reality is very close," Zidan stated.
​
Zidan also paid solemn tribute to the recent, massive funeral processions held in Iraq for the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, describing the turnout as a spontaneous and deeply popular expression of respect.
​
"We will never forget the positive stances of the Islamic Republic of Iran toward Iraq, nor will we forget the Iranian martyrs, especially General Soleimani, who was martyred in Iraq," Zidan said.
​
He described the joint martyrdom of General Soleimani and Abu Mahdi al-Muhandis as a "divine message" that carries the profound solidarity of the two nations.
​
Concluding the meeting, Zidan emphasized that the harmony and unity between the Iranian and Iraqi peoples is an "eternal solidarity" that will continue to anchor the region's future.

🔊 Israeli airstrikes, artillery shelling target Southern Lebanon

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Israeli regime’s fighter jets and artillery have targeted areas in southern Lebanon, according to Lebanese media reports.


Lebanon’s Al-Manar TV channel reported late on Tuesday that Israeli warplanes bombed the town of Mansouri, while Israeli artillery simultaneously shelled the same area.
​
The attacks come amid continued Israeli military aggression in southern Lebanon. Since March 2, the Israeli regime has launched a wide-ranging offensive against Lebanon, killing and injuring Lebanese civilians and displacing more than one million people. Parts of southern Lebanon also remain under Israeli occupation.
​
Meanwhile, Lebanon and the Israeli regime signed a US-mediated framework agreement last month aimed at ending the conflict between the Israeli regime and Hezbollah. The agreement followed several rounds of direct negotiations between the two sides in Washington.
​
The deal has faced criticism over its unclear legal status and the absence of a specific timetable for the withdrawal of Israeli forces from Lebanese territory.

📡 Pezeshkian seeks to bring marginalized Iranians back into society’s mainstream: VP

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Vice President for Rural Development Affairs and Disadvantaged Regions Abdolkarim Hosseinzadeh says President Masoud Pezeshkian’s administration is seeking to bring marginalized groups back into the mainstream of Iranian society and use the capacities of all Iranians to advance the country.


Speaking to the Presidential Office’s Communications and Information Department, Hosseinzadeh said Pezeshkian had emphasized since the beginning of his tenure that groups pushed to the margins should be returned to the center of society.
​
“Our view is that all those who are not in the mainstream of the economy, society, culture and politics must return to the mainstream of society. We must spread the umbrella of being Iranian over all of Iran, and the only criterion should be one thing: being Iranian,” he said.
​
Hosseinzadeh said measures pursued over the past two years included establishing a nationwide system to monitor deprivation, preparing territorial development plans for all 31 provinces, implementing the “Empowering Soldier” initiative, identifying children left out of education and expanding educational justice programs.
​
He said restoring human and social capital, using the capacities of Iranians inside and outside the country, strengthening national belonging, and moving beyond ethnic, religious, and political approaches in governance were among Pezeshkian’s priorities.
​
“Iran will become powerful when all Iranians consider themselves to have a share in its destiny,” Hosseinzadeh said.
​
He also quoted President Pezeshkian as saying, “We are beautiful together and alongside one another,” stressing that inclusive participation was essential to the country’s future.
