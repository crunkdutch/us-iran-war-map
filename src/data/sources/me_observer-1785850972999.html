⚡️ *In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful* *Statement by an official spokesman for the Islamic Jihad Movement in Palestine* What has been announced regarding an agreement between the factions and the enemy in the media is inaccurate. We…
⚡️ Outwardly, the agreement appears good for Israel, given that this agreement includes the complete dismantling of Hamas's weapons.
However, there are two problems:
1. Anyone who believes that Hamas has suddenly abandoned its desire to destroy us is making a grave mistake.
Who guarantees that Hamas will not return stronger during the period of calm, and eventually, when it accumulates enough power to threaten Israel, regain control of Gaza again, as happened with the Palestinian Authority?
Will it truly abandon its weapons? Will it stop building terrorist tunnels in Gaza after the Israeli army withdraws from the Strip?
2. Is there anyone who guarantees that the new authority will be free of terrorism, as the authority will remain Islamic, and in its ideology - as is the case for many Gaza residents - they want to drive the Jews in Israel into the sea.
In short:
Hamas, despite its apparent dismantling of weapons, has not abandoned its ideology.
This ideology is very strong, to the extent that we can be almost certain that it will not stop trying to destroy us, by any means.
This agreement, in short, raises doubts.
Yair Goldblatt
