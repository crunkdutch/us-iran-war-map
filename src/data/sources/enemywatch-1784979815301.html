🇮🇷 | Around four hours ago, following what American officials described as Iran’s involvement in the downing of an Apache helicopter over the Strait of Hormuz, America launched a series of limited strikes against targets in southern Iran.

Reports indicated explosions and air defense activity across parts of Hormozgan Province, particularly in Sirik, Qeshm Island, Jask, and Kuh Mobarak, with officials stating that several locations were struck by enemy projectiles. According to local reports, at least six explosions were heard on Qeshm Island, while state sources confirmed the fall of a projectile in Sirik. There were also unconfirmed reports of an explosion in Ahvaz.

The IRGC Aerospace Force announced that a “heavy response” to the enemy’s actions would be delivered in the coming moments. Informed sources stated that the projectiles striking Qeshm were apparently launched from fighter aircraft.

Preliminary reports suggested that the following locations may have been among the intended targets:

• Sirik Naval Base

• Jask Naval Base

• Bandar Abbas air defense positions

• Qeshm coastal missile positions

Subsequent clarifications indicated that no commercial port on Qeshm Island was targeted. Informed sources stated that earlier reports of an attack on a commercial port were incorrect and stemmed from confusion following the six explosions heard on the island.

In Sirik, local reports stated that two water tanks in the Bomani district were hit, resulting in a disruption of drinking water supplies to the area.

Meanwhile, reports of strikes on Minab were denied by local authorities. Officials stated that no attack on the city had been reported, contradicting some of the earlier circulating claims.

The situation has since largely returned to calm, although assessments regarding damage and the exact targets involved remain ongoing.
