🎙 IRGC targets, stops two violating oil tankers in Strait of Hormuz

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says two oil tankers violating regulations had been struck and stopped after ignoring its warnings.


According to the statement issued by the IRGC, in the early hours of Friday, two violating tankers proceeded along an unsafe and illegal route, ignoring Iranian warnings, assuming that they could pass through an undeclared path under the aerial escort of “the child-killing US army”. The statement added they were subsequently struck and halted.
​

​
The IRGC Navy concluded the statement with a strong warning to the US military, stating, “The illegal interventions and orders of the child-killing US Army to vessels in the region will not go unanswered”.
​

The Iranian Armed Forces have in turn targeted vessels that defy Iran’s terms for passing through the strait.

🌍 Iran joins world AI cooperation organization

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to China has signed the founding charter of the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO) on behalf of the government of the Islamic Republic of Iran, officially registering the country as a member state of the newly established body.


Meeting with Chinese Deputy Foreign Minister Liu Bin after the signing ceremony on Friday, Ambassador Abdolreza Rahmani-Fazli said Iran was ready to actively participate in the scientific, research, and applied development of artificial intelligence, as well as in the creation of international mechanisms to ensure fair and equitable access to this technology.
​
The envoy emphasized that Iran’s membership in WAICO reflects its commitment to constructive engagement in new technologies and to using AI capabilities for sustainable development.
​
The World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO) was established on July 16, 2026, on the sidelines of the World Artificial Intelligence Conference in Shanghai, following an initiative by China.

🎙 Iraq’s PMF official says funeral of Iran’s martyred Leader symbolized Shia unity

​
Al-Ghanami said the Iraqi people, particularly PMF fighters, have long admired Iran’s martyred Leader, describing him as not only the Leader of a revolution but also a symbol of resistance against oppression for oppressed nations, including Iraq.
​

​
Referring to this year’s Arbaeen pilgrimage, Al-Ghanami described the annual event as the world’s largest gathering of Muslims and Shia believers, saying it represents unity, solidarity, and brotherhood beyond national, ethnic, and linguistic differences.
​
He added that Iraq remains committed to welcoming millions of pilgrims from around the world, providing services rooted in the values of Ashura and devotion to the infallible households of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).
​
According to Iranian officials, more than 1.3 million Arbaeen pilgrims have crossed through the Shalamcheh and Chazabeh border crossings in Khuzestan Province so far.

According to Lebanon’s Al Mayadeen TV network, an explosion occurred in the town of Zawtar al-Sharqiyah in southern Lebanon. The broadcaster also reported that the regime’s artillery shelled the Mashaa al-Mansouri area in the Tyre district.

He noted that the timing of the attacks, ahead of planned talks in Rome on advancing the trilateral framework, sent negative signals and undermined international efforts to preserve security and stability in southern Lebanon.
​
The Lebanese president called on the guarantor states and the international community to fulfill their responsibilities by taking action to halt violations that threaten diplomatic progress.
​
Local media also reported powerful explosions across several villages in southern Lebanon on Friday, with blasts heard as far as the city of Sidon.
​
The framework agreement, brokered by the United States last month, has faced criticism over implementation and troop withdrawal provisions.

🔊 Second International Conference on Iranian–Islamic Civilization calls for articles

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Cultural Center in Austria has issued an official call for papers for the Second International Conference on Iranian–Islamic Civilization, with a focus on the “Women's Agency in Iranian civilization.”


The event will take place on January 31, 2027, in a hybrid format, with sessions in Vienna, Tehran, and also online, and invites submissions from scholars, researchers, and postgraduate students worldwide.
​
Its second edition offers a documented, interdisciplinary view of women’s contributions to Iranian civilization, with themes spanning governance, cultural memory, science, health, and spirituality.
​
The conference follows the success of its first edition, held in February 2026 in Vienna, which brought together university professors, Iranologists, and researchers from various countries.
​
During that first edition, participants explored themes such as cultural identity, Persian language and literature, heritage studies, philosophy, arts, medicine, science, spirituality, and the future of Iranology.
