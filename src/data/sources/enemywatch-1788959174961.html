• Arab sources reported renewed Yemeni drone attacks against Saudi Arabia.

• U.S. media reporting said the Yemeni attacks struck oil-storage facilities in Abha.

• Saudi Civil Defense reportedly issued an alert through the National Early Warning Platform in Khamis Mushait Governorate at 06:28.

• The alerts in Khamis Mushait were reportedly lifted after being active for three minutes.

🇮🇷 Iran

• The spokesperson for the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps announced that if any vessel crosses the sanctioned zone in the Strait of Hormuz, the provision of maritime, insurance and support services to that vessel will be halted.

• The spokesperson said that even if such a vessel later sails through the Strait of Hormuz, it would be deprived of receiving these services.

• According to the statement, the sanctioned zone begins approximately from the Chabahar side and extends to parts of the Sea of Oman and Arabian Sea.

• Sardar Mohebbi, deputy head of the IRGC Public Relations Department and spokesperson for the IRGC, said media has a decisive role in strengthening public resolve during hybrid warfare.

• He said modern warfare has a combined nature and encompasses different arenas, with media being one of the most important.

🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates

• An oil tanker off the coast of Dubai was reportedly struck in an Iranian drone attack.

• Another report stated that an oil tanker was struck by an Iranian drone off the coast of Dubai that morning.

• UKMTO confirmed an incident 24 nautical miles northwest of Port Rashid.

• The master of a tanker reportedly reported seeing a listing vessel at anchor, possibly indicating water ingress following an attack by an unknown projectile.

• Another report described the incident as the targeting of a ship northwest of Rashid Port.

• At 09:17, UKMTO announced that it had received a report of an incident 24 nautical miles northwest of Port Rashid.

• The incident was described as the second maritime incident in the Persian Gulf.

• Iranian involvement was reported in some accounts, while the UKMTO account referred to an unknown projectile and did not establish responsibility.

🇮🇷 Iran

• An Iranian drone attack was reported in connection with the tanker incident off Dubai.

• However, the UKMTO account described the projectile as unknown, meaning Iranian responsibility was not independently established in the supplied reports.
