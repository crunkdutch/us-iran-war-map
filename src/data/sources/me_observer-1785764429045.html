⚡️ 🔺Rain showers predicted in some parts of the country
🔹The Meteorological Organization has predicted rain showers, thunder and lightning, temporary strong winds, and dust in susceptible areas for today, Sunday, in some parts of East Azerbaijan, West Azerbaijan, Ardabil, Mazandaran, and the heights of Tehran, Alborz, Gilan, Golestan, and Semnan provinces, at certain hours, especially in the afternoon and early evening.
🔹These conditions are also predicted for the south of Sistan and Baluchestan, Kerman, and the heights of Hormozgan provinces.
🔹The Meteorological Organization has also issued an orange alert, predicting an intensification of the rain system's activity today and tomorrow for the heights of Golestan and North Khorasan.
🔹According to this alert, from Sunday afternoon until late Monday, rain showers, thunder and lightning, and temporary strong winds will likely lead to flash floods, seasonal river overflows, and street flooding in the mentioned areas.
🔹Citizens in these areas should avoid تردد and camping along the banks and beds of rivers and seasonal waterways.
