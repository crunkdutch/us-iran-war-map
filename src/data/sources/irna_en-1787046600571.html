📡 Kurdish VP says Iran won’t allow conspiracies to drive wedge with Iraqi Kurdistan

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Vice President for Rural Development Affairs Abdolkarim Hosseinzadeh says that Iran will not allow any conspiracy to drive a wedge between the Islamic Republic and Iraq’s Kurdistan Region, stressing that Iran and the northern Iraqi region are united.


Hosseinzadeh made the remarks late Monday on X, reacting to a drone attack on the office of Kurdistan Region Prime Minister Masrour Barzani earlier in the day.
​
The vice president strongly condemned the attack and called for those responsible to be held accountable.
​
“As an Iranian Kurd and a member of the Iranian government, I strongly condemn the attack on the office of my brother, Masrour Barzani, Prime Minister of the Kurdistan Region of Iraq,” he said, emphasizing the unity between Iran and the Iraqi region.
​
He said that Iran and Iraq are “more than just neighbors”. “We are one. We will not allow any attack or conspiracy to drive a wedge between Iran and the Kurdistan Region. The truth must come to light, and those responsible must be held accountable.”
​
Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei described Monday’s attack on Barzani’s office as “highly suspicious” and urged regional vigilance to thwart attempts to destabilize ties between Iran and Iraq.

⚡️ Iran provides AI training to 4 million students

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Education Minister Alireza Kazemi says special artificial intelligence (AI) training courses have been held for about 4 million students during difficult wartime conditions.


Speaking on Tuesday at the third day of a conference of provincial education chiefs, Kazemi said the ministry’s key programs also include the Iran Digital initiative, coding, programming, and promoting AI education.
​
He said empowering teachers was another major priority, noting that 250,000 teachers have received AI-related training.
​
Kazemi said Iran’s education system had pursued two major policies over the past year: “justice” and “quality.” He added that major achievements had been made under both policies with the government’s support.
​
He also announced the construction of nearly 2,400 educational facilities containing 24,000 classrooms.
​
Other measures included standardizing 50,000 classrooms, renovating more than 150,000 classrooms, and equipping schools with modern technologies.

📡 Enemies seek to divide Iran, weaken national power: IRGC commander

📌 Bojnourd, IRNA — Major General Mostafa Izadi, Deputy Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), says the enemies have been plotting for decades under different pretexts to weaken and divide the Iranian nation.


Speaking at a ceremony to mark the 40th day since the burial of the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, in North Khorasan Province, General Izadi noted that the Islamic Revolution has been a thorn in the enemies’ eyes because of its message of peace and justice to humanity.
​
He said the enemies hatched plots and pursued various conspiracies and tricks, intending to divide Iran, steal its oil resources, weaken the Iranian Armed Forces, and destroy the country’s peaceful nuclear industry, adding that the nuclear issue was nothing more than an excuse to launch aggressions.
​
Referring to wars and conflicts imposed on the country, including the recent joint US–Israeli aggression, General Izadi said the Iranian nation and Armed Forces turned victorious in battles with the enemies in the past years, through their steadfastness and the guidance of the leadership.
​
The deputy IRGC commander said that Iranian martyrs—above all, the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution— always followed the path of excellence and salvation of the nation and stood firm against the enemies, whether it was the Sacred Defense period or during the recent wars.
​
Today, the Islamic Republic has brought the world’s hegemonic powers to their knees, which is the result of the leadership’s authority, the nation’s resistance, and the power equation that has been formed following the victory of the revolution, he explained.
​
Stating that the world is closely watching the decline of America’s power, General Izadi said Iran, despite all the conspiracies and pressures of the enemies, will continue to prosper and move forward by relying on the people, leadership, and the ideals of the martyrs.

📡 Iran’s Sistan and Baluchestan Province prepares to inaugurate major infrastructure projects

📌 Zahedan, IRNA – A series of road, housing, and urban renewal projects worth more than 60 trillion rials are ready for inauguration in northern Sistan and Baluchestan Province, southeastern Iran, during the Government Week, the provincial director general of Roads and Urban Development says.


Mehdi Parsi told IRNA on Tuesday that the projects include major highway and main road schemes, National Housing Movement projects and urban renewal plans aimed at addressing infrastructure shortages, improving road safety and meeting residents’ housing and development needs.
​
He said 89 kilometers of highways and main roads are ready for inauguration, with their current value estimated at more than 62.55 trillion rials.
​
“The development of highways and main roads, in addition to reducing travel time and increasing traffic safety, facilitates the transportation of goods and passengers, reduces transportation costs, and strengthens national and international connectivity in this border province,” Parsi said.
​
He added that the projects would contribute significantly to the region’s economic development and improve living conditions for locals.
