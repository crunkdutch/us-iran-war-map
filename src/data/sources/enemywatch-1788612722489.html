🇾🇪🇾🇪❌ 🇸🇦 | Fighting erupts on western Taiz fronts: Sana’a opens a new route toward Bab al-Mandab

Sana’a forces have made military advances over the past several hours in a number of strategic areas overlooking the Bab al-Mandab Strait in western Taiz, following heavy clashes with Saudi-backed factions on the strategic fronts of al-Kadha and Maqbana. The two fronts overlook the main supply routes in western Taiz province that the Saudi-backed factions use to reinforce their forces across the western rural fronts. Both fronts are considered highly important for the security of the Bab al-Mandab Strait, as they form the geographic depth and land gateway leading toward the western coast, the city of Mokha, and the strait. The al-Kadha area also serves as a link between the mountains and the western coast, and control of it opens the way for an advance toward al-Barh and then Mokha, while also allowing control over supply routes coming from Aden and Lahj toward the western coast. For this reason, Saudi-backed factions are increasingly concerned that these fronts could fall completely into the hands of Sana’a forces, which would represent, according to sources close to them, a “new threat to maritime navigation in the Bab al-Mandab Strait.”

Local sources in Taiz told Al-Akhbar that the clashes, involving various types of weapons, took place across four main sectors of the Taiz axis: al-Tuwair, al-Ahtoub, al-Kuwaiha, and Jabal Ghubari. According to the sources, Sana’a forces launched attacks on military gatherings in al-Kadha after receiving intelligence about plans by Saudi-backed factions, including the Victory Brigade affiliated with the Taiz axis and controlled by the Islah Party, to launch an attack on the contact fronts with Saudi support and coordination. Military sources affiliated with the Saudi-backed side said the clashes extended to four fronts, including areas belonging to al-Wazi’iyah district, areas belonging to Mawza, and the al-Barh axis west of Taiz, in addition to continuing clashes on the al-Kadha front in al-Ma’afer district and the al-Ahtoub and al-Kuwaiha fronts in Jabal Habashi district.

Control of the al-Kadha and Maqbana fronts opens the way for Sana’a forces to advance toward Mokha. Amid accusations by UAE-aligned activists against the Victory Brigade of handing over the al-Kadha front, informed field sources told Al-Akhbar that Sana’a forces engaged in heavy clashes with Saudi-backed factions on the Maqbana and Jabal Habashi fronts after taking control, at dawn yesterday, of the areas of Humrah, Lower al-Qouz, al-Rahbah, the Water Tank Hill, Masna’ah, and other strategic positions, as well as mountain ranges overlooking the villages of al-Afira, al-Nuwaiha, and al-Udhair, several hills in Mawza district, and the Barasha mountain range overlooking Hays district. According to the sources, forces affiliated with Tariq Saleh are currently attempting to retake some of the positions that fell to Ansar Allah, with support from formations of the southern Salafi Giants Brigades.

While Saudi-backed factions acknowledged the deaths of several of their commanders in the clashes in al-Kadha and Maqbana, Agence France-Presse quoted sources within those factions as saying that 13 of their members were killed. Media outlets affiliated with the Aden government also said the fighting was at its fiercest across the various fronts in western Taiz, expressing concern over the possible loss of the strategic road linking Taiz and Mokha through al-Kadha. At the same time, they said that groups aligned with Riyadh had retaken two military positions on the al-Kadha front after Sana’a forces had taken control of them. Later, sources aligned with the Aden government acknowledged the fall of Jabal Na’man on the Jabal Habashi front, which contains important positions and overlooks extensive military supply routes linking several fronts.
