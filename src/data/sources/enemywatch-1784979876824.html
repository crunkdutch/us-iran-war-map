🇮🇷 | IRGC

The region will not see peace until the Zionist regime is eliminated

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), in a statement mourning and congratulating the martyrdom of two Hamas commanders, Mohammad Oudeh (“Abu Amr”) and Ezzeddine al-Haddad (“Abu Suhaib”), stated that the region will not witness peace except through the elimination of the Zionist regime. It also said that the so-called peace spoken of by the “malicious and gambling American president” brings nothing but killing, assassination, and terror.

Part of the statement said that the martyrdom of “Mujahid Mohammad Oudeh (Abu Amr),” along with his wife Umm Amr and their three children, Yasser, Yahya, and Jamila, in an Israeli airstrike on Gaza City, as well as the martyrdom of Ezzeddine al-Haddad (“Abu Suhaib”), one of the commanders of the Izz ad-Din al-Qassam Brigades who fought the Zionists for nearly four decades, once again exposed what it described as the savage and “devilish” nature of the Zionist regime.

The statement further emphasized that the record of nearly eight decades of crimes committed by what it called the “fake and occupying Zionist regime” once again proves to the world, especially to those claiming to support human rights and freedom, that West Asia will not experience peace until this “child-killing and evil regime” is removed. It added that the peace plans promoted by the American president amount only to bloodshed, murder, and terrorism.

Referring to the steadfastness of the people of Gaza and Hamas fighters, the statement declared that the martyrdom of these commanders would not weaken the resistance, but would instead strengthen the continuation of jihad until the liberation of Palestine and Al-Aqsa.

The statement concluded by expressing condolences and congratulations over the martyrdom of the Palestinian resistance commanders, while reaffirming the full support of the Islamic Republic of Iran and the IRGC for the Axis of Resistance, as well as their continued determination to confront those occupying Al-Aqsa and their supporters.
