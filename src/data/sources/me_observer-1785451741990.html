Press Release



A delegation from the leadership of the Islamic Resistance Movement (Hamas), headed by Dr. Khalil Al-Hayya, head of the movement's political bureau, met with His Excellency the Turkish Foreign Minister, Dr. Hakan Fidan. The two sides discussed developments related to the Palestinian issue, politically and on the ground, especially the ongoing negotiations in Cairo.

The meeting discussed the field escalation continued by the occupation, the daily violations of the ceasefire agreement in the Gaza Strip, and the continuous killing and assassination operations. It also addressed the crimes of the settlers and their provocations in the West Bank, and the incursions into the blessed Al-Aqsa Mosque.

Ways to achieve the unity of the Palestinian people were also discussed, as well as the announced plan for holding elections, and the necessity of achieving national consensus to arrange the Palestinian house, according to a strategic vision that achieves the unity of our people, preserves their interests, and serves their national goals.

The head of the movement expressed his appreciation for the Turkish efforts and its continuous endeavors to end the suffering of the Palestinian people, restore their legitimate rights, and put an end to the occupation's practices and crimes in the West Bank, Gaza Strip, and Jerusalem.
