⚡️ Alamut Castles Become Global Heritage

The dossier of Alamut Castles and associated fortifications in Qazvin has been registered as Iran's 30th site on the UNESCO World Heritage List.

Alamut is a complex comprising seven historical castles, over a thousand years old, spanning an area of two thousand square kilometers in the Alborz Mountains.

Traditional architectural knowledge, traditional medicine, herbal medicine, and traditional water supply knowledge are among the features of this dossier.
