🇮🇷 | IRGC Announces Arrest and Elimination of Terrorist Cells in Sistan and Baluchestan:

The Public Relations Department of the IRGC Ground Force’s Quds Base announced the arrest of eight armed individuals linked to terrorist groups in Sistan and Baluchestan Province.

According to the statement, the individuals had formed armed cells to create insecurity, carry out anti-security operations, kidnap personnel and engineers involved in infrastructure projects, attack military and law enforcement patrols and bases, conduct armed robberies, and spread fear in central areas of the province. They were reportedly identified through intelligence operations and arrested during several special missions.

The IRGC stated that weapons and ammunition were seized from those arrested. It added that the groups had targeted military and law enforcement facilities, as well as companies and personnel involved in provincial development and reconstruction projects. The statement further says that the individuals coordinated with cross-border terrorist groups to abduct prominent figures and engineers and hand them over to groups operating outside Iran.

In a separate statement, the IRGC Quds Base announced the dismantling of another terrorist team in southern Sistan and Baluchestan before it could carry out planned operations.

The statement said the group, which entered Iran from neighboring countries carrying weapons and ammunition, was detected through intelligence monitoring and confronted by IRGC forces. According to the statement, members of the group were killed during an armed clash.

The IRGC Quds Base warned terrorist groups and their affiliates that their activities were being monitored and that they would face a decisive response.
