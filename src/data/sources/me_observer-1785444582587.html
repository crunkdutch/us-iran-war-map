⚡️ Israel Hayom:

The United States and Israel are pushing for the establishment of four additional concrete factories in the Gaza envelope, with the aim of meeting the increasing demand for sealing Hamas tunnels in the Strip, as well as building the giant military base that international stabilization forces are supposed to use.

The need to establish additional factories stems from the enormous quantities of concrete required for these two objectives.

The system is currently operating under financial flow difficulties. Concrete factories stop operating every week due to budget problems, which significantly affects the continuous ability to seal tunnel routes.

It turns out in practice that the Israeli army does not have an organized and comprehensive action plan to seal all the tunnels discovered in the Strip, and that the activity is carried out according to the immediately available budget.

The method of destroying tunnels relies on pumping liquid concrete at high pressure directly into the underground passage. For example, engineering forces pumped more than 30,000 cubic meters of concrete to seal the giant tunnel in Rafah, where the body of officer Hadar Goldin was held, and in sealing another tunnel north of the Strip, 20,000 cubic meters were used, pumped through a special pipe system extending for kilometers.

Hundreds of other tunnels still remain in the Strip today.

Concrete currently arrives from two factories in Kerem Shalom and Nahal Oz, which only recently began operations, and it is clear that the current production rate does not meet existing needs.
