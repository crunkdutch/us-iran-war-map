🥀 | The dream Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei reportedly saw before his martyrdom:

On the first night following the martyrdom of the Leader, Sayyid Mahdi Khazri, the Friday prayer leader of Qazvin, recounted a dream that Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei had reportedly shared with him several weeks before his martyrdom during a gathering at his home.

According to Sayyid Mahdi Khazri, the Imam said:

“I saw myself in a dream inside Imam Khomeini Hussainiyah. A congregational prayer was taking place, and I was standing among the worshippers as a follower. After a short while, I asked myself, ‘If I am praying in congregation, why am I among the followers and not leading the prayer? Who is the Imam?’ I looked toward the front and saw that the Imam of the congregation was Imam al-Hujjah ibn al-Hasan (a). After the prayer, I approached him and began speaking about the responsibilities and hardships we face. The Imam of the Age reassured me and said, ‘Your mission is over.’”

Sayyid Mahdi Khazri added:

“At the time, we understood this to mean that the reappearance of Imam al-Mahdi (a) was near. But now, after the martyrdom of Imam Khamenei, we understand that it referred to the completion of his mission and his martyrdom as an oppressed and devoted servant.”
