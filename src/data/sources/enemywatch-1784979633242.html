🇮🇷: 🇮🇷 | Mashhad funeral epic today: Final procession, funeral prayers, and burial of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r):

Today marks the final procession, funeral prayers, and burial of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei (r) in the holy city of Mashhad, where he will be laid to rest in the Holy Shrine of Imam Ali al Ridha (A), bringing the historic funeral ceremonies to their conclusion.

To recall the journey:

3 July 2026: The sacred coffin first appeared at Hussainiyeh Imam Khomeini, where the families of the martyrs bid their final farewell.

4-5 July 2026: Two days of ceremonies were held at Musalla Imam Khomeini in Tehran. The first funeral prayer was led by Grand Ayatullah Jafar Subhani (H), followed by the first grand funeral procession, which gathered an estimated 15 million mourners along a route stretching approximately 8 to 10 kilometers.

6 July 2026: Qom witnessed an unprecedented funeral gathering. The second funeral prayer was led by Grand Ayatullah Abdollah Javadi Amoli (H). The procession from Jamkaran Mosque to the Holy Shrine of Sayyidah Fatimah al Masumah (S), covering nearly 10 kilometers, was estimated by supporters at around 35 million participants.

7 July 2026: The ceremonies continued in Najaf, where the procession began from Sadr Square and proceeded to the Holy Shrine of Imam Ali (A). The third funeral prayer was led by Ayatullah Sayyid Muhammad Taqi al Hakim (H). According to the Popular Mobilization Forces (PMU), approximately 3.8 million mourners participated in the 6 kilometer procession.

Later, Karbala witnessed another historic farewell. The procession moved from Abu Mahdi al Muhandis Street to the Holy Shrines of Imam Husayn (A) and Hazrat Abal Fadhl al Abbas (A), covering approximately 5.8 kilometers. Combined participation across Najaf and Karbala was estimated by supporters at around 24 to 25 million mourners. The fourth funeral prayer was led by Hujjat al Islam Sheikh Abdul Mahdi al Karbalai at the Holy Shrine of Imam Husayn (A), followed by the fifth funeral prayer led by Hujjat al Islam Sayyid Ahmad al Safi at the Holy Shrine of Hazrat Abal Fadhl al Abbas (A).

Today, Mashhad prepares for the final chapter. The last and most solemn stage of the funeral ceremonies will conclude with the burial of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei (r) beside Imam Ali al Ridha (A). Current expectations place attendance at around two million mourners, with estimates suggesting the gathering could rise to three million as pilgrims continue arriving from across Iran and beyond.

🇮🇷: 🇮🇷 | Mashhad final epic: Final funeral procession, prayers, and burial of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei (r):

Date: 9 July 2026

Final procession route

1. The final funeral procession will begin at 14:00 from Imam Reza Street in Mashhad.

2. The procession will proceed toward the Holy Shrine of Imam Ali al Ridha (A).

3. The burial and final farewell ceremonies are expected to conclude on Thursday evening or Friday night, depending on the movement of the procession and the crowds.

Final funeral prayer

1. The final funeral prayer will be held along Sheikh Tabarsi Street, with prayer rows extending through Tabarsi, Shirazi, and Navab Safavi Streets, surrounding the Holy Shrine.

2. Grand Marja Ayatullah Naser Makarem Shirazi (H) will lead the final funeral prayer.

3. Following the conclusion of the procession along Imam Reza Street, funeral prayers will also be offered for the martyred members of Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei's family.

Burial

1. Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei (r) will be buried within the Holy Shrine of Imam Ali al Ridha (A).

2. According to the Custodian of the Holy Shrine, several locations were examined and presented with their respective advantages and disadvantages.

3. Imam Sayyid Mujtaba Husayni Khamenei (H) instructed that the burial place should be located along the main pilgrimage route, allowing pilgrims to recite the Holy Quran, offer prayers, and pay their respects while visiting the shrine.

4. A proposal to create a separate visitation area similar to the tomb of Sheikh Baha'i was considered but was not approved.

5. The final decision regarding the exact burial location rests with the honorable family.

Official remarks

1. The Custodian of the Holy Shrine stated that the late leader belongs not only to the present generation but also to future generations, and numerous opinions regarding the burial location were carefully received and conveyed.

2. He described the martyrdom of the late leader as the reward for a lifetime of struggle and service.

3. He stated that the funeral processions in Tehran, Qom, Najaf, and Karbala had become historic epics and expressed hope that Mashhad would witness the most magnificent funeral procession of all.

The Mashhad ceremonies will conclude the funeral journey of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei (r), ending with his burial beside Imam Ali al Ridha (A) after funeral ceremonies that drew unprecedented participation across Iran and Iraq.
