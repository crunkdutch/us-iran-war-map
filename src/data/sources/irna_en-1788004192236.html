⚡️ President Pezeshkian: Iran real winner of Islamabad MoU

📌 Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has described the Islamabad memorandum of understanding (MoU) as a major diplomatic achievement, saying international and domestic experts believe Iran emerged as the real winner of the agreement.


Speaking in a televised interview on Friday evening, President Pezeshkian said the understanding secured key commitments from the United States on sanctions relief, the release of Iranian funds and the resumption of investment, while ongoing talks are focused on establishing a framework for reopening the Strait of Hormuz. He also stressed that Iran would continue diplomatic engagement but would not remain bound by commitments if the other side failed to honor its obligations.
​
President Pezeshkian said the MoU reached by Iran’s Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) was the result of collective and expert decision-making. He noted that 12 of the council’s 13 members supported the understanding.
​
The president said nine provisions of the agreement constituted US commitments, while several others were joint commitments and one concerned Iran’s nuclear program and required further negotiations.
​
President Pezeshkian said negotiations had initially produced tangible results, including progress toward lifting sanctions and releasing Iran’s frozen funds. He added that the parties had also discussed foreign investment in Iran of up to $300 billion and restoring normal passage through the Strait of Hormuz under Iran’s agreed framework.
​
He said the current negotiations had produced a roadmap for reopening the strategic waterway, which has been presented to Iran’s Leader Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei and approved.
​
According to the president, Oman initially raised some reservations but later agreed to the coordinated framework. Iran is now seeking guarantees that the United States will fulfill commitments concerning the lifting of sanctions and blockade, the release of Iranian oil and petrochemical revenues, resumption of investment and an end to military actions in Lebanon.
​
President Pezeshkian stressed that Iran would continue dialogue while responding proportionately if the other side failed to honor its commitments.
​
He said reducing tensions, expanding relations with eastern countries and seeking sanctions relief would remain key foreign-policy priorities during the rest of his administration.
​
He also called for stronger ties with neighboring countries, particularly Muslim states, saying Iran seeks greater coordination with Pakistan, Afghanistan, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, the UAE, Qatar, Oman, Egypt, Azerbaijan, and Turkmenistan.
​
President Pezeshkian said regional countries should work together to establish security and stability and prevent the Israeli regime from easily attacking Islamic countries.
​
He emphasized that regional peace requires dialogue, mutual understanding and adherence to agreements.
​
President Pezeshkian argued that reducing tensions and expanding regional and international economic relations were essential to improving Iran’s economic conditions and strengthening the country’s resilience.

📡 Pezeshkian administration strengthens cultural diplomacy, pursues cultural justice

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Ministry of Culture and Islamic Guidance has made efforts to strengthen cultural diplomacy and international interactions while pursuing national unity, cultural justice, and reduced government intervention in cultural affairs, IRNA’s review of the ministry’s performance under President Masoud Pezeshkian’s administration shows.


Under the Pezeshkian administration, which took office on July 28, 2024, the Culture Ministry has based its policies on three main principles: “national unity and cohesion,” “strengthening the cultural and artistic economy,” and “reducing government intervention and developing cultural justice.”
​
It has also sought to address legal and security problems faced by some artists, with efforts to resolve many of those cases.
​
The possibility of Iranian artists living abroad returning to the country has also received greater attention under the Pezeshkian administration. Some Iranian artists returned in the 2000s but did not receive adequate support.
​
Culture and Islamic Guidance Minister Abbas Salehi has said the administration has adopted a more positive approach toward Iranians abroad, including singers who wish to return.
​
“After the 12-day war, during which Iranians abroad became part of Iran’s social capital — both during that war and the Ramadan war — a positive view fairly emerged, and the law on supporting Iranians abroad was approved by Parliament,” he said.
​
His ministry has also emphasized expanding international cultural engagement despite sanctions and imposed wars, while seeking to improve interaction between the government and cultural and artistic communities.

🖥 Exclusive | Hamas representative in Tehran calls Israel ‘enemy of humanity’

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Khaled Qaddoumi, Hamas representative in Tehran, has called Israel “an enemy of humanity,” emphasizing unity of regional countries in the face of the regime.


“This enemy is truly an enemy of humanity; Israel, an enemy that has come to the region at the beginning of this century. Today, we must all unite,” Qaddoumi said in an exclusive interview with IRNA published on Saturday.
​
He praised Iran for sustained support to Palestinian groups, saying that Palestinians and Iranians shared concerns over Israeli crimes.
​
He also said the Israeli regime is committing crimes in Palestine and across the region; despite that the Palestinian resistance movement, Hamas, will continue pursuing resistance and demanding implementation of Gaza ceasefire commitments.
​
According to Qaddoumi, Hamas’s recent selection of Khalil al-Hayya as head of its political bureau demonstrated Palestinian unity, arguing that the killing of resistance leaders, including Ismail Haniyeh and Yahya Sinwar, would not change the movement’s course.
​
Hamas is not seeking political positions and intends to maintain administrative structures in Gaza based on local needs, Qaddoumi said.
​
Regarding weapons, he noted that decisions about whether Palestinian resistance groups should retain or surrender weapons should be made by Palestinians themselves after establishing a political framework, including representative institutions and a government.
​
Gaza ceasefire and humanitarian aid
​
The Hamas official criticized the implementation of the Gaza ceasefire agreement, saying its initial provisions called for an Israeli withdrawal from Gaza and the reopening of crossings.
​
He said the agreement envisaged the entry of 600 trucks of aid into Gaza each day, rising to 1,500 in a subsequent phase. According to Qaddoumi, only 297 trucks entered under the cited arrangement, including 160 commercial trucks, which he said represented less than 20 percent of the expected humanitarian assistance.
​
Qaddoumi urged mediators and guarantors, including the United States and Egypt, to pressure the Israeli regime to facilitate the entry of the National Committee for the Administration of Gaza (NCAG) into Gaza. The committee, he said, would address humanitarian needs, reconstruction, aid delivery and preparations for future elections.
​
Qaddoumi said roughly 70 percent of Palestinian fatalities were women and children, saying images and information from Gaza had exposed the Israeli regime’s atrocities worldwide.
​
He said public opinion in Europe and the United States was increasingly focused on Gaza’s humanitarian situation, and praised journalists for documenting events.
​
West Bank and regional tensions
​
He also said Israeli settlers are attacking Al-Aqsa Mosque and Palestinian communities in the occupied West Bank, burning property and killing Palestinians, with military support.
​
Qaddoumi urged the international community to respond, warning that inaction would lead to further displacement and bloodshed.

🔻 Exclusive | Iran envoy: Europe must accept reality of a strong Iran

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Ambassador to Germany Majid Nili says Europe must accept the reality of a strong Iran, stressing that Tehran will continue pursuing dialogue and engagement based on mutual respect while firmly defending its national interests.


In an exclusive interview with IRNA, Nili said Iran’s resistance to the recent US-Israeli military aggression has further demonstrated the country’s decisive position, defense capabilities, and resilience.
​
“Despite the existing political atmosphere and pressures, the path of dialogue and engagement based on mutual respect is still being pursued,” he said.
​
On February 28, the United States and Israel initiated a war of aggression against Iran with the assassination of Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei and several top military commanders. They also targeted a school in Minab, southern Iran, killing scores of elementary school children.
​
In response, the Iranian Armed Forces carried out retaliatory attacks during the 40 days of the war, targeting US and Israeli military assets, which resulted in significant damage.
​
Nili also criticized Europe’s response to the war against Iran, saying the country’s regional position, defense capabilities and resilience has attracted growing attention among political and expert circles.
​
He said even Iran’s critics have realized that the country could not be excluded from the region’s political and security equations.
​
The ambassador also said the embassy’s main priorities over the past year included addressing the affairs of Iranians living in Germany, maintaining channels of communication with German officials, and defending Iran’s national interests.
​
He identified supporting Iranians abroad, expanding economic and trade relations, strengthening public diplomacy and engaging with German society as other priorities for the embassy.

🔊 Iranian referee Milad Ghalavand selected for World U23 Wrestling Championships

📌 Abadan, IRNA – Iranian international wrestling referee Milad Ghalavand has been selected as a referee at the 2026 World U23 Wrestling Championships in the United States, according to the head of the Arvand Free Zone Wrestling Board.


Omid Vaziri told IRNA on Saturday that Ghalavand, a Category I international referee, was selected with the approval of the Iranian Wrestling Federation to officiate at the competition.
​
He said Ghalavand is an experienced and active international referee who has officiated at major Asian and international competitions in several countries.
​
According to Vaziri, Ghalavand has recently served as a referee at competitions in Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Spain, Turkey, Jordan, Hungary, Thailand, Albania, and Mongolia.
​
Vaziri said Ghalavand’s participation in major international events demonstrates his experience and standing in the wrestling refereeing community and reflects the confidence of Iran’s wrestling authorities in his abilities.
​
The World U23 Wrestling Championships will be held in Los Angeles, US, from October 11 to 19.
