📄 Iran to inaugurate 122 fisheries projects this week: Official

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Fisheries Organization has announced that 122 fisheries projects will be inaugurated across 23 provinces during Government Week, which starts on August 24.


Hamzeh Rostampour, head of the Fisheries Organization, made the announcement on Saturday, saying the new projects will increase national fisheries production by more than 59,000 tons and create over 27,000 direct and indirect jobs.
​
According to the official, the projects cover a range of areas, including fishing vessel operations, warm-water, cold-water and sturgeon farming, cage fish farming, shrimp farming, jetty construction, feed production, seafood packaging and processing, ornamental fish farming, and leech farming.
​
He said Hormozgan Province in southern Iran ranks first in terms of investment despite wartime conditions. The projects to be inaugurated in the province include fleet development, vessel equipment, shrimp farming and the restructuring of aquaculture farms, he added.

🌍 Iranian Armed Forces chief hails physicians on National Doctors’ Day

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Chief of Staff of the Iranian Armed Forces Major General Ali Abdollahi has praised the sacrifices of the medical personnel, from the eight-year Sacred Defense to their tireless efforts during the COVID-19 pandemic and recent wars imposed by Israel and the United States over the past year.


In a message on Sunday marking National Doctors’ Day and commemorating the birth anniversary of Avicenna, the renowned Iranian physician and father of modern medicine, Abdollahi congratulated physicians, pharmacists and other healthcare workers on the occasion.
​
He honored the martyrs of the medical community and underscored the lasting inspiration provided by their commitment to safeguarding life and health in service to the nation.
​
Abdollahi further noted that the birth anniversaries of Avicenna and Zakariya Razi provide a valuable opportunity to appreciate the noble role of doctors, pharmacists, nurses and other medical personnel who have shouldered the responsibility of protecting public health with sincerity and sacrifice.

🖥 Quds Force commander says Palestinian cause remains alive and unchanging

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps’ (IRGC) Quds Force, Brigadier General Esmaeil Qa’ani, says the Palestinian cause, from the sea to the river, has never been more alive and attainable.


In a message on Sunday, Qa’ani said the expansion of settlements and the crimes committed by the Israeli regime are attempts to escape a deep military, security, political and social deadlock in the occupied territories and cannot conceal the regime’s strategic failures taking place since October 7, 2023.
​

​

🔻 Iran rejects interference by two French diplomats

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has said the interventionist conduct of two French diplomats in Iran is completely unacceptable.


Speaking to reporters on Sunday, Baqaei said Iran promptly responded after determining that the French Embassy in Tehran had exceeded its diplomatic mandate, violated the provisions of the Vienna Convention and engaged in interference in Iran’s domestic affairs.
​
He said Iran had officially announced that the two diplomats involved in such activities must leave the country and would not be allowed to return.
​
Relations between countries, including Iran and France, must be based on mutual respect and adherence to international law, including the principle of non-interference in the internal affairs of states, he added.
​
“Accordingly, Iran regulates its relations with all countries, and whenever necessary, issues warnings and takes the required measures,” Baqaei noted.
​
He said France’s subsequent action was unjustifiable, describing it as a reaction to a completely legal measure taken by Iran in accordance with international law. Such actions, he added, would be detrimental to the continuation of Iran-France relations.
​
On August 18, Iran’s Foreign Ministry declared two French diplomats working at the French Embassy in Tehran persona non grata.
​
The move came a few days after Iran’s Intelligence Ministry said the two diplomats had taken part in a secret meeting in Tehran in July with suspects in a major case involving foreign infiltration and interference. The Foreign Ministry said the diplomats’ activities had violated international law, particularly the 1961 Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations.
