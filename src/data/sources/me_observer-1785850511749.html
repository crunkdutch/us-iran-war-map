⚡️ The Wall Street Journal:
The United States has withdrawn the last 5 Patriot (MIM-104) air defense missile systems from Erbil Airport, as it approaches the completion of the long-delayed withdrawal process from Iraqi territory.
While US officials insist that the withdrawal is a "routine measure" and part of a larger plan to reduce the US presence in the Middle East, the US position in Iraq has become unsustainable after the outbreak of the Third Gulf War in February, as US bases became under virtual siege due to missiles launched by Iraqi and Iranian militias.
There is no doubt that this withdrawal represents a victory for Iran, as without Patriot batteries in Erbil, Iranian drones and missiles will find it easier to reach Jordan and perhaps Israel.
However, the biggest losers from this decision to remove all US forces from Erbil are the Kurds, who benefited greatly from the presence of US air defenses. The loss of these systems will leave the Kurds in the grip of Iranian leaders.
