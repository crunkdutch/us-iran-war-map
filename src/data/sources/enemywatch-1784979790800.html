🇮🇷 | Announcement No. 3 of the Central Committee for Commemoration of the Ascension of the Martyred Leader, Imam Mujahid, Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei (r):

In the Name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful

“Among the believers are men who have been true to their covenant with Allah; some of them have fulfilled their vow, and some are still waiting, and they have never changed in the least.”

Following the bloody ascension of the esteemed marja of the Shi’a world, the wise Leader of the Islamic Revolution and standard-bearer of the front of truth and resistance, Imam Mujahid, the martyr Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei (r), the Islamic world is in mourning for a great man who devoted his entire blessed life to elevating the word of truth, defending Islam, serving the people, and guiding the Islamic Ummah.

That martyred leader, with firm faith, sincere struggle, steadfast patience, and divine insight, always kept the banner of Iran’s independence and the dignity and guidance of the Islamic Ummah raised high. His name and memory will remain eternal in the history of Iran, the Islamic world, and in the hearts of the free people of the world.

The Central Committee for Commemoration of the Bloody Ascension of Imam Mujahid, Grand Ayatollah Khamenei, while extending condolences to the presence of the Imam of the Age (A), to the Supreme Leader Sayyid Mujtaba Hosseini Khamenei, and to all free nations and seekers of truth, expresses its deep gratitude for the sincere and devoted participation of the Iranian people and all lovers of Islam and the Revolution.

In continuation of previous announcements, it is hereby informed that the farewell, funeral, and burial ceremonies of the pure body of the martyred Imam Mujahid and his family members will be held as follows:

19 Muharram and 20 Muharram—04 July 2026: Farewell ceremonies at Imam Khomeini Grand Mosque in Tehran.

21 Muharram—06 July 2026: Funeral procession in Tehran.

22 Muharram—07 July 2026: Funeral procession in the holy city of Qom.

24 Muharram—09 July 2026: Funeral procession in the holy city of Mashhad and burial in the blessed shrine of Imam Reza (A).

The general public, free peoples of the world, and lovers of Islam and Iran are invited to participate in these grand ceremonies and accompany the martyred leader on his final journey.

At the end, while appreciating the extensive preparations by responsible institutions and public groups, it is emphasized that the main role in organizing these ceremonies belongs to the people themselves, and a magnificent and unprecedented popular event will be realized, Allah willing.

Further details will be announced later.
