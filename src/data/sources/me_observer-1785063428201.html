1. Dear citizens can now complete all necessary steps without leaving their home or workplace.
2. The citizen enters the official platform and completes the electronic identity verification process.
3. Document upload: Documents, lawsuits, or complaints are uploaded to the system and sent to the judicial services office's inbox.
4. Office expert review: The relevant expert reviews the documents. If there are deficiencies, the case is returned for correction, and if approved, the final draft is issued.
5. Online payment of fees: The payment ID for the "Sakha" system is issued, and the fee is collected electronically through banking portals.
6. Electronic signature: The signature code is issued from the "AdlIran" system, and the citizen enters it into the platform to confirm final approval.
7. Submission to judicial authority: The director of the services office sends the approved request to the referral deputy of the judiciary.
