🇾🇪 | Latest updates from Yemen:

• Yemeni missile attacks and advances west of Taiz

Yemeni forces launched rocket and missile attacks against positions of Saudi-backed forces west of Taiz. A barrage of Yemeni missiles was reported heading toward its targets. In the past few hours, Yemeni forces reportedly took control of the villages of Al-Darha, Ukmah Al-Qasas, Wadi Shbq, Al-Burjah, Jundin, Shat Al-Rqa’, Al-Mazanda, Al-Khadra’, Al-Jinan, Jabal Naba, and Al-Shqah in Jabal Habshi District, west of Taiz city. Yemeni armed forces also launched an attack using several ballistic missiles against positions and gatherings of Saudi-backed militias in Taiz Province.

• Saudi airstrikes in Yemen

The Saudi Air Force conducted a large aerial campaign against AnsarAllah on several fronts. More than 20 Saudi airstrikes targeted the city of Al-Bayda. Saudi aviation also bombed Network 733 in Jabal Thara in the city of Al-Buaida, reportedly cutting communications for civilians in areas under the control of the Saudi-backed forces.

Earlier tonight, AnsarAllah air defenses reportedly attempted to shoot down a Saudi jet over Sanaa, prompting the aircraft to deploy countermeasures. Projectiles subsequently fell in Al-Thawal Province in the Jazan region of southwestern Saudi Arabia, reportedly injuring two people.

• AnsarAllah activity around Marib

Over the past 24 hours, AnsarAllah forces have reportedly positioned themselves on the southern and western highlands surrounding Marib, making limited progress while taking up strategic positions. Overall, the frontline situation has not changed significantly, but AnsarAllah is reportedly bringing additional equipment and forces into the area in preparation for a possible offensive in the near future. At the same time, morale among Saudi-backed PLC fighters stationed in Marib city is reportedly low, while there are reports that local tribal leaders are already negotiating with AnsarAllah.

• Yemeni attacks toward Saudi Arabia

Yemeni missiles were detected heading toward targets in Abha and Khamis Mushait in Saudi Arabia, with Saudi civil defense issuing warnings to residents.

• Bab al-Mandeb maritime traffic

AnsarAllah reported that 73 ships passed through the Bab al-Mandeb Strait on September 10 and 11, with 36 ships transiting on the first day and 37 on the second. According to the report, the maritime embargo on Saudi shipping had not disrupted international maritime traffic, with the total number of vessels passing through the strait over the 48-hour period reaching 98.6 percent of the average recorded before the ban.

• Tareq Saleh and reports of movement to Saudi Arabia

General Tareq Saleh reportedly left Yemen by sea and is currently in Somalia. A separate report stated that Tareq Saleh, a pet affiliated with Saudi Arabia, was summoned to Riyadh for questioning following the defeat of forces under his command.

According to informed sources cited in the report, Tareq Saleh is in Riyadh attempting to meet senior Saudi officials, where he intends to provide a report on the situation on the ground and justify the collapse of his forces’ structure in the strategic area. Arab sources reportedly linked his departure to the loss of the strategic city of Al-Mokha on the Red Sea coast by forces under his command.

The report described the reported liberation of Al-Mokha by the Yemeni army stationed in Sanaa as a significant development because of the city’s location on the Red Sea and its proximity to the Bab al-Mandeb Strait. Al-Mokha has also reportedly served as one of the main bases for Tareq Saleh’s forces in their opposition to AnsarAllah.

🔔 | Regional developments | September 13, 2026

🇮🇷 Iran • 🇴🇲 Oman

An informed source told Tasnim that Iran and Oman reached a final agreement in early September on new shipping routes through the Strait of Hormuz, while stressing that the agreement does not mean the reopening of the Strait. Under the proposed arrangement, inbound traffic would pass entirely through Iranian territorial waters, while part of the outbound route would also pass through Iranian waters and operate under Iranian arrangements. The U.S.-backed southern route would be closed. Gulf states and Iraq would be briefed on the agreement but would not participate in its technical decisions. Separately, Iran has reportedly presented Washington with seven conditions for reopening the Strait. Until those conditions are fulfilled, the Strait will remain closed, and any future reopening would take place under the new Iran-Oman framework.

UKMTO reported that a vessel was struck while attempting to pass through the Strait of Hormuz. Separate reports claimed that another vessel attempting to cross the Strait via the Omani route was struck by a projectile, with the condition of the vessel and its crew remaining unknown. Reports also claimed that the IRGC Navy continued launching missiles and drones against vessels attempting to use the Omani-U.S. corridor, while explosions were reported near the Iranian coast following alleged anti-ship missile launches against vessels attempting to pass through the area. These battlefield claims have not been independently verified.

🇮🇷 Iran • 🇹🇷 Turkey

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi held a telephone conversation with Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan to discuss the latest regional developments. The two sides reviewed the evolving situation and emphasized the need to prevent further escalation and expansion of the regional crisis. They also stressed the continuation of consultations and diplomatic efforts aimed at contributing to stability and calm in the region.

🇮🇷 Iran • 🇮🇶 Iraq

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi held a telephone conversation with Iraqi Foreign Minister Fuad Hussein, during which they discussed bilateral issues, regional developments, and ongoing political and security developments. The two ministers reviewed key issues in Iran-Iraq relations, emphasizing the importance of maintaining stability and security along their shared border and continuing coordination. They also stressed the need for responsible approaches to regional developments and the importance of preventing further escalation and expansion of conflict.

🇱🇧 Lebanon

Israeli forces continued attacks across southern Lebanon on September 12. Israeli warplanes carried out raids on Kfar Tibnit, the Al-Arish-Hadatha area, Al-Mansouri, the area between Meiss Al-Jabal and Shaqra, Al-Qantara, Wadi Zibqin, and Nabatieh Fawqa. Israeli artillery shelling targeted Wadi Al-Salouqi, Wadi Al-Hujeir, Nabatieh Fawqa, Meiss Al-Jabal, Kfar Tibnit, Hula, Sribbin, Zawtar Sharqiyah, Ali Al-Taher, Al-Mansouri, Aita Al-Jabal, and Beit Yahoun. Additional explosive operations were reported in Al-Mansouri, Al-Taybeh, Ainatha, Al-Qantara, Meiss Al-Jabal, and Al-Khiam.

🇮🇶 Iraq

An attack targeted an Iraqi Army position in Kirkuk Province, northern Iraq. Initial reports said the attackers destroyed a thermal camera and engaged in heavy clashes with Iraqi Army forces before fleeing the area. The identity of the attackers was not immediately established.

Local sources also reported a heavy security deployment by the Popular Mobilization Forces (PMF) across western Anbar’s desert areas toward the border with Syria after suspicious movements by remnants of terrorist groups were detected.

Separately, initial reports claimed that two ISIS cells consisting of 22 members had been arrested in Halabja. The reports said all of those detained were Kurdish, although the claim had not been independently verified.
