TLDR:

The Iranian-American understanding, which included Lebanon, did not include Gaza due to multiple factors. Hamas, which governs Gaza, has different relations with Iran than Hezbollah and did not seek Iranian mediation during the Gaza war. Additionally, Israel and the U.S. have consistently excluded Iran from Gaza-related negotiations, promoting separate tracks involving Qatar, Egypt, and Turkey. Including Gaza in the agreement could also have alienated regional states that support Hamas and fueled accusations that Iran is using the issue for leverage. Moreover, from a negotiation standpoint, introducing Gaza at this stage could have jeopardized the entire process, as it is a non-negotiable red line for Israel and the U.S. While Gaza is not included, Iran's support for the broader resistance in Lebanon is seen as indirectly benefiting the Palestinian cause in the long run.
