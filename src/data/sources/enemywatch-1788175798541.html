According to the claim of an American official, the two launchers were preparing to fire missiles carrying naval mines toward the Strait of Hormuz. In response, the IRGC confirmed that the American-Zionist enemy of carrying out an act of aggression that resulted in the martyrdom and injury of several Iranian soldiers and civilians, and vowed retaliation.

The IRGC said the operation destroyed maintenance infrastructure, technical facilities, and aircraft concentration sites at the two American air bases in Jordan, warning that every future attack on Iran would be met with a stronger response. As regional tensions escalated, Qatar raised its security threat level and urged residents to remain indoors while denying reports of attacks inside the country, Saudi Arabia temporarily halted operations at Jeddah Airport, missile alerts were issued across the United Arab Emirates, and five of the six American Air Force tanker aircraft patrolling the Strait of Hormuz were reported to be operating from Al Udeid Air Base in Qatar after being repositioned because of increasingly accurate Iranian strikes across the Persian Gulf statelets.
