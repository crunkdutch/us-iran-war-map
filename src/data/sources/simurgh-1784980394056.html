🇮🇷 🇮🇷 | Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r) is to be buried in the Dar al Zuhd porch of the Holy Shrine of Imam Ali al Ridha (A). According to the available information, the burial will take place at the center of this porch.

Dar al Zuhd is also known for its continuous Qur’an recitation gatherings, where groups of Qur’an reciters (Qaris) recite the Holy Qur’an day and night. Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r) himself visited this porch on several occasions, including meetings with members of the Resistance Axis, particularly the Fatemiyoun Brigade.

There have been widespread public requests regarding the construction of a gravestone or a protective shrine structure (zarih) over the grave. According to the information presented here, these requests were not approved by Imam Sayyid Mujtaba Husayni Khamenei (H). However, this has not been officially confirmed. Members of EnemyWatch, together with several senior individuals, have also joined the wave of written multiple letters to the representatives of Imam Sayyid Mujtaba Khamenei (H) requesting permission for a zarih to be placed over the grave. Time will tell what decision is ultimately made.
