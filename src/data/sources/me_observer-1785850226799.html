⚡️ 🔹From this perspective, it can even be said that true loyalty to the martyrs is not in the unquestioning repetition of all their decisions; it is in completing the path, correcting mistakes, and preventing the recurrence of weaknesses. If they sacrificed their lives to defend the country and their ideals, it is not logical for us, in the name of respecting them, to refrain from reforming structures whose inefficiency has become apparent. The boundless sanctification of past decisions sometimes seemingly stems from affection, but in practice, it can turn their legacy into an obstacle to transformation.
🔹More importantly, in professional military culture, a lesson learned only becomes meaningful when no decision and no commander is exempt from scrutiny. After every battle, one must ask: Which assumption was incorrect? Which sign was ignored? Which part of the organization failed to act in time? Where did the enemy think better than us? Who provided an unrealistic assessment? What structure prevented the truth from reaching the commander? And which decision, despite good intentions, did not produce the desired outcome? If the answers to these questions are halted due to the position, popularity, or martyrdom of individuals, the learning cycle will also stop.
🔹Ultimately, my point is neither to deny the status of the martyr nor to disparage our dear martyred commanders; the point is to distinguish between two matters: the spiritual sanctity of a person and the criticizability of their organizational performance! One can love someone, hold them in high esteem, consider them a martyr and a person of high status, but at the same time, evaluate their decisions by professional standards. These two are not contradictory. Conflict begins when affection replaces reason and respect turns into a prohibition of questioning.
🚫I dare to state this reality that some of our failures, surprises, structural weaknesses, and incorrect perceptions are the product of decisions made by those who are now martyrs and beloved! Accepting this truth does not diminish their status but can help us reform. Perhaps the greatest disloyalty to the blood of the martyrs is to sanctify past mistakes in their name and continue on the same path for which future generations will pay the cost; therefore, all those who have accused me and shown severe disrespect to me these days should know that their actions are illogical, irrational, and even irreligious, and they are continuing the wrong path of the past.
