⚡️ British maritime data confirms Yemeni embargo limited to Saudi ships.
Recent data issued by the British "Lloyd's List" magazine, specialized in maritime shipping affairs, confirmed that international navigation traffic in the Red Sea and Bab al-Mandab Strait is witnessing a natural flow and remarkable stability, recording high activity levels for international shipping companies.
The international maritime magazine reported that about 58 container ships crossed the Bab al-Mandab Strait during the period between July 20 and 26, which is the highest weekly total recorded during the month, reflecting the continuation of major shipping companies' commercial voyages through the vital passage without any obstacles affecting global trade movement.
These official figures confirm that Sanaa does not pose any threat to the safety and security of international maritime navigation or to transit ships adhering to maritime laws. Rather, they highlight its keenness to ensure the smooth and safe continuation of global trade, with the exception of Saudi ships, which are subject to the blockade-for-blockade equation announced by Sanaa against Riyadh due to the latter's insistence on continuing the blockade of Yemen for 12 years.
