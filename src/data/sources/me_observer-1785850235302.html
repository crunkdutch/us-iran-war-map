⚡️ Vice President of the Palestinian National Council: Resistance factions made great efforts with mediators to ensure the success of the ceasefire and genocide war agreement... and Israel continues shelling to thwart the agreement
■ The Vice President of the Palestinian National Council and Deputy Secretary-General of the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine conducted a number of interviews with visual media, most notably Al Magharibia, TRT, and Al Alam channels, during which he discussed the ceasefire agreement in the Gaza Strip and future developments.
He affirmed that the resistance factions were up to the level of national responsibility and exerted all necessary efforts to stop the war of extermination and the ceasefire and its implementation in accordance with Security Council Resolution 2803.
He clarified that confining weapons to Palestinian hands, under the responsibility of the National Committee for the Administration of Gaza, remains contingent on Israel's approval of a gradual withdrawal, starting with a return to the Yellow Line and extending to the so-called Gaza envelope settlements.
He indicated that the second phase of the agreement is contingent on Israel's commitment to implementing the first phase, which includes a ceasefire, cessation of assassinations and shelling, the withdrawal of the occupation army to the Yellow Line, and adherence to the humanitarian protocol for the entry of 600 trucks of food daily, in addition to the reconstruction of infrastructure and hospitals and the provision of drinking water.
He added that the Peace Council and US President Donald Trump are responsible before the mediators, guarantors, and the Palestinian people for obliging Israel to the agreement in all its provisions, and for removing it from the systematic maneuvering.
He called for securing the entry of the National Committee, international stabilization forces, and the international verification committee into the Gaza Strip to ensure the implementation of the agreement in all its stages, protect the presence of the Palestinian people, prevent any displacement, and ensure the return of displaced persons to their cities, villages, and camps.
He accused the fascist Israeli extremist government of continuing shelling and killing with the aim of thwarting the agreement, after its failure to achieve its goals, and attempting to exploit Palestinian blood in the Israeli election market.
He affirmed adherence to the role of the mediators from Egypt, Turkey, and Qatar, appreciating their efforts, and calling for the continuation of these efforts to ensure a ceasefire and end the war of extermination and the humanitarian catastrophe experienced by Palestinians in the Gaza Strip ■
