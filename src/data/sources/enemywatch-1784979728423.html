🇮🇶 | 🇮🇶 Iraq’s Popular Mobilization Forces (PMF): Millions expected to attend the funeral of the Martyred Leader:

Lt. Gen. Saad Maan, spokesman for the Higher Committee overseeing the funeral of Martyred Leader Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r), stated that initial estimates indicate millions of mourners are expected to participate in the funeral ceremonies.

Speaking at a press conference held at the PMF’s Directorate of Public Information, Maan said the committee has prepared a comprehensive plan to ensure the smooth movement of participants and the efficient management of large crowds.

He added that coordination meetings with all relevant authorities are ongoing and that all institutions are on full alert and fully prepared to ensure the success of the event, reflecting Iraq’s ability to organize major national occasions.

Maan also announced that another press conference will be held on Sunday or Monday to reveal the final arrangements, noting that the operational plan is in its final stages and has been designed with a high degree of flexibility.

He further confirmed that no funeral procession will be held in Baghdad due to time constraints. Instead, the ceremonies will be limited to the provinces of Najaf and Karbala, within a 24-hour timeframe, with the highest levels of organizational and logistical readiness.
