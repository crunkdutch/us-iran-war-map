⚡️ The Russians did not build this pistol merely to replace the Makarov; its design also addresses a behavioral problem among Russian shooters. For years, most Russian police and security forces fired Makarovs, whose barrel axis is high, causing the muzzle to jump up slightly more during rapid firing. The result? Incorrect muscle memory; Russian shooters unconsciously lower their wrists slightly to compensate for the Makarov's recoil. The Lebedev was designed precisely to correct this habit: the barrel axis has been lowered as much as possible so that the muzzle does not jump up, and the shooter is not forced to make artificial compensations. This point has not been widely discussed, but in initial test sessions, experienced FSB instructors explicitly stated that the Lebedev is intended to eliminate the "incorrect muscle behavior of the Makarov" from the new generation of shooters. Overall, this approach has transformed the Lebedev from a new pistol into a tool for organizational skill correction.
