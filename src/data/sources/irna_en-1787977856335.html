💬 Iranian, Qatari foreign ministers hold talks in Tehran

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has met with his Qatari counterpart, Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani.


During the meeting held on Thursday, the two sides exchanged views on key regional and bilateral issues.
​
Earlier on Wednesday, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei said that Al Thani, who is also prime minister, will meet Iranian officials to discuss “de-escalation” and “create conditions for dialogue.”
​
Pointing to Qatar’s commendable efforts in recent months to help prevent an escalation of regional tensions following the military aggression by the US and the Israeli regime against Iran, he said that the Qatari prime minister would discuss the continuation of Qatar’s mediation efforts and initiatives, as well as other regional developments, during his meetings with Iranian officials.

🔊 Minister warns of hostile plot to sow division in Iran

📌 Hamedan, IRNA – Minister of Justice Amin Hossein Rahimi has warned that attempts to sow division and weaken national unity pose a greater threat to Iran than external pressure, stressing the country will not yield to threats.


Speaking at a ceremony in Hamedan marking Government Week on Thursday, Rahimi said that the resistance of Iran’s Armed Forces and security agencies, along with public support, thwarted the enemy’s objectives in the recent imposed war and its efforts to create insecurity and unrest inside the country.
​
He said that despite years of sanctions and economic pressure, Iran has met its basic needs and overcome challenges through proper management.
​
The justice minister stressed that the enemy is now focusing on creating divisions and polarization in society, warning that weakening national unity could be more damaging than external pressure.
​
He said that differences of opinion are natural, but once legal decisions are made, all officials and political groups must move in one direction to safeguard national security and interests.
​
Rahimi stressed that Iran will not yield to pressure or threats and will defend the rights and interests of its people by relying on the public, the leadership, and domestic capabilities.

🔊 Pezeshkian: No country can secure its interests through excessive demands

📌 Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has emphasized, “No country can secure its interests through excessive demands,” adding that conditions should be created for both countries to benefit from their shared interests through interaction.


During a ceremony on Thursday to see off Iran’s newly appointed ambassadors to South Korea, Belarus, the Czech Republic, Kazakhstan, and Singapore, Pezeshkian emphasized that expanding engagement and strengthening political relations with all countries are key priorities and important missions for Iranian ambassadors abroad.
​
Emphasizing the importance of ongoing efforts to strengthen cooperation in scientific, economic, and political areas, the president said that one of the main responsibilities of Iranian ambassadors abroad is to create an environment conducive to enhancing bilateral cooperation and fostering effective communication to develop relations, ensure security, and establish lasting peace.
​
He also stressed the need for ambassadors to work toward removing obstacles to stronger bilateral ties, adding that Iranian ambassadors should constantly seek to overcome existing challenges and pave the way for the expansion of relations.
​
Pezeshkian highlighted the importance of developing and improving Iran’s foreign interactions and cooperation based on shared interests and a “win-win” approach.
​
He said that Iran’s approach to the international arena has always been peaceful and engagement-oriented and that the country’s ambassadors should properly convey this approach to people around the world.
