🖥 Iran’s Gilan showcases cultural, culinary potential at Armenian festival

📌 Rasht, IRNA – Gilan Province showcased its cultural, culinary and economic potential at the fifth edition of the Navasard Armenian-Iranian Cultural and Culinary Festival that was held on August 10 and 11 in the Armenian town of Sisian, a provincial official has said.


Younes Ranjkesh, director general for investment and employment affairs at the Gilan Governorate, told IRNA on Monday that the two-day festival provided an opportunity to introduce the province’s products and capabilities to the Armenian market and could serve as a model for similar events in other neighboring countries.
​
The festival, held at the initiative of Iran’s embassy in Armenia, featured exhibitions, a cooking competition and cultural programs, he said.
​
The official added that 45 Gilan artists and traders took part, presenting food products, handicrafts and other local goods.
​
A chef representing Rasht Municipality won first place and a gold medal in the cooking competition. Folk music and traditional performances by Gilan’s Deylamoon group also drew attention, according to the local official.
​
A sister-city agreement was also signed between Lahijan in Gilan and Sisian to expand cultural, tourism and economic cooperation, Ranjkesh said.

🔻 18th National Handicrafts Exhibition opens in Iran’s Hamedan

📌 Hamedan, IRNA — The 18th National Handicrafts Exhibition opened with the presence of the Governor of Hamedan and the deputy minister of Cultural Heritage, Tourism, and Handicrafts, at the city's permanent international fair ground on Monday.


Featuring handicrafts and traditional arts from 29 provinces across the country, displayed in 181 booths, the exhibition will be open to visitors every day from 4:00 PM to 9:30 PM until August 21.
​
Speaking on the sidelines of the event, the Director General of Cultural Heritage, Tourism, and Handicrafts of Hamedan Province, Mohsen Masoum Alizadeh, told IRNA that 53 booths are from Hamedan, and 33 are from other parts of the province.
​
Precious and semi-precious stones, leather goods, woodturning, wood marquetry, mirror work, pottery painting, gilding, crocheting, carpet repairing, Pateh embroidery, traditional embroidery, doll weaving, traditional jewelry, turquoise inlay, copper engraving, and geometric woodwork are among the products and handicrafts on display at this exhibition, Alizadeh said.
​
Pottery and ceramics, leather goods, carving and furniture, kilim weaving, cutting of precious and semi-precious stones, wood and metal marquetry, copper engraving, woodturning, and bead weaving are other handicrafts displayed at this exhibition, he added.

🔊 Araghchi honors martyred Iranian teachers, students as symbols of resistance

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has paid tribute to teachers and students who were killed in the US-Israeli aggression against Iran, stressing the need to preserve the historical record of the war and pass it on to future generations.


Speaking at the 39th Conference of Heads of Education Departments across Iran on Monday, Araghchi outlined the diplomatic efforts made during the military aggression and emphasized the role of the education system in documenting the historic epic of the Iranian people.
​
He specifically honored the martyred teachers and students of Shajareh Tayyebeh School in Minab, southern Iran, describing them as enduring symbols of the country's resistance.
​
“The 40-day imposed war had its heroes; from the martyred Leader of Iran (Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei) to the martyred students and teachers of Shajareh Tayyebeh School in Minab, whose innocent martyrdom became an enduring epic,” he said.
​
He added that the deliberate missile attack on the school, which killed 168 students and teachers “in a single moment,” was a crime that would never be erased from the historical memory of Iranians or the conscience of humanity.
​
Araghchi said Iran’s resilience and victory rested on reliance on God, the leadership of the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, national unity and the strong defense provided by the Iranian armed forces.
​
On the first day of the US-Israeli war of aggression against Iran on February 28, the Shajareh Tayyebeh elementary school in the southern Iranian city of Minab was targeted in a US missile strike, killing at least 175 people, including 168 schoolgirls.
