📢 | Statements:

1. Iranian MP: Mashhad farewell ceremony changed to funeral procession

An Iranian Member of Parliament said that Mashhad was originally scheduled to host a farewell ceremony for Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (QS). However, by the directive of the Leader of the Revolution, the event was changed into a funeral procession.

He added that if ground transportation is feasible, the procession will proceed by road. Otherwise, the transfer will be carried out by special helicopters.

2. Expediency Council member: Sayyid Mujtaba Khamenei served as a key liaison with the Armed Forces

Gholam Reza Mesbahi Moghadam, a member of Iran's Expediency Discernment Council, said that Ayatullah Sayyid Mujtaba Khamenei was entrusted by the Leader with matters related to the Armed Forces, and part of the communication between the Leader and the military was conducted through him.

He added that Sayyid Mujtaba was also entrusted with regional affairs, and that Martyr General Qassem Soleimani's communication with the Leader was conducted through him.

3. Babil Governorate declares two-day public holiday ahead of Imam Khamenei's funeral procession

To facilitate the participation of residents of Babil Province and mourners from across Iraq in the funeral procession of Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (QS), the Babil Provincial Council has declared Wednesday, July 8, and Thursday, July 9, 2026, as official public holidays.

The decision excludes security and essential service institutions.

4. Ayatullah Sheikh Isa Qassim: Millions bid farewell to Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei

Ayatullah Sheikh Isa Qassim, Bahrain's senior Shia scholar, said that millions across the Islamic world are bidding farewell to the great scholar, jurist, and revolutionary, Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei, who devoted his life to Islam, the Ummah, and humanity.

He added that the leadership of Imam Khomeini and Imam Khamenei opened the doors of goodness to the world and closed the doors of evil, praying that Allah grant the Islamic Republic of Iran, the Islamic Resistance, and the faithful Ummah a near, lasting, and honorable victory.
