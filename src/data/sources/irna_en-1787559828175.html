🔻 Iran rejects interference by two French diplomats

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has said the interventionist conduct of two French diplomats in Iran is completely unacceptable.


Speaking to reporters on Sunday, Baqaei said Iran promptly responded after determining that the French Embassy in Tehran had exceeded its diplomatic mandate, violated the provisions of the Vienna Convention and engaged in interference in Iran’s domestic affairs.
​
He said Iran had officially announced that the two diplomats involved in such activities must leave the country and would not be allowed to return.
​
Relations between countries, including Iran and France, must be based on mutual respect and adherence to international law, including the principle of non-interference in the internal affairs of states, he added.
​
“Accordingly, Iran regulates its relations with all countries, and whenever necessary, issues warnings and takes the required measures,” Baqaei noted.
​
He said France’s subsequent action was unjustifiable, describing it as a reaction to a completely legal measure taken by Iran in accordance with international law. Such actions, he added, would be detrimental to the continuation of Iran-France relations.
​
On August 18, Iran’s Foreign Ministry declared two French diplomats working at the French Embassy in Tehran persona non grata.
​
The move came a few days after Iran’s Intelligence Ministry said the two diplomats had taken part in a secret meeting in Tehran in July with suspects in a major case involving foreign infiltration and interference. The Foreign Ministry said the diplomats’ activities had violated international law, particularly the 1961 Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations.

🌍 Isfahan nuclear fuel plant was first target of US-Israeli assaults: Nuclear chief

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The head of the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) says the nuclear fuel production plant in Isfahan was the first target of military assaults by the United States and Israel during their wars of aggression against the Islamic Republic over the past year.


Speaking at a ceremony marking Doctors’ Day in the nuclear industry on Sunday, Mohammad Eslami said the plant produces the fuel required by the Tehran Research Reactor.
​
“The Tehran reactor is a research reactor and is mainly used for research and production work related to radiopharmaceuticals,” Eslami said.
​
He added that a radiopharmaceutical production facility was also among the facilities hit in the air raids.
​
Eslami further pointed to the bombing of an oxygen-18 production line at the Khondab complex, saying the material is needed for PET scans and is produced domestically to support the country’s PET scan services.
​
He said the acts of aggression were “a clear and naked symbol of their school of thought,” arguing that the targets were scientific activities whose products are used by the public.
​
According to Eslami, products made by the AEOI are used annually by 1.5 million patients or people seeking medical care for diagnostic purposes, while several countries are now destinations for Iran’s nuclear-industry exports.
​
The US and Israel have launched two unprovoked wars against Iran since June 2025 that have claimed the lives of thousands of civilians.

🌍 Friendship associations can support Iran’s public diplomacy: Foreign Ministry spokesperson

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has highlighted the role of friendship associations and civil society organizations in strengthening Iran’s public diplomacy and expanding people-to-people ties, announcing plans to establish a working group with activists in the field within the next two months.


Speaking at a meeting marking the national week of civil society organizations and social participation on Sunday, Baqaei said non-governmental organizations were both a product of a strong civil society and an important force for its further development. He noted that many such groups, particularly friendship associations, maintain links with international communities even when their activities are primarily domestic.
​
Baqaei described friendship associations as valuable partners for the Foreign Ministry in both formal diplomacy and public diplomacy. He said the ministry would share its experience with civil society activists and seek greater coordination with them on international issues.
​
He also stressed the importance of documenting environmental damage resulting from recent US and Israeli military aggressions against Iran and raising international awareness about their consequences, arguing that environmental impacts extend beyond national borders.
​
The spokesperson said Iranian NGOs could also help present the country’s positions internationally on issues including sanctions and violations of international humanitarian law. He added that the Foreign Ministry could facilitate their participation in international forums, including UN Human Rights Council sessions, where appropriate.
​
Baqaei further said NGOs could contribute to documenting criminal attacks in Minab and Lamerd for historical records and potential presentation to domestic and international judicial bodies. He maintained that civil society organizations could help communicate Iran’s experiences and perspectives to global public opinion.
​
He emphasized that friendship associations also contribute to people-to-people relations and can provide valuable cultural and diplomatic links with communities abroad. According to Baqaei, identifying these capacities and using them more effectively would strengthen Iran’s public diplomacy and broaden the country’s international outreach.

⚡️ Iran announces discovery of major new hydrocarbon reserves

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Oil Minister Mohsen Paknejad announces the discovery of major new hydrocarbon reserves in the country, describing the finds as a significant asset for Iran’s future energy needs.


Paknejad made the announcement on the sidelines of a ceremony on Sunday, marking the renewal of allegiance to the ideals of the late Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Imam Khomeini, on the occasion of Government Week.
​
The minister said more than 7.5 trillion cubic feet of gas has been discovered in southern Fars province, adding that around 5.7 trillion cubic feet of the reserves are estimated to be recoverable.
​
Responding to a question about the impact of economic sanctions and pressure on Iran, Paknejad rejected fears of an illegal US blockade, saying, “I have good news to share about the discovery of new hydrocarbon reserves.”
​
He added that further details about the discovery would be announced at an appropriate time.
​
“By the grace of God and through the relentless efforts and hard work of our colleagues, a vast wealth of hydrocarbon reserves has been discovered, which will serve as a very strong foundation for the country’s energy future. Let’s start with the good news,” Paknejad said.
​
The announcement comes as Iran continues to face extensive economic sanctions that have affected key sectors of its economy, particularly its oil industry and access to international markets.
​
Despite the economic pressure, Iran has continued efforts to develop its domestic energy resources and strengthen its capacity to produce and export energy.
