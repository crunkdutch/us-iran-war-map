⚡️ Correspondent Karakhana Tahsin "Al-Jadeed" The Lebanese negotiating delegation, headed by Ambassador Simon Karam, departs Beirut for Rome to participate in a new round of negotiations.
⚡️ Al-Akhbar learned that senior officials in the authority received a series of notes over the past two weeks from *regional and international officials warning of the disadvantages of involvement in such an agreement with "Israel"*. However, this did not prompt them to amend their approach, but rather to expand the Lebanese delegation participating in the Rome round by including legal and economic experts concerned with border and reconstruction files.
While Ambassador Simon Karam heads the delegation, with the participation of Lebanon's Ambassador to the United States Nada Hamadeh Maawad, in addition to the military delegation consisting of six officers, Al-Akhbar learned that the delegation also expanded to include the President of the Republic's economic advisor Roua Harati, and lawyer Antoine Safir. However, the most prominent was the inclusion of the expert in the maritime border file, Najib Masihi, in addition to an officer in the Lebanese army from the Al-Khazen family, in a move that suggests that Lebanon agrees to what the enemy's foreign minister Gideon Sa'ar had previously announced some time ago about "Israel's" intention to cancel the maritime border agreement.
While Lebanon tries to present itself as a party seeking to establish stability through institutions and agreements, "Israel" deals with the negotiations as a tool to gain time and improve its conditions, not a path to implement its obligations. It accompanies the political process, but at the same time continues its military operations, thereby consolidating new facts on the ground and redrawing the border scene according to the logic of force.
Has Lebanon agreed to re-discuss with Israel the maritime demarcation of economic zones?
In this context, informed sources told Al-Akhbar that the enemy succeeded in convincing the United States to prepare new working papers related to the framework agreement, and that the US Central Command (CENTCOM), which coordinates communications between the Lebanese army and the occupation forces, presented a draft executive protocol that defines the mechanisms of work in the executive phase of the security annex. According to the sources, the American side adopted a large part of the Israeli vision in this draft, especially the clauses that pressure the Lebanese army, either to push it into a confrontation with Hezbollah or with the residents of the border villages, or to show it as incapable of fulfilling the required obligations, which gives "Israel" a pretext to maintain its occupation and refrain from any withdrawal.
The sources add that the Israeli proposals include a comprehensive survey of the southern villages, including counting the number of homes and the names of their owners, shops, and religious and social institutions, leading to demanding the Lebanese army to prevent the return of some villagers, based on lists prepared by Israeli intelligence of those it claims are elements or military leaders in Hezbollah, and that their presence poses a security threat to Israel. Tel Aviv also reiterated its old demand to conduct inspections of homes and private property without the need to obtain a judicial warrant.
