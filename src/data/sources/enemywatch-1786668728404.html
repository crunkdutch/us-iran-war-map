🇾🇪 ❌ 🇸🇦 | Yemen continues to attack Saudi-terror forces operating inside Yemen, with attacks ongoing every hour:

In the past hours, Drone attacks were reported near Maashiq Palace in Saudi forces's occupied Aden, accompanied by explosions. Air defenses were also active over Aden. Separately, reports emerged of drone attacks targeting positions of Saudi-terror forces in southern Yemen. Saudi-terror forces also intensified artillery shelling against populated villages in the areas of Hadhran and Al-Ruba’i in At-Ta’ziyah District.

AnsarAllah carried out repeated missile and drone attacks against Al-Mokha, targeting gatherings, camps, strongholds and weapons depots of Saudi-terror forces. Explosions were reported in the port, including a third explosion, while Yemeni forces launched another missile salvo targeting Saudi military supplies. The attacks marked the second assault on the port city in three days, with another wave of drones subsequently launched toward Saudi-backed camps and strongholds. Reports also stated that AnsarAllah launched ballistic missiles and drones toward Al-Mokha.

Explosions were heard in Ma’rib, where AnsarAllah struck gatherings of Saudi-terror forces with missiles and drones. AnsarAllah also shelled Bayhan al-Qisab in the direction of Ma’rib, an area controlled by the pro-Aden Shabwah Emergency Forces. Large AnsarAllah convoys from Mawiyah were reported moving toward frontline positions in al-Husha, al-Baher and al-Musaymir in the direction of Ad Dali. Meanwhile, Presidential Leadership Council forces attacked AnsarAllah positions around western Ma’rib with rockets and artillery.

AnsarAllah also eliminated senior officers belonging to Saudi-backed militias on Sunday during direct and precise strikes on Saudi positions. Brigadier General Yahya Mohammed Al-Qadi and Staff Colonel Ramzi Mohammed were killed in the strikes.

Nasr al-Din Amer, Deputy Head of the Media Authority of AnsarAllah, stated that the Yemeni Armed Forces are targeting Saudi military gatherings and weapons depots, which he said had been deployed against the Yemeni population. He said the Saudi record was known worldwide and argued that Saudi media reports portraying the attacks otherwise were fabrications intended to distort the stated objectives of the Yemeni armed forces. He described lifting the blockade on Yemen as the central objective and said the blockade had produced what the United Nations describes as the world’s worst man-made humanitarian catastrophe. He further argued that those responsible for the blockade and years of military raids had no right to express concern over Yemeni civilians while continuing the blockade, and said those fighting to break it were acting as a protective shield for their people.

Meanwhile, the response blockade is in effect: Saudi Aramco postponed the reopening of the Jazan oil refinery to August 30, from the previously announced August 15 date. The refinery has remained shut since July 27 following a Yemeni attack, and AnsarAllah attacked it again the previous day. Saudi Arabia’s Ministry of Energy confirmed a fire but provided no further operational details. The continued shutdown is expected to place additional pressure on global fuel markets, which are already affected by disruptions at regional refineries and the war in Ukraine.
