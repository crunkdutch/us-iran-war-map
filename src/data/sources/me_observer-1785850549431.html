⚡️ In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful

Press Release issued by the Popular Resistance Committees in Palestine

On the occasion of the second anniversary of the martyrdom of the great Islamic, Jihadi, and national leader Dr. Ismail Haniyeh "Abu Al-Abed":

The Popular Resistance Committees in Palestine recall, with great pride and reverence, the second anniversary of the martyrdom of the great Islamic, Jihadi, and national leader Dr. Ismail Haniyeh "Abu Al-Abed," with whose departure Palestine lost a steadfast national figure who was a symbol of unity and steadfastness, and an emblem of resistance and devotion to the Palestinian cause.

The martyred leader Dr. Ismail Haniyeh represented a unifying national model with his responsible and proactive presence in various stages, and with his positions that transcended organizational affiliation, to become a sincere unifying voice that rose above disagreements, and always sided with the supreme national interest, far from narrow alignments and factional calculations.

The great martyred leader "Abu Al-Abed" remained steadfast in his principles until the last moment, engaged in the field of confrontation alongside his people, aware of the magnitude of the challenges, and believing that national unity and resistance are the shortest path to freedom and liberation.

The martyrdom of the great leader Abu Al-Abed Haniyeh came at a critical stage in which our Palestinian people are engaged in an open confrontation amidst the ongoing aggression on the Gaza Strip, and his departure constituted a great loss for a unifying national leadership figure who was present in various fields and pivotal stages.

We extend a tribute of reverence and respect to the martyr's family, which presented the finest examples of steadfastness and sacrifice, and from among its members dozens of martyrs ascended during the aggression on the Gaza Strip, including a number of the children and grandchildren of the martyred leader Ismail Haniyeh.

Fidelity to the path of the martyred Islamic, Jihadi, and great national leader Ismail Haniyeh requires continuing to follow his approach, by strengthening the bonds of national unity on the basis of full partnership, and continuing the struggle in all its forms until the suffering of our Palestinian people ends, and confronting all projects that target their national rights.

We renew our pledge to the martyred leader Ismail Haniyeh, and to all the martyrs of our Palestinian people, that we will remain loyal to their pure blood, and continue on the path of liberation and return, until our Palestinian people achieve all their legitimate national rights.

Popular Resistance Committees in Palestine.

Friday: July 31, 2026 AD, Safar 17, 1448 AH
