🔊 Iranian Army vows stronger response to any possible aggression

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Army has warned that it will respond with an even harsher and more crushing blow to any possible aggression.


In a statement issued on Monday to mark the anniversary of Operation Mersad, the Army said the operation remains a symbol of Iran’s strength, resilience, and the determination of the country’s armed forces to defend the nation.
​
It described the fifth day of the Iranian month of Mordad as one of the “golden pages” in the history of Iran’s resistance, saying it demonstrated that the Army and the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic are always prepared to confront “the ruthless enemy and the mercenaries and hirelings of global arrogance” and “will cut off the feet of any aggressor that steps on even an atom of the homeland’s soil.”
​
On July 26, 1988, the terrorist group Mujahadeen-e-Khalq (MEK, MKO), with the support of the Iraqi military, started their Operation Forough Javidan (Eternal Light) in central Iran.
​
The Iranian army launched its counter-attack, Operation Mersad (Ambush), which became the last major military operation of the Iraq-Iran War, ending in a decisive victory for Iran.
​
The Army’s statement said Operation Mersad reflected the Iranian nation’s resolve to confront enemy aggression and represented one of the greatest displays of unity between the people and the armed forces during the Sacred Defense.
​
It credited the operation’s success to the “wise strategy” of the martyred Lieutenant General Ali Sayyad Shirazi, whose military planning and command resulted in what it described as a decisive victory over the invading forces.
​
Referring to the 12-Day War, the Ramadan War, and the Battle of Hormuz, the Army said the Iranian armed forces had once again demonstrated their indigenous power, national resolve, and faith.
​
The Army of the Islamic Republic of Iran emphasizes that “it will not show the slightest negligence in safeguarding the independence and territorial integrity of the Islamic homeland and will answer the enemies’ aggressions with even more severe and crushing attacks,” the statement added.

📡 Ukraine’s attack on Iranian vessel violates states’ obligations to protect human life: Top rights body

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s High Council for Human Rights has condemned Ukraine’s military attack on an Iranian commercial vessel in the Caspian Sea, calling it an unjustifiable act that violated international law and states’ obligations to protect human life and security.


In a statement released on Sunday, the council said targeting a commercial ship and endangering the lives of civilians has serious humanitarian consequences and demonstrates a clear disregard for the fundamental principles of international humanitarian law and the international obligations of states to protect the lives and security of individuals.
​
The full text of the statement is as follows:
​
The High Council for Human Rights of the Islamic Republic of Iran strongly condemns the military attack by Ukraine on an Iranian merchant vessel in the Caspian Sea, which resulted in the death of one of the vessel’s crew members and the injury of another, and considers it an illegal act, contrary to the fundamental principles of international law, and a clear violation of the fundamental right to life of human beings.
​
Targeting a merchant vessel and endangering the lives of civilians constitute an unjustifiable act that result in grave humanitarian consequences and demonstrates a blatant disregard for the fundamental principles of international humanitarian law and for States' international obligations to protect the lives and security of individuals.
​
The High Council for Human Rights of the Islamic Republic of Iran emphasizes that the right to life, as one of the most fundamental human rights, must never be sacrificed for political or military objectives or in the course of inter-State conflicts. Any deliberate or reckless act that places the lives of defenseless individuals and civilian personnel in the maritime transport sector at risk warrants the strongest condemnation by the international community and the pursuit of accountability for those responsible.
​
This act, in addition to violating the principles of the Charter of the United Nations concerning the prohibition of the use of force and respect for the territorial integrity and sovereignty of States, constitutes a threat to the safety of navigation, regional stability, and international peace and security. The transformation of international waterways into battlefield for military operations and the targeting of merchant vessels represent a dangerous course of conduct that may give rise to far-reaching humanitarian and security consequences.
​
The Islamic Republic of Iran reiterates that it has not interfered in the conflict between Russia and Ukraine and has always emphasized the need to resolve disputes through dialogue, respect for international law, and refrain from resorting to force. However, the attack on the merchant vessel of the Islamic Republic of Iran in the Caspian Sea, in addition to being a clear violation of the fundamental rules of international law and the principles of the United Nations Charter, is also a dangerous act aimed at expanding the scope of the conflict and threatening regional and international peace and security. The High Council for Human Rights of the Islamic Republic of Iran calls on the United Nations Security Council, the Human Rights Council, the Secretary-General of the United Nations, and all responsible international governments and institutions to categorically condemn this act against a merchant vessel and its civilian passengers, avoiding any selective approach.

🔻 Germany approves nearly €800m of arms sales to Israel

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Germany has approved nearly 800 million euros in arms export licenses to the Israeli regime during the first five months of 2026, surpassing the combined total of the previous 20 months, the Berlin government has confirmed.


The approvals reveal a dramatic surge in weapons exports to Israel from one of its most steadfast European allies, despite the German government’s stated concerns over the military destruction in Gaza.
​
According to German officials, more than 60 percent of the total license value is tied to a single, unique project. While the German government declined to comment further, industry analysts believe this project involves a nuclear-capable submarine built by the manufacturer ThyssenKrupp Marine Systems (TKMS).
​
Germany remains one of the world's leading arms exporters. Data published by the country's economics ministry in mid-July shows that Berlin granted 13.87 billion euros in defense export licenses in the first half of 2026—more than quadrupling the figures recorded during the same period in 2025.

💬 Iran says Strait of Hormuz talks with Oman constructive, unrelated to US

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says that ongoing bilateral talks with Oman regarding the management of shipping traffic in the Strait of Hormuz have been constructive and beneficial, and they are unrelated to the United States.


During his weekly press conference in Tehran on Monday, Baqaei highlighted that negotiations between Iran and Oman are solely between the two neighboring countries, adding that the goal of these talks is to establish mechanisms for safe shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, while respecting Iran’s sovereign rights and national interests. He emphasized that these discussions have nothing to do with the United States.
​
Baqaei also said that the Strait of Hormuz remains closed due to the aggressive actions of the US, which have created insecurity in the region, confirming that there have been no changes in the status of the strait.
​
In response to a joint statement from the Persian Gulf Cooperation Council and Jordan regarding Iran’s reprisal attacks, Baqaei described their comments as a distortion of reality. He urged these countries to instead condemn the United States for violating the memorandum of understanding (MoU).
​
Baqaei expressed concern that some regional countries have knowingly aligned themselves with US military aggression against Iran, calling on them to reconsider their stance.
​
He urged regional countries to prevent the US from misusing their lands and to stop political posturing.
​
China a strategic partner of Iran with shared concern over US unilateralism
​
Referring to Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi’s meeting with his Chinese counterpart on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit, Baqaei described China as Iran’s strategic partner, stating that the two countries share mutual interests but also have common concerns about regional and global peace and security, particularly regarding the destructive unilateralism of the United States.
​
Blacklisting IRGC by Britain a breach of international law
​
Additionally, the foreign ministry spokesperson condemned Britain’s decision to classify the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) as a threat to its national security, describing this action as a political maneuver that violates international law and is intended to exert pressure on Iran.
​
Baqaei highlighted the IRGC’s role in defending Iran and combating Daesh (ISIS) terrorism, asserting that such labeling lacks any legal basis. He affirmed that the Islamic Republic of Iran would take necessary measures to counter this unjust British action.
