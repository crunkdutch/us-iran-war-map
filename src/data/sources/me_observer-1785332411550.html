⚡️ Ammonium Nitrate in Houla...
Military source clarifies the truth about the discovered quantity
The announcement by UNIFIL forces about the discovery of about 4,000 kilograms of ammonium nitrate in the town of Houla, southern Lebanon, sparked a wide wave of interaction among Lebanese, especially since this substance was associated in the minds of many with the Beirut port explosion that occurred in August 2020. The news quickly topped social media platforms, where reactions were divided between those who expressed their fears of the presence of large quantities of this substance, and those who linked the issue to the ongoing political tensions in Lebanon.
Military source: Indications of possible Israeli use
In contrast, a military source clarified that Israeli forces, during their military operations in southern Lebanon, used containers or "gallons" designated for carrying out demolition operations targeting buildings, tunnels, and underground facilities.
The source added that the discovery of Hebrew writings on the ammonium nitrate containers, which was also indicated by Agence France-Presse, may point to the possibility of this substance being used by Israeli forces in demolition operations aimed at causing widespread destruction in ground or underground facilities, while emphasizing that this information still requires official investigations to determine the source of the substance and the party that used it.
Why is ammonium nitrate considered a dangerous substance?
Ammonium nitrate is primarily used in the manufacture of agricultural fertilizers, but it can turn into a highly dangerous substance if stored in large quantities or used under unsafe conditions or for military purposes, which makes the discovery of large quantities of it raise the concern of security authorities and relevant parties.
Investigations continue
Until now, official Lebanese authorities or UNIFIL forces have not issued final details about the source of the discovered quantity or the purpose of its presence in the area, while ongoing investigations are expected to reveal more information in the coming period.
