🔻 Tehran, Moscow stress scientific cooperation

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to Moscow and the Russian president’s scientific aide have agreed to make the necessary arrangements for exchanging high‑level scientific and knowledge‑based delegations.


In a meeting on Monday between Iran’s ambassador to Moscow Kazem Jalali and Andrei Fursenko, an aide to the Russian president and former science minister, the two sides reviewed academic and scientific ties and discussed new prospects for cooperation.
​
Both sides stressed expanding university, research and knowledge‑based collaboration, and emphasized emerging technologies and joint research and development.

🔊 Pakistani interior minister calls talks with Iranian officials constructive

📌 Islamabad, IRNA – Pakistan’s Interior Minister Syed Mohsin Raza Naqvi says that meetings with Iranian officials in Tehran were very positive and constructive, expressing hope that the diplomatic efforts would help pave the way for lasting peace in the region.


Naqvi, who accompanied Pakistan Army Chief Field Marshal Asim Munir during his official visit to Iran, said he and the army chief held a productive meeting with President Masoud Pezeshkian.
​
He said the discussions focused on the Iran-US dispute, tensions in the Middle East and the path forward, adding that President Pezeshkian openly shared Iran’s views and concerns.
​
Naqvi said significant progress had been made during the talks and described the outcome as very positive. He expressed hope that the momentum would contribute to further progress and lasting regional peace.
​
During the meeting, President Pezeshkian stressed the need for the United States to honor its commitments to Iran in accordance with international law and Iran’s rights.
​
Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf also said Iran was pursuing implementation of the Islamabad memorandum of understanding and stressed that Washington must fulfill its commitments under the agreement.
