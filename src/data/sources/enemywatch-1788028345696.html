In the Name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful.

I pray for the elevation of their ranks and Allah’s mercy upon them.

In the current sensitive and complex circumstances, where the goal is achieving “an even stronger Iran,” one of the essential requirements, alongside tireless effort and continuous innovation, is to explain and narrate the power and strength of Iran and its noble people.

Weaknesses, shortcomings, and deficiencies do not need to be highlighted; they require planning and action to solve them. The people of Iran, who over the past six months have demonstrated their true character through jihad against the enemy, their presence in the field, unity, solidarity, and mutual support, deserve to feel the sincere service of the country’s institutions as they strive to reach the lofty standards worthy of this sacred system and this honorable nation.
