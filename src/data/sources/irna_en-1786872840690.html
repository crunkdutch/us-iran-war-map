💬 Iran, Tajikistan stress speeding up transport cooperation

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran and Tajikistan have stressed the need to accelerate the implementation of bilateral cooperation in rail, road, air, port, and logistics sectors.


Minister of Roads and Urban Development Farzaneh Sadegh met with Tajik Minister of Energy and Water Resources Daler Juma and Transport Minister Azim Ibrohim, according to Iran’s Ministry of Roads and Urban Development.
​
During the meeting, Sadegh pointed to an increase in international rail freight between the two countries in 2025, saying the growth demonstrated the depth of bilateral ties and the administration’s emphasis on transport diplomacy.
​
She said the Chabahar-Zahedan railway would become operational within three months, noting that track-laying had been completed.
​
Chabahar could help Tajikistan develop a logistics hub and gain access to international waters, she added.
​
Sadegh also welcomed Tajik investment in Chabahar Port and Tehran’s Aprin dry port, inviting Tajik officials to visit the facility.

💬 Six Iranian universities make Shanghai Ranking’s top 1,000

📌 Shiraz, IRNA – Six Iranian universities have been included among the world’s top 1,000 universities in the latest Shanghai Ranking for 2026, according to the head of the Islamic World Science and Technology Monitoring and Citation Institute (ISC).


Mohammad-Mehdi Alavian-Mehr announced the figure on Sunday, saying that, as in previous years, three US institutions, namely Harvard University, Stanford University and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), ranked first, second and third respectively in the 2026 edition of the ranking.
​
He described the Shanghai Ranking as one of the world’s most prestigious university rankings, whose results are published annually by Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
​
The ISC chief said Tehran University of Medical Sciences and the University of Tehran jointly took the top spot among Iranian universities, both ranking in the 501–600 bracket.
​
Amirkabir University of Technology ranked third among Iranian universities, placing in the 801–900 bracket, he noted, adding that Shahid Beheshti University of Medical Sciences, Sharif University of Technology and Tarbiat Modares University were also included in the ranking, each placing in the 901–1,000 bracket.

💬 Iran’s Armed Forces vow to continue resistance until ‘complete defeat’ of enemies

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran has pledged to continue resistance against the United States and the Israeli regime in the region until the complete defeat of its enemies and the fulfillment of the Iranian people’s demands.


In a statement issued Sunday to mark the anniversary of the return of Iranian prisoners of war following the 1980–1988 Iran–Iraq War, the General Staff praised the former prisoners and veterans of the conflict, describing their experience as a symbol of resistance, self-reliance and national resilience.
​
The statement said the legacy of the eight-year war had been passed on to younger generations, who, according to the General Staff, have continued to uphold the principles of resistance in confronting US military power.
​
It also referred to more than 160 consecutive days of public expressions of support for Iran’s Armed Forces and calls for retaliation over the assassination of the former Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, according to the statement.
​
The General Staff said the country’s experience of wartime resistance remained a source of national determination and opposition to foreign pressure.
​
The statement concluded by commending the Iranian people for their steadfastness and patience, while pledging that the Armed Forces would not retreat from the legitimate demands of the public and the country’s leadership.

🎙 Iran urges Qatar to allow Air Force fact-finding team over missing pilots

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Commander of the Missing in Action Search Committee of Iran’s Armed Forces General Staff has urged Qatar to allow an Iranian Air Force fact-finding team to enter the country and investigate the status of Iranian pilots held by the country.


Brigadier General Seyyed Mohammad Baqerzadeh made the remarks on Sunday after Qatar’s Foreign Ministry denied reports that Iranian pilots were being held captive in the country.
​
“Instead of denying the matter and creating restrictions on the path to finding the truth, the Qatari government should allow the fact-finding and expert team of the Islamic Republic of Iran Air Force to travel to Qatar,” he said.
​
According to the commander, a team of highly experienced Iranian Air Force experts has been waiting for several months to enter Qatar and conduct a field investigation.
​
He said repeated obstruction and delays by Qatari officials have so far prevented the return of the Iranian pilots to the country.
​
Baqerzadeh also stressed the need for the International Committee of the Red Cross to urgently follow up on the issue.
​
He further warned that Kuwait had similarly avoided providing information about the fate of four Iranians held by the country.
​
The remarks came after Qatar denied holding Iranian pilots in secret detention following the downing of their aircraft during hostilities earlier this year.

💬 East Azarbaijan prepares for post-war tourism market with major investment: Official

📌 Tabriz, IRNA – Iran’s East Azarbaijan Province is preparing for a recovery in international tourism following the recent war of aggression by the US and Israel, with billions of dollars invested in tourism projects and 36 hotels currently under construction, according to the provincial tourism chief.


Ahmad Hamzehzadeh, head of the Cultural Heritage, Tourism, and Handicrafts Department of East Azarbaijan Province, told IRNA on Saturday that the recent conflict had created a new opportunity to introduce Iran’s cultural identity, history and civilization to international audiences and potentially encourage foreign visitors to experience the country firsthand.
​
He said foreign tourist arrivals at official accommodation centers in the province fell in the first three months of the current Iranian year (March 21-June 21), as regional tensions led travelers to postpone trips and tour operators to plan more cautiously.
​
Despite the downturn, tourism infrastructure development has continued, Hamzehzadeh said, adding that authorities were easing investment procedures, including waiving construction fees for hotels and reducing administrative barriers.
​
He stressed that accommodation and tourism infrastructure must be developed before demand returns, so the northwestern province is ready when international travel gradually recovers.
