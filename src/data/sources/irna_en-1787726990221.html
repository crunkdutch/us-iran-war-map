💬 Ghalibaf: Iran will not yield to pressure; US must honor Islamabad MoU

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says Iran will not yield to pressure, calling on the US to honor its commitments under the Islamabad memorandum of understanding (MoU).


During a meeting with Pakistan’s Army Chief Field Marshal Asim Munir in Tehran on Monday, Ghalibaf reviewed instances where the United States has failed to uphold its commitments under the Islamabad MoU.
​
Ghalibaf, who leads the Iranian negotiating team, emphasized that the obligations of both parties under the Islamabad MoU are clear, pointing out that the United States’ failure to fulfill its commitments has hindered regional stability and further eroded trust in Washington.
​
He also rejected the notion that external pressure could influence Iran, asserting that Tehran remains committed to implementing the terms of the MoU.
​
Ghalibaf said that the United States must honor its commitments as stipulated in the agreement.
​
For his part, Asim Munir said that he would continue his efforts to help restore stability in the region.

💬 President Pezeshkian: US coercion and bullying will not work

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and Pakistani Army Chief Asim Munir have stressed the need for a shift in US policy toward Iran, strict adherence to the Islamabad Agreement, and stronger Islamic unity to counter regional threats during high-level talks in Tehran on Monday.


During a meeting with Field Marshal Asim Munir on Monday evening, President Pezeshkian expressed gratitude for Pakistan’s constructive mediation efforts in promoting regional peace and stability, voicing confidence that joint cooperation would realize the goals of the Islamabad agreement.
​
President Pezeshkian urged Washington to respect international law and honor its commitments toward Iran. He noted that the US must alter its posture toward Tehran, warning that reliance on coercion only complicates diplomatic efforts.
​
Pointing to the Iranian public’s resilience, President Pezeshkian said that national unity would allow Iran to withstand external pressures and threats.
​
Highlighting regional peace and Muslim solidarity as core priorities, Pezeshkian affirmed Iran’s readiness to expand economic, political, and security cooperation with neighboring Islamic nations. He added that unity among Muslim countries serves as the primary deterrent against plots by the Israeli regime and adversaries of Islam.
​
For his part, General Asim Munir commended President Pezeshkian’s efforts toward regional stability, emphasizing that thorough diplomatic processes are essential to achieving a comprehensive agreement.
​
General Munir praised the trajectory of bilateral relations, noting that the shared historical, religious, and cultural ties between Iran and Pakistan provide a foundation for navigating current challenges. Reaffirming Pakistan’s commitment to its mediating role, General Munir expressed optimism that resolving the present crisis would mark the beginning of a new era of regional development and collective security.

🔻 Pezeshkian administration appointed more women to managerial posts than previous governments: VP

📌 Zanjan, IRNA – Iran’s Vice President for Women and Family Affairs, Zahra Behrouz-Azar, says that the administration of President Masoud Pezeshkian has appointed more women to managerial positions than its predecessors, including at senior executive levels.


Speaking at a meeting of the Zanjan Provincial Administrative Council on Monday evening, Behrouz-Azar said the appointments reflected the Pezeshkian administration’s attention to women’s capabilities and potential.
​
She also pointed to women’s achievements in scientific and managerial fields, saying women account for more than 60 percent of applicants seeking admission to universities in Iran.
​
She added that women are responsible for 24 percent of registered inventions in the country, compared with a global average of around 14 to 17 percent.
​
According to the vice president, women run about 12 percent of Iran’s knowledge-based companies and make up nearly 48 percent of the government’s executive workforce.
​
Behrouz-Azar said the figures show that the status and position women have achieved over the years have been recognized and taken into account by the Pezeshkian administration.
​
She further referred to the legal foundations of the government’s policies on women and family affairs, saying the administration has placed various issues on its agenda and is seeking to formulate and implement its policies and programs based on the actual needs of women and families.
