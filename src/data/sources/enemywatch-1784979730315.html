🇮🇶: 🇮🇷 | Iraqi tribes call for mass participation in the funeral of the Martyred Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r):

Tribal leaders and prominent figures from across Iraq have issued a series of statements calling on their members to participate in the funeral ceremonies of Martyred Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r) in Najaf and Karbala.

Among those issuing invitations are Sheikh Munim Jabbar Al Sandouh, chief of the Al-A’ajib tribe in Samawah, who called upon his tribe and the wider Bani Hujaym confederation to attend the funeral, describing it as a demonstration of the loyalty and noble traditions of Iraq’s tribes.

The Arab Bani Lam tribe, the Al Omar tribe, and the Al-Sawa’id tribe have also released statements urging broad participation. In its appeal, the Al-Sawa’id tribe invited its members to gather in Najaf and Karbala, describing attendance as an expression of religious devotion and fidelity to the legacy of the Martyred Imam.

Sheikh Nazim al-Saeedi, head of the Executive Council of the Harakat al-Nujaba, stated that the large public turnout at the funeral would serve as “a resounding slap in the face” to those seeking to undermine or diminish the legacy of the Martyred Imam.
