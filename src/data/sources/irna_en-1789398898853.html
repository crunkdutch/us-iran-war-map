🔻 Projectile hits Iranian commercial vessel in Strait of Hormuz, killing one, injuring four

📌 Qeshm, IRNA – An Iranian commercial container ship was hit by an unidentified projectile early Sunday near Hengam Island in the Strait of Hormuz, killing one crew member and injuring four others, according to the governor of Iran’s Qeshm Island.


Hossein Amir-Teymouri said the vessel was operating in the Strait of Hormuz, near Hengam Island, when the incident occurred in the early hours of Sunday.
​
He said the ship was a commercial container carrier with 10 crew members aboard at the time of the incident.
​
According to the information released so far, one crew member, identified as Jamshid Rajabi, was killed after the projectile struck the vessel, while four other crew members sustained injuries.
​
Amir-Teymouri said the incident took place near Hengam Island within the Strait of Hormuz and that an investigation into the circumstances was underway.
​
He stressed that the projectile involved in the incident was unidentified and that further details regarding its origin, nature and the circumstances surrounding the strike had not yet been released.

🌍 Baqaei: Iran to pursue justice for victims of US attack on Lamerd

📌 Shiraz, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says the United States undoubtedly committed a war crime by using banned weapons in airstrikes on the southern city of Lamerd, emphasizing that Iran is making every effort to pursue justice for the victims of the attack.


Baqaei made the remarks on Saturday afternoon during a press conference in Lamerd, attended by journalists and reporters from Iranian and foreign media outlets.
​
The press conference was part of Baqaei’s daylong trip to Lamerd, a city in Fars Province that was struck on February 28, the first day of the second US-Israeli war of aggression against Iran in less than a year. Missile strikes hit several residential areas in Lamerd that afternoon, including a sports complex, leaving scores of casualties, among them children.
​
“Twenty-one people were martyred at four locations on February 28, 2026, and around 180 others were injured and are still struggling with health problems,” the spokesperson said.
​
“There is no doubt that the US crime constitutes a war crime. Investigations have shown that prohibited weapons were used, including cluster munitions, each containing 180,000 pellets. All the sites hit were residential and civilian areas,” he further said, adding that another investigation by Iran’s Health Ministry also revealed that chemical agents were used in the munitions to make them deadlier.
​
“Four missiles struck the 30,000-strong city of Lamerd within 35 seconds, leaving the city in mourning. We will seriously pursue justice and make every effort to shed light on this crime, so that the international community will join us in seeking justice for the martyrs of Lamerd,” said Baqaei, who was joined by two teenage boys injured in the US attack.
​
He also named Aveena, a two-year-old girl who lost her life in the attack.
​
“Aveena was a symbol of the innocence of the Iranian people in the face of aggression by the US and the Zionist regime. The pellets pierced her body so deeply that they tore her apart,” he said.
​
“The world must realize that if it remains indifferent towards a crime and injustice, this will not be the last kind of this crime,” he said when asked what message Iran was sending to the world regarding the crime in Lamerd.
​
“We, as humans, bear responsibility toward each other’s pain and suffering. The crime in Lamerd was not committed against a specific group of humans; it was against humanity,” he emphasized, noting that the US intentionally carried out the attack in Lamerd to test a new weapon of mass destruction.
​
Baqaei’s press conference also covered other key topics, including security in West Asia and the need for regional countries to cooperate to protect and promote that security amid warmongering by the Israeli regime and the United States.

​
Addressing the gathering, Baqaei praised the resilience of the people of Lamerd and paid tribute to the martyrs of the US-Israeli attacks there.
​
He also said that the people of Iran have no choice but to resist their enemy, expressing confidence that the nation will survive as all groups make efforts to protect national interests.

💬 Pezeshkian meets his South African counterpart in India
