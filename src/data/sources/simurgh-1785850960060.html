🇮🇷💬🇺🇸— The Iranian news agency Mehr, citing a source close to the Iranian negotiating team, revealed new details of the draft memorandum of understanding between Iran and the United States, which includes 14 points:

1. An immediate and permanent ceasefire in all arenas, including Lebanon.

2. A US commitment not to interfere in Iran's internal affairs and to respect the sovereignty of the Islamic Republic.

3. The complete lifting of the naval blockade within 30 days.

4. A US commitment to withdraw its forces from areas adjacent to Iran.

5. The reopening of the Strait of Hormuz within 30 days, according to Iranian arrangements.

6. The suspension of sanctions on the sale of oil, petrochemical products, and their derivatives, and granting Iran full access to its financial resources.

7. A commitment by the United States and its allies to submit plans for the reconstruction of Iran with a minimum value of $300 billion.

8. Sixty days of negotiations to draft a final agreement based on nuclear issues, the complete lifting of all major and minor US sanctions, and the cancellation of all resolutions and measures taken by the UN Security Council and the IAEA Board of Governors.

9. Iran's reaffirmation of its commitment, under the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT), not to develop nuclear weapons.

10. During the negotiation period, the United States commits to not increasing its forces in the region and not imposing new sanctions.

11. The release of $24 billion in frozen Iranian assets within 60 days of the final negotiations, with half of that amount to be provided to Iran before the start of talks.

12. The establishment of a monitoring mechanism to oversee the implementation of the agreement.

13. The final agreement will be endorsed by a UN Security Council resolution.

14. Final negotiations will not begin until half of the frozen assets are released, sanctions on Iranian oil are suspended, and the naval blockade is lifted.

According to the source, the final agreement will be limited to discussing the issue of enriched materials and nuclear enrichment, lifting sanctions, and a plan for the economic reconstruction of Iran, while the Iranian missile program and support for "resistance factions" have been completely excluded from the agenda.
