Updates starting from 11PM Tehran time

• CENTCOM says it has prevented 12 ships due to the blockade

🇰🇼 US military equipment warehouses at Camp Buehring, Kuwait
🇰🇼 A US troop deployment site at Camp Doha, in Kuwait
🇰🇼 A US forces’ deployment site at Camp Arifjan, in Kuwait.

🇰🇼 A very large ammunition depot at the US-operated Ali Al Salem Air Base, and through a series of explosions, completely destroyed it using advanced, super heavy OWA drones.
