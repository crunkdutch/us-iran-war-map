Statement by the Yemeni Armed Forces on targeting the Saudi oil tanker “Amzan” off the coast of Yanbu, and targeting mobilizations belonging to the Saudi enemy in the Al-Abr and Al-Wadiah areas and mobilizations in the Al-Kanā’is area – 24 August 2026

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful

Allah Almighty says: {So do not transgress except against the wrongdoers.} Allah Almighty is Truthful

With the help and guidance of Allah, the Yemeni Armed Forces successfully targeted the “Amzan” oil tanker belonging to the Saudi enemy in the northern Red Sea, off the coast of Yanbu, with a ballistic missile. The operation resulted in the burning and destruction of more than ten trucks loaded with weapons that were coming from Saudi territory to target the Yemeni people.

The second operation targeted mobilizations belonging to the Saudi enemy in the Al-Kanā’is area using a number of ballistic missiles and drones. It resulted in dozens being killed and wounded, including commanders and officers, and a number of weapons depots being burned and destroyed.

The Armed Forces, with the help of Allah Almighty, will continue to impose the ban on maritime navigation on the Saudi enemy within the declared area of operations, as part of implementing the “blockade for blockade” and “comprehensive escalation for comprehensive escalation” equations.

We are fully prepared, by the power of Allah, to confront any escalation with stronger and more severe strikes and broader and larger-scale operations.

As the Yemeni Armed Forces congratulate our great Yemeni people and our Arab and Islamic nation on the occasion of the blessed birth anniversary of the Prophet, may the choicest prayers and peace be upon him and his family, they call upon our dear steadfast people – the people of faith and wisdom, Yemen, the supporters – to turn out in a dignified manner to commemorate this great occasion in all designated squares tomorrow, God willing.

Allah is Sufficient for us, and He is the Best Disposer of affairs; the Best of Protectors and the Best of Helpers.

Long live Yemen, free, proud, and independent
