🇮🇷 | Meeting Between the Spokesperson of the Islamic Republic of Iran Army and the Foreign Ministry Spokesperson

Diplomacy and the Field, Side by Side for Iran

Brigadier General Mohammad Akraminia, spokesperson for the Army of the Islamic Republic of Iran, visited the Ministry of Foreign Affairs on Saturday evening and held talks with Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei.

The Army spokesperson emphasized the complementary roles of diplomacy and the military field in safeguarding national interests and security, stating:

“Today, diplomacy and the field are closer and more coordinated than ever in pursuing the country’s interests and national dignity. The results of this coordination and synergistic effort can be seen in the major achievements of the Iranian nation and in frustrating the enemies’ wars and conspiracies over the past year.”

The Foreign Ministry spokesperson honored the memory of those killed while defending the homeland in the Army of the Islamic Republic of Iran and the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. He also praised the continued and empowering presence of the people in public spaces and on the streets over the past 90 days, which helped thwart hostile actions by Iran’s adversaries.

He described the sacrifices and bravery of Iran’s armed forces in defending the country as a source of pride for every Iranian and stressed that the strength and power of the armed forces provide firm backing for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in protecting Iran’s national interests in the diplomatic arena.

Regarding the meeting, the Foreign Ministry spokesperson wrote on X:

Ready for any situation.

I fasten my belt if you seek peace.

Whatever you show, of warmth or coldness,

I accept, whether reconciliation or war.

Come then, with whatever you hold, sword or cup,

For I possess a complete hand in both.

📱 | Hanzala infiltrates Holocaust Victims Support Center; 2 million confidential documents allegedly leaked:

The hacking group “Hanzala” announced in a new statement that it carried out a cyber operation targeting the systems and databases of a Holocaust Victims Support Center, claiming to have gained access to a vast amount of information belonging to the organization. The group stated that all databases, documents, emails, and confidential correspondence of the center were extracted. According to its claim, more than two million documents, totaling over one terabyte of data, have been published on the group’s website. Hanzala further asserted that its review of the documents indicates links between the center and several Israeli defense industry companies, including alleged financial support from those firms for the center’s activities. Hanzala referred to Israeli security institutions, criticizing what it described as weaknesses in their cyber defenses and issuing warnings regarding the continuation of its future operations.
