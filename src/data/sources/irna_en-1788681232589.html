🖥 IRGC targets US aircraft carrier, destroyer in ballistic missile attack

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has announced that its Aerospace Force had targeted a US aircraft carrier and a destroyer with several ballistic missiles during an ongoing maritime confrontation.


In a statement early Sunday, the IRGC said the two American warships had been involved in disrupting Iranian shipping and participating in a naval blockade.
​
It added that both vessels suffered damage and subsequently withdrew from the area, fearing further attacks.
​
The statement said the United States had been forced to officially acknowledge that two of its naval vessels had come under attack. The IRGC described US Central Command’s reported confirmation of the operation as evidence of a strategic defeat for Washington and proof of Iran’s offensive capabilities.
​
The IRGC said Iran’s Armed Forces remained firmly determined to defend the country and warned that continued hostile or aggressive actions by the United States would trigger severe responses.
​
The IRGC also praised public support for Iran’s Armed Forces, saying national solidarity would prevent the adversary from achieving its objectives. It concluded by declaring that Iran remained stronger than before and would continue resisting external pressure again.

🌍 IRGC attacks US unmanned vessel seeking access to Strait of Hormuz

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Naval Forces of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) have attacked a US military unmanned surface vessel (USV) that was attempting to enter the protected area of the Strait of Hormuz.


“The heroic IRGC Navy has attacked a US military unmanned surface vessel (USV) that was attempting to enter the protected area of the Strait of Hormuz,” the IRGC said in a statement early Sunday.
​
The statement said the force is closely monitoring any enemy move and will confront it decisively.
​
The statement added that the Strait of Hormuz, a strategic waterway, is under the management of the Islamic Republic of Iran, warning that any move by the enemy would be met with a firm response.
​
“The aggressor United States should know that it has no choice but to leave the region and stop its mischief,” the IRGC emphasized.

💬 Statue honoring iconic Iranian artist Akbar Abdi unveiled

📌 Tehran, IRNA – A memorial ceremony marking the 40th day since the death of acclaimed Iranian actor Akbar Abdi was held in the presence of fellow artists, friends, colleagues and family members, culminating in the unveiling of a statue honoring him.


The ceremony, held Saturday evening, featured figures including Iraj Tahmasb, Hamid Jebeli, Farhad Aslani, Kianoush Ayari, Mohammadreza Sharifinia, Mahmoud Kalari, Alireza Khamseh and Salar Aghili. The program opened with Quranic recitation by Hamed Shakernejad and was hosted by Amir Yal Arjmand.
​
Actress Akram Mohammadi recalled Abdi’s distinctive humor and his enduring place in people’s memories, highlighting their collaboration in the acclaimed film “Mother,” produced about 37 years ago.
​
Director Behrouz Afkhami said Abdi should not be judged merely by his box-office record or roles, emphasizing his generosity and charitable nature.
​
Director Kianoush Ayari described Abdi as a figure who had become part of Iranians’ collective memory through his diverse performances and deep understanding of Iranian society.
​
Abdi’s wife said she still could not believe he was gone, recalling four decades of marriage and his kindness toward family and others.
​
Friend Shahram Zamani proposed establishing a comedy festival in Abdi’s name to discover and support emerging talents, with his statue presented to winners.
