⚡️ Al-Wefaq National Islamic Society in Bahrain:
Appeals court postpones case of two children facing one-year imprisonment
The two children, Nasrallah Shaker Al-Mawali (15 years old) and Qassem Mohammed Hussein Al-Mawali (14 years old), face one year in prison on charges of participating in a peaceful march, while the Court of Appeal postponed consideration of their case until July 28.
The courts had issued their verdict against the two children on May 21, after their arrest on charges of participating in an unlicensed march.
The courts have recently returned to issuing harsh sentences against citizens, especially children arrested on political grounds due to their participation in peaceful marches in the regions.
From time to time, protests arise from within juvenile and minor prisons about prisoners being subjected to violations and ill-treatment by prison guards, while families affirm their children's right to freedom and respect for children's rights in accordance with international conventions.
