📄 Iran rejects UAE claim of missile launch

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has categorically dismissed the United Arab Emirates’ claim that Iran launched a missile toward its territory.


Baqaei expressed regret over unfounded accusations against the Islamic Republic, saying such claims run counter to the principle of good-neighborliness and undermine ongoing efforts to strengthen trust among regional countries and prevent further insecurity.
​
He called on all regional parties to refrain from baseless allegations against Iran, citing the complex regional circumstances created by continued US and Israeli malign activities against regional peace and security.
​
The spokesperson also referred to the history of multiple alleged false-flag operations in the region.

🎙 Ghalibaf terms Iran-Iraq ties as historical, unbreakable

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has described the bond between the Iranian and Iraqi nations as “historical and unbreakable” ahead of his strategic visit to Iraq.


“Our neighborliness is based on brotherhood during the hardest days, resistance against foreign interference, and belief in the shared destiny of the two countries,” Ghalibaf wrote on X on Wednesday morning.
​
He said the shared past would guide the two countries’ future, adding that his visit would seek “practical and major steps” to strengthen shared, indigenous security and promote the welfare of both nations.
​
Ghalibaf left for Baghdad on Tuesday to meet with senior Iraqi officials and high-ranking figures of the country, with the aim of discussing bilateral relations, border security, the fight against terrorism, and the development of economic cooperation.

🌍 Iran, Indonesia discuss developing environmental cooperation

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Head of Iran’s Department of Environment Shina Ansari and Indonesia’s Minister of Environment Muhammad Jumhur Hidayat have reviewed the development of environmental cooperation between the two countries.


The meeting was held on the sidelines of the BRICS Environment Ministers’ Meeting in New Delhi.
​
Ansari stressed the shared environmental capacities of Iran and Indonesia, and the importance of strengthening bilateral cooperation, exchanging experiences and technical knowledge, and expanding expert-level collaboration.
​
She underlined protecting biodiversity, natural habitats, forests, water resources, marine ecosystems, climate change, and waste management.
​
Meanwhile, Hidayat emphasized the expansion of specialized and technical environmental interactions between the two countries.
​
He referred to Indonesia’s efforts in combating plastic pollution and underscored the importance of cooperation and consensus among countries on operational mechanisms for waste management, especially in coastal areas.
​
The two sides also stressed the continuation of consultations and the use of multilateral mechanisms, particularly BRICS, to expand environmental cooperation.
​
The 12th BRICS Environment Ministers Meeting was on Tuesday, August 18th, in New Delhi.
​
This meeting took place within the framework of India’s 2026 BRICS chairmanship under the theme: ‘Building for Resilience, Innovation, Collaboration, and Sustainability.

🎙 Iran’s security chief: Martyr Larijani was capable, qualified political and security manager

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council Major General Mohsen Rezaei has described the late Ali Larijani as a capable and qualified manager in political and security fields, praising his commitment to serving the Islamic Republic until the end of his life.


Rezaei made the remarks on Tuesday during a meeting with the family of Larijani at his home in Tehran, where he also paid tribute to their martyred son, Morteza.
​
Ali Larijani was the secretary of the Supreme National Security Council when he was assassinated in an Israeli airstrike near Tehran on the night of March 16 during the 40-day war of aggression launched by the regime and the US terrorist regimes against Iran on February 28. His son, Morteza, was killed in the same airstrike.
​
“Martyr Ali Larijani was not only a man of strong cultural outlook and insight, but also a capable and qualified manager in the political and security fields who remained steadfast in his efforts and service to the Islamic Republic until the final moments of his life,” Rezaei said.
​
He added that the Israeli regime had been angered by Larijani’s efforts to help resolve Iran’s diplomatic issues.
