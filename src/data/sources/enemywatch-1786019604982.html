— It seems the time to say goodbye to Telegram may be drawing very near. If it is removed from major platforms or subjected to widespread restrictions, we may have no choice but to shift elsewhere. There is also the possibility that our work could be disrupted for an extended period or, in the worst-case scenario, stopped permanently if no suitable alternative is found.

We are currently evaluating several alternative platforms and carefully analyzing whether they would be practical for our work and capable of sustaining the same level of communication, security, and reach. Moving to X presents its own challenges, particularly censorship, content moderation, account restrictions, algorithmic suppression, and the risk of suspension. Operating there in the same manner as on Telegram would be extremely difficult, and overcoming those limitations may not be possible.

Other options such as Signal, Viber, or similar platforms currently lack Telegram’s scale, public channel infrastructure, and active user base. While they may offer certain advantages, they do not provide the same accessibility or audience reach, making a transition highly challenging.

If these restrictions continue to expand across multiple platforms, independent channels and communities may face increasing pressure, reduced visibility, and serious operational difficulties. We are therefore studying every possible option in advance so that, if migration becomes unavoidable, we can continue our work with the least possible disruption. However, we must also acknowledge that there remains a possibility that some activities could be suspended temporarily or, if no viable solution exists, even permanently (sadly).

It is entirely possible that nothing will happen and Telegram will continue operating as usual. We are simply preparing in advance so that we are not caught off guard if circumstances change.
