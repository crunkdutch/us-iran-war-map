Local residents in the Bandar Abbas and Sirik areas reported hearing three explosions approximately two kilometers off the coast, prompting widespread speculation regarding a possible naval confrontation.

According to sources, the explosions were linked to an encounter between the IRGC Navy and a vessel accused of violating navigation restrictions in the Strait of Hormuz. Iranian officials stated that the vessel was an oil tanker that initially attempted to continue its passage but ultimately complied with IRGC Navy instructions after receiving warnings.

The IRGC Navy reportedly declared that any attempt to pass through the Strait of Hormuz in violation of announced restrictions would be considered a disruption of regional security and maritime order. Iranian military sources further emphasized that all vessels, regardless of nationality or type, would be dealt with firmly if they ignored navigation directives issued by Iranian authorities.

At the same time, officials in Hormozgan Province stated that no projectile impacts or direct clashes had been confirmed in Sirik, noting that the sounds heard from the sea were associated with ongoing activity in the Strait of Hormuz. This suggests that while an incident may have occurred, details regarding its exact nature remain unclear.

Iranian military sources also said that the Strait of Hormuz is under continuous surveillance and monitoring by the IRGC Navy, which has instructed captains and shipmasters to remain anchored until further notice and to avoid any actions that could escalate tensions in the waterway.

There were also reports that a U.S. Air Force F-35 Lightning II transmitted emergency code 7700 while operating over the UAE, indicating a general in-flight emergency, although no connection between the aircraft and events in the Strait of Hormuz has been established.

The most significant development remains Iran’s reported enforcement of navigation restrictions in the Strait of Hormuz and its warning that any vessel attempting to violate those restrictions will face a direct response from Iranian naval forces.
