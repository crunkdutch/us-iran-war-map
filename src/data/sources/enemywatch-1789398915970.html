🇾🇪 ❌ 🇸🇦 | Yemeni forces advance across western front as Khalid camp falls and Bab al-Mandab comes closer:

Over the past two days, Saudi-backed gangs, supported by the Saudi Air Force, launched operations aimed at disrupting control lines in several areas of Yemen, particularly on the Al-Jawf front around the Al-Labnat and Al-Itma bases. Battles took place around the Jabal Al-Rawadi mountains between the two bases, while official AnsarAllah media later released footage of the fighting, saying the Saudi-led coalition attacks had failed. Yemeni forces then advanced in Al-Wazi’iyah District and continued their advance through Mawza District, eventually taking control of Khalid bin Al-Walid Camp.

As of now, AnsarAllah reportedly established full control over Camp Khalid, a major strategic position overlooking the Taiz-Mokha road. Field sources subsequently reported significant and rapid advances by Sana’a forces, including the capture of the Al-Mokha junction, while Saudi-backed factions began retreating. Fierce battles continued around Jabal Al-Nar near Al-Mokha as Saudi-backed PLC forces brought in reinforcements in an attempt to recapture Khalid Camp. As Sana’a forces continued advancing toward Al-Mokha, Saudi-aligned factions reportedly began a large-scale withdrawal from the Hayisan and Al-Khawka districts in southern Al-Hudaydah.

Military sources said forces from the Al-Amaliqa faction, including Tariq forces and the Al-Tahamiya Resistance, began withdrawing amid concerns over disrupted supply lines and possible encirclement. The sources reported that Sana’a forces had cut all supply lines to the Saudi-backed factions, leaving them with only the coastal route. Khalid military camp had previously served as a major western-coast base for Tariq forces. Saudi aircraft reportedly then bombed retreating Saudi-aligned forces in Al-Mokha, causing dozens of casualties. With Yemeni forces continuing their advance in Al-Wazi’iyah toward Dhubab, they are now moving closer to the Bab al-Mandab Strait. AnsarAllah has also reportedly destroyed three additional oil tanks at the Jazan oil refinery in southern Saudi Arabia.
