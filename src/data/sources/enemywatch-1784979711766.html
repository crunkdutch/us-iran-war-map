🇾🇪 | Statement by the Yemeni Armed Forces:

In the Name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful

Allah the Almighty says:

“And whoever defends himself after having been wronged, for such there is no blame.”
(Qur’an 42:41)

This morning, Friday, at approximately 5:20 a.m., a formation of Saudi warplanes violated the airspace of several Yemeni provinces in an attempt to prevent an Iranian civilian aircraft carrying more than 200 stranded civilians, wounded, and sick passengers from landing at Sana’a International Airport, which, along with Yemen itself, has been subjected to what the statement describes as an unjust blockade for nearly 11 years.

According to the Yemeni Armed Forces, the attempt failed after Yemeni air defense units responded by launching several surface-to-air missiles, forcing the Saudi aircraft to leave Yemeni airspace.

The Yemeni Armed Forces therefore declare the following:

They warn Saudi Arabia against repeating any violation of Yemeni airspace or carrying out any aggression against Yemen, stating that any such action will be met with a comprehensive response targeting Saudi airports and vital interests on land and at sea.

They state that they will not accept the continuation of what they describe as the Saudi-American blockade on Yemen indefinitely and will take all legitimate measures to end it.

They call upon the Yemeni people to continue general mobilization and maintain combat readiness in response to the call of their leader, Abdul-Malik Houthi.

They affirm that all branches of the Yemeni Armed Forces remain fully prepared to implement any decisions issued by their leadership aimed at ending the Saudi-American blockade and expelling foreign forces.

In conclusion, the statement reiterates the demand to end the blockade and restrictions imposed on Sana’a International Airport. It also expresses appreciation for what it describes as the Islamic Republic of Iran’s initiative in helping to break the blockade by transporting patients, stranded civilians, and the official and public delegation participating in the funeral of Martyred Sayyid Ali Khamenei. The statement further declares that flights between Sana’a and Tehran will continue, by the will of Allah, as part of efforts to break the blockade and alleviate the suffering of the Yemeni people, regardless of the consequences.

The statement concludes:

Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs. Blessed is the Protector, and blessed is the Helper. Long live Yemen, free, dignified, and independent. Victory belongs to Yemen and to all the free people of the Ummah.
