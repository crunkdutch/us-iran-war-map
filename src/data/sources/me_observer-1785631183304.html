Press Release

A delegation from the leadership of the Hamas movement, headed by the mujahid brother Zaher Jabarin, head of the movement in the West Bank, met with brothers in the leadership of the Islamic Jihad movement, headed by the mujahid brother Ziyad al-Nakhala, the movement's Secretary-General, in the Egyptian capital, Cairo.

During the meeting, emphasis was placed on developing joint action and unified national stances, and exerting all efforts to protect the Palestinian people and restore their legitimate rights.

The attendees affirmed the necessity of obliging the Zionist occupation to stop all forms of aggression, killing, and assassinations, to fully withdraw from the Gaza Strip, to begin early recovery steps, and to allow the entry of all citizens' needs, calling for the swift entry of the national committee to manage Gaza and immediately assume its responsibilities towards the citizens.

The meeting also discussed the escalating situation in the occupied West Bank and Jerusalem, the crimes and accelerated Judaization operations carried out by the occupation, and the intensification of its crimes against prisoners, as well as ways to deal with these crimes in light of a complete international silence, which confirms that it is a green light for the continuation of these violations.

In the same context, a phone call was made during the meeting with the mujahid brother Khalil al-Hayya, head of the political bureau of the movement, and his brother, the mujahid Ziyad al-Nakhala, Secretary-General of the Islamic Jihad movement, emphasizing the necessity of supporting our Palestinian people and stopping the ongoing aggressive war against them.
