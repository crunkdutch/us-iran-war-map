📡 Iran Army chief: Recent war highlights vital role of air defense

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Chief Commander of the Islamic Republic of Iran Army Major General Amir Hatami has described air defense as a symbol of Iran’s defensive power and self-reliance, saying the experience of recent imposed wars has once again demonstrated its vital and strategic importance in securing the country and protecting its airspace.


In a message marking Air Defense Day and the anniversary of the establishment of the Khatam al-Anbiya Joint Air Defense Headquarters on Tuesday, Hatami congratulated Brigadier General Alireza Elhami, commander of the headquarters and the Army’s Air Defense Force, as well as its commanders, personnel, and families of fallen service members.
​
He said the establishment of the joint headquarters reflected the “valuable and farsighted initiative” of the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, to make integrated air defense a top priority in the country’s defense strategy.
​
He said the measure created coordination and unity among the air defense capabilities of Iran’s Armed Forces, providing a strong foundation for protecting the country’s airspace.
​
The Army chief highlighted the role of air defense personnel during the eight-year Iran-Iraq war, saying they demonstrated resistance and sacrifice on the front lines of the country’s defense.
​
He added that in subsequent years, Iranian specialists and young experts accelerated efforts toward self-sufficiency, indigenization, and upgrading air defense systems.
​
“The experience of recent imposed wars once again revealed the vital and strategic importance of air defense in ensuring security and protecting the country’s airspace,” Hatami said.
​
He described the use of advanced indigenous equipment and systems, alongside the knowledge, faith, and round-the-clock readiness of air defense personnel, as a symbol of Iran’s defensive power and self-reliance.
​
General Hatami also paid tribute to air defense personnel who were killed while defending Iran’s skies, saying they “stood by the systems and in the trenches of defending Iran’s airspace until their last moments” and sacrificed their lives for the nation’s security and dignity.

​
Over the past 35 years, despite ups and downs in relations, Iran and Tajikistan have always strived to develop bilateral ties, and today a strategic partnership has been formed between the two countries, he added.
​
One of the most important components of Iran-Tajikistan relations is the shared historical, linguistic, and cultural values that have long connected the peoples of the two countries, he stated.
​
The sustained and uninterrupted high-level political dialogue over the past 35 years has set Iran-Tajikistan relations apart, he reiterated.
​
Over the past 30 years, President Emomali Rahmon has made 22 official and working visits to Iran, and the Iranian presidents have also visited Tajikistan 16 times, Zahidi said.
​
The volume of bilateral trade in 2020 was about $55 million; previously it was higher, but for a period it declined due to some misunderstandings, the diplomat said.
​
However, in 2025, the trade volume between the two countries reached $450 million, which shows that over five years, bilateral trade has increased more than fivefold, he added.
​
He termed cultural cooperation as the fourth factor in developing bilateral relations and said the two countries share a common culture, language, and history.
​
Highlighting countering common threats as the fifth effective factor in Tehran-Dushanbe relations, he said at the top of the common threats are the fight against terrorism, organized crime, and drug trafficking, and the two countries have extensive cooperation in this field.
​
The combination of these factors has brought Iran-Tajikistan relations into a phase of strategic partnership, he emphasized.
​
He also referred to the existing mechanisms for developing bilateral cooperation, saying that so far, 18 sessions of the Joint Commission on Iran-Tajikistan Cooperation have been held in Tehran and Dushanbe, and the latest session of this commission was held last month in Dushanbe.
