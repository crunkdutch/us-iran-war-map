🇮🇷 | According to Mehr News Agency, sources have revealed the 14 clauses of the Memorandum of Understanding between Iran and the United States. On the financial side, sanctions on Iranian oil, petrochemical products, and derivatives will be suspended, with full access restored to Iran’s financial resources. Crucially, $24 billion in frozen Iranian funds will be released during the 60-day final negotiation period, with half, $12 billion, made available to Iran before negotiations even begin. The US and its allies are also required to present reconstruction plans for Iran amounting to at least $300 billion.

The agreement calls for a permanent and immediate cessation of war on all fronts, including Lebanon, a US commitment to non-interference in Iranian affairs and respect for Iran’s sovereignty, and the complete lifting of the naval blockade within 30 days. US forces are committed to withdrawing from around Iran, while the Strait of Hormuz will reopen within 30 days under Iranian arrangements. The agreement also includes a 60-day negotiation window to reach a final deal covering nuclear issues and the complete lifting of all sanctions. Iran reaffirms its commitment under the NPT not to produce nuclear weapons, while the US commits not to deploy additional forces to the region or impose new sanctions during the negotiation period. A supervisory mechanism will oversee implementation, and any final agreement will be be approved through a UN Security Council resolution.

Notably, Iran’s missile program and its support for resistance groups are definitively removed from the agenda. Final negotiations will not begin until the $12 billion is released, oil sanctions are suspended, and the naval blockade is lifted. According to Tasnim, an informed source confirmed that the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz will begin after the signing of the Memorandum of Understanding on Friday. Sources close to the negotiating team revealed that the first clause of the memorandum provides for an immediate and permanent end to the war and all military operations on all fronts, including Lebanon, along with respect for Lebanon’s territorial integrity.

According to Fars News, it has also been decided that the legal framework governing navigation in the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz will be arranged jointly by Iran and Oman. The report further states that following the Israeli strike on Dahieh in Beirut, Iran cancelled negotiations and was preparing to strike israel. However, last-minute concessions from Donald Trump, including guarantees of Lebanon’s territorial integrity, an Israeli withdrawal from the Lebanese border, and the immediate lifting of the US naval blockade, persuaded Iran to stand down.

Pakistan subsequently announced that a peace agreement between the United States and Iran had been reached following intensive talks, with both sides declaring an immediate and permanent end to military operations on all fronts, including Lebanon. The official signing ceremony is scheduled for Friday, June 19, in Switzerland. Pakistan expressed gratitude to Qatar for its mediation efforts and to Saudi Arabia and Turkey for their contributions to the agreement. Mediators will facilitate a series of pre-implementation meetings this week to lay the groundwork for technical talks ahead of the signing ceremony.

Pakistan also released further details of the Memorandum of Understanding, stating that sanctions on Iran will be lifted, between $10 billion and $14 billion of the approximately $28 billion in frozen Iranian assets will initially be released, a complete ceasefire will take effect on all fronts together with an Israeli withdrawal from Lebanon, Iran will retain its uranium and nuclear facilities, a $300 billion reconstruction and compensation fund will be established for Iran, the US blockade on Iran will be lifted, and Iran will manage the Strait of Hormuz while later receiving navigation fees.
