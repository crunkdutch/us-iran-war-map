🇮🇷: 🇮🇷 | Jamkaran Mosque in Qom and the Beginning of a New World Order:

Today, the body of the martyred Imam Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei and the bodies of his martyred family members arrived in the holy city of Qom. Following the funeral procession in Tehran, Qom is the second city to host the funeral ceremonies.

Qom is a city with a history spanning several thousand years, and throughout its history it has been home to many scholars, jurists, and intellectuals. A number of the members of the Prophet Muhammad’s (S) progeny are buried there. The city’s largest shrine is dedicated to Lady Fatimah Masumah (A), the daughter of the Seventh Imam and the sister of the Eighth Imam of the Shia Muslims. Around one hundred years ago, the Seminary of Qom was established beside the Shrine of Lady Masumah, later becoming one of the most influential centers of Islamic learning in the world.

Another of Qom’s most important pilgrimage sites is Jamkaran Mosque. Jamkaran was originally a small village located to the south of the city of Qom. More than one thousand years ago, during the early period of the Occultation of the Twelfth Imam of the Shia Muslims, Jamkaran Mosque was established. Shia Muslims believe that Imam Mahdi (A), during his Occultation, lives anonymously among the people. However, under certain circumstances, he meets with some of his sincere believers and true supporters. According to historical reports, in addition to issuing the command to establish the mosque, there have been several other occasions on which faithful believers met Imam Mahdi (A) at this very mosque. This has further enhanced the mosque’s sanctity and significance among Shia Muslims. They also believe that, when the divinely ordained conditions are fulfilled, the Twelfth Imam will reappear publicly and carry out his mission of establishing justice.

These days, millions of people across Iran are attending the funeral ceremonies of the martyred Imam Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei while carrying red flags bearing the slogan of vengeance, “Ya Li-Tharat al-Husayn.” These same red flags were previously raised following the martyrdom of General Qasem Soleimani. The red flag of vengeance has long flown above the dome of the shrine of Imam Husayn (A), who was martyred alongside members of his family in Karbala, and remains one of the enduring symbols of the call for justice.

Now, the funeral prayer for the martyred Imam Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei and his family is being held at Jamkaran Mosque, and the funeral procession will begin from this sacred site. It is a moment in which several profound symbols converge: the mosque associated with the awaited Imam Mahdi (A), the banner calling for justice for Imam Husayn (A), and the funeral of a leader whose life was devoted to the ideals of the Islamic Revolution and the Resistance. For many observers, these events together represent a historic turning point whose significance will continue to unfold in the years ahead.

🇮🇷: 🇮🇷 | Notice No. 3: Special arrangements announced at the Holy Shrine of Imam al Ridha (A) for the funeral of the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution:

The Administration of the Holy Shrine of Imam al Ridha (A) has announced comprehensive arrangements to receive and serve pilgrims attending the funeral and burial ceremonies of the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution and his martyred family members.

As part of these preparations, 30 service mawakib (hospitality stations), operated by Razavi volunteers, have been established along the main highways leading to Mashhad, particularly at the Imam al Ridha (A) roadside service complexes, where they are providing food, refreshments, and assistance to pilgrims.

Within the city of Mashhad and around the Holy Shrine, more than 400 mawakib have been set up through the combined efforts of the shrine administration, shrine servants, volunteer attendants, and community organizations to welcome and serve mourners.

Accommodation has also been arranged for tens of thousands of pilgrims, with lodging available at shrine guesthouses, Zaer Shahr Razavi, affiliated sports and youth institutions, as well as schools, universities, and other facilities associated with the shrine.

To support the large number of visitors, all medical facilities under the Holy Shrine administration, including Razavi Super Specialty Hospital and Dar al Shifa Imam (A), have been placed on full alert. A total of 120 fully equipped medical teams, comprising approximately 2,500 healthcare personnel, will provide medical services throughout the ceremonies.

1. The pure body of the martyred Leader will arrive in Najaf tonight

The officials organizing the funeral in Iraq announced that the pure body of the martyred Leader will arrive tonight through Najaf International Airport. Two separate ceremonies have been arranged in his honor.

The first will be an official reception and funeral attended by senior Iraqi government officials, military commanders, and the Popular Mobilization Forces (Hashd al-Shaabi). This will be followed by a massive public procession from the shrine of Imam Ali (A) in Najaf to the shrines of Imam Husayn (A) and Hazrat Abbas (A) in Karbala, covering approximately six kilometers.

With pilgrims already traveling from across Iraq and abroad, hundreds of mawkibs have entered full readiness, while extensive transportation, parking, and logistical arrangements have been made for the ceremonies.

2. The official funeral will begin in Najaf

Mr. Saad Maan, spokesperson for the organizing committee, stated that the official funeral of the martyr Imam Khamenei will first be held in Najaf and that all necessary security measures have been completed.

Coordination meetings have taken place in Baghdad, Najaf, and Karbala. The Iraqi Army, Iraqi Police, and Popular Mobilization Forces will jointly secure the ceremonies.

A total of 351 registered convoys have been prepared in Najaf, alongside family convoys and Hussainiyahs, while more than 400 convoys have also been organized in Karbala.

3. Official request by more than 100 Iraqi members of Parliament

More than 100 members of the Iraqi Council of Representatives officially requested Ayatollah Mujtaba Khamenei to permit the funeral of his father, the martyred Ayatollah Ali Khamenei (r), to be held on Iraqi soil.

The published request also included a pledge by the MPs to renew their covenant, continue the path of the martyred Imam, and remain committed to the Resistance.

4. Governor of Maysan calls for public participation

The Governor of Maysan called on the people of the province to participate in the funeral of His Eminence the Martyr Sayyid Ali al-Husayni Khamenei (r).

Residents are asked to gather on Tuesday at 3:00 PM in front of the Governorate building, with departure scheduled for 5:00 PM. More than 120 buses have been allocated to transport participants free of charge to Najaf and Karbala and return them after the ceremonies.

5. Najaf declares an official holiday

Najaf Governorate announced Wednesday as an official public holiday to mark the funeral ceremony of Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r).

The holiday is intended to facilitate broad public participation in the funeral ceremonies taking place in the holy city.

6. “The martyr of Iran will visit the Holy Shrines after 69 years”

Karbala Governorate welcomed the funeral of the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution by describing him as “the first Arbaeen pilgrim of this year.”

The governorate issued the welcoming slogan: “Welcome to the first Arbaeen pilgrim of this year,” in anticipation of the funeral procession.

7. Invitation by Sayyid Ammar al-Hakim

Sayyid Ammar al-Hakim, leader of the Iraqi National Wisdom Movement, called on the Iraqi people to participate in large numbers in the funeral of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei.

He said that widespread participation would symbolize solidarity, loyalty, and the deep bond between the Iranian and Iraqi peoples, urging all Iraqis to attend the ceremony.

8. Kirkuk declares an official holiday

Kirkuk Governorate announced Wednesday as an official holiday for all government institutions and offices.

The decision was made to allow residents to participate in the funeral ceremony of Martyr Ayatollah Khamenei, described as the martyred Leader of the Ummah.
