🎙 President Pezeshkian congratulates Muslims on Prophet Muhammad’s birth anniversary

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated the leaders and people of Muslim countries on the anniversary of the birth of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), emphasizing the great Prophet’s message of peace, security and unity for humanity.


In his message on Saturday, President Pezeshkian described the Prophet’s noble conduct as the highest model of human perfection and a guiding light for humanity as it navigates the challenges of an increasingly complex world.
​
We are proud to belong to the community of a Prophet who brought a message of peace and security to all humanity; a Prophet whose religion is rooted in peace and whose title is a mercy to all the worlds, President Pezeshkian said, adding that the Prophet is the Messenger who ennobled creation, while his noble conduct stands as the highest model of human perfection and the finest embodiment of virtue and noble character, serving as a guiding light on humanity’s challenging journey through the world.
​
The president stressed the importance of further strengthening unity and solidarity among Muslims, expressing hope that the occasion would provide an opportunity to draw inspiration from the Prophet’s life and teachings.
​
He called for Muslims to help bring security, justice and reconciliation to the wider world, particularly to Muslim countries, through adherence to the Prophet’s values.
​
President Pezeshkian also expressed hope that the shared faith, determination and resolve of Muslims worldwide would lead to stronger unity and solidarity within the Islamic community and contribute to the continued growth and advancement of Islam across the globe.

🖥 Gharibabadi: Implementation of Hormuz understanding with Oman hinges on US’s fulfilling its commitments

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi says implementation of the understanding Iran has reached with Oman concerning arrangements for transit through the Strait of Hormuz hinges on the United States’ fulfillment of its commitments towards the Islamic Republic that Washington has suspended.


Gharibabadi made the remarks on Saturday, commenting on a temporary arrangement reached between Tehran and Muscat that had been announced by the official, himself, earlier this week.
​
“The Islamic Republic has reached an understanding with Oman concerning arrangements for transit through the Strait of Hormuz. However, this understanding will not automatically enter the implementation phase, as its implementation is conditional upon the fulfillment of commitments by the other party, specifically the United States,” he said.
​
Gharibabadi rejected the idea that Iran should initiate the process of fulfilling commitments linked to reopening the waterway.
​
“It is not for us to take the first step in fulfilling the commitments related to reopening the Strait of Hormuz. Rather, it was the United States that suspended its commitments. Washington must return to fulfilling those commitments, and we have made entirely clear the steps that must be taken by the United States,” he stated.
​
“We have explicitly stated that whenever the conditions are met and the commitments incumbent upon the United States are fulfilled, the Islamic Republic is likewise prepared to take its own measures regarding the Strait of Hormuz.”

🔊 Minab school massacre leaves an eternal wound on Iran: FM Araghchi

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has described the Minab school massacre as an eternal wound on Iran, urging people and contemporary art enthusiasts to visit an exhibition featuring paintings inspired by the incident.


In an Instagram post on Saturday night, Araghchi said he had visited the exhibition the previous day, describing it as a place where art weeps and makes others weep.
​
He said the paintings convey the suffering and innocence of the children of Minab, with colors speaking in place of words and canvases expressing what cannot be put into language.
​
“Behind these colors and images stands the bitter memory of a crime committed by enemies of Iran against innocent children of this land,” Araghchi said, stressing that the tragedy would neither disappear with time nor be erased from the memory of the grieving nation.
​
The foreign minister said artists had appropriately used their work to preserve the memory of the victims and ensure that their suffering would not be forgotten.
​
The Shajareh Tayyabeh primary school was targeted in a missile attack on February 28, the first day of the second US-Israeli war of aggression on Iran in a year. Nearly 170 people, including students, teachers and parents were killed in the attack.
​
Foreign Minister Araghchi invited Iranians and those interested in contemporary Iranian art to visit the exhibition.

🌍 Muslims in Russia celebrate birth anniversary of Prophet Mohammad

📌 Moscow, IRNA – Sunni and Shia Muslims across Russia have commemorated the birth anniversary of the Prophet Mohammad (PBUH), a key Islamic occasion marking unity among Muslim nations.


Ceremonies were held at mosques in the capital, Moscow, and other Russian cities on Saturday evening to mark the occasion.
​
Addressing one of the ceremonies at Moscow Islamic Center, the Grand Mufti of Russia’s Sunni Muslims, Sheikh Kefah Mohammad, said that Muslims should follow the Holy Quran and the teachings of the Prophet Mohammad (PBUH) in order to neutralize the conspiracies of the arrogant US and the criminal Zionists.
​
Islamic countries should not fall into the traps of arrogance and Zionism, he said, while calling on Islamic governments to not allow their lands to be used against other Muslim nations.

💬 Iranian officials congratulate U-17 boys’ national volleyball team on world championship

📌 Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian and Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, in separate messages, have congratulated Iran’s Under-17 Boys’ National Volleyball Team on winning the world championship.


President Pezeshkian expressed congratulations to the country’s national youth volleyball team, as well as its players, coaches, technical staff, their families, and the Iranian sports community, on their inspiring victory at the World Championships in Qatar.
​
“This brilliant victory is a manifestation of the talent, willpower, self-belief, and capabilities of the Iranian generation, who, whenever we trust them and provide them with opportunities to showcase their abilities, can achieve glory at the highest global level,” he said on Sunday.
​
Speaker Ghalibaf also expressed his congratulations, saying: “The glorious championship of Iran’s Under-17 Boys’ National Volleyball Team at the world championships and its decisive victory over France in the final once again raised the name of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the pinnacle of world volleyball and hoisted Iran’s flag over the Persian Gulf.”
​
“Iranian youth have proven that whenever knowledge and technical skills are combined with national pride, determination, and a champion’s spirit, no great arena is beyond their reach,” he added.
​
Ghalibaf appreciated the efforts of all those who contributed to the achievement and wished the Iranian volleyball players a bright and glorious future.
​
IRGC chief expresses congratulations
​
In a separate message, the Commander-in-Chief of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps, Major General Ahmad Vahidi, congratulated Iran’s national youth volleyball team on winning the world championship/
​
He said that the players’ determination and relentless efforts, even amid the enemy’s intense psychological warfare, had once again brought the spirit of “We Can” to life.
