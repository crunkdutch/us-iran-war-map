🌍 Gharibabadi stresses foreign interference violates sovereignty

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi says that foreign interference in the destiny of nations is an assault on their sovereign rights.


On the anniversary of the US-led coup in Iran on August 19, 1953, during which democratically elected Prime Minister Mohammad Mossadeq was toppled, Gharibabadi wrote on X that the event marked direct intervention by the US and the UK in the will of the Iranian people and their right to determine their own future, stressing that such interference is a form of colonialism and a violation of national sovereignty.
​
This chapter of history can neither be whitewashed nor forgotten, he added.
​
Gharibabadi emphasized that although generations may pass, one truth remains unchanged — any country that hands its destiny over to foreigners will pay a heavy price.
​
He noted that today’s Iran determines its own future, with the nation refusing to retreat under pressure and standing firmer in the face of compulsion and threats.

🔊 Ukraine must compensate for attack on Iranian ship: Envoy

📌 Moscow, IRNA — Iran’s ambassador to Russia has called on Ukraine to compensate for all damages caused by its drone attack on the Iranian merchant ship Anna in the Caspian Sea, which killed one crew member and injured several others.


Kazem Jalali told Russia’s RIA Novosti news agency on Wednesday that the attack was a “terrorist and criminal act” targeting a civilian vessel carrying commercial cargo under the Iranian flag.
​
The attack was a deliberate attack on a civilian ship carrying commercial cargo under the flag of Iran, he said.
​
Jalali said Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has discussed the incident in phone calls with his Russian counterpart Sergey Lavrov and the EU foreign policy chief, while Ukraine’s foreign minister apologized and provided explanations during a call with Araghchi.
​
He added that European countries and the US, as supporters of Ukraine, should also accept responsibility and hold Kyiv accountable for its actions.
​
The ambassador said Ukrainian officials have accepted responsibility for the attack and pledged that similar attacks on Iranian vessels would not happen again.
​
The privately owned Anna was struck by a Ukrainian drone while carrying rolled sheet iron in the Caspian Sea on July 11.

🔻 Bushehr Chamber, Iran-Vietnam Joint Chamber sign MoU to expand trade ties

📌 Bushehr, IRNA – The head of the Bushehr Chamber of Commerce, Industries, Mines and Agriculture says that a memorandum of understanding (MoU) was signed between the Bushehr Chamber of Commerce and the Iran-Vietnam Joint Chamber of Commerce, aimed at expanding trade, export and investment ties between economic operators on both sides.


In an interview with IRNA on Wednesday, Khorshid Gazderazi said that an MoU was signed between Bushehr and Vietnam to effectively utilize the resources of both sides and create new opportunities for enhancing trade, exports, and investment relations among economic operators.
​
He further noted that, as part of the MoU, a “Vietnam Desk” will be established at the Bushehr Chamber of Commerce to facilitate economic connections by providing market information, trade regulations, and tariffs, as well as identifying potential business partners and opportunities for exports and investments.
​
Gazderazi said that areas of cooperation will include coordinating the dispatch and reception of trade and exhibition delegations, organizing specialized meetings and training courses, conducting market studies and feasibility assessments, and showcasing Bushehr’s economic capacities.
​
Referring to the province’s capacities in production, exports, fisheries, agriculture and food industries, he expressed hope that the cooperation would lead to increased trade exchanges and a stronger presence of Bushehr’s products in the Vietnamese market.

🎙 Strait of Hormuz will remain under Iranian control: Leader’s adviser

📌 Tehran, IRNA – A senior adviser to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution says the Strait of Hormuz has been and will always remain Iranian, rejecting remarks by US President Donald Trump regarding the strategic waterway.


In an interview with Al Mayadeen, Mohammad Mokhber said Trump was “fantasizing” about the Strait of Hormuz and stressed that Iran is the sole party that will continue to determine how the waterway is managed.
​
“We have a law in this regard that exists in the Islamic Consultative Assembly, and there is also a decision that the Leader makes regarding the Strait of Hormuz,” Mokhber said.
​
He added that the Strait of Hormuz will be managed solely according to arrangements established by the Islamic Republic of Iran.
​
Mokhber dismissed Trump’s remarks as “illogical, ineffective and futile,” saying they would have no impact.
​
“We are fully prepared; we do not want war, but we are always prepared to defend the country,” he said.
