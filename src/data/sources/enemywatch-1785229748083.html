🇾🇪 | Al Masirah TV has released a special report highlighting the dramatic impact of the 12-year blockade and military campaign on Sana’a International Airport, comparing its normal operations before the conflict with its activity afterward.

According to the report, Sana’a International Airport has operated at only 2 to 3 percent of its normal capacity during the past 12 years. Before the conflict and blockade, the airport handled around 2.5 million passengers annually, compared with only about 50,000 passengers per year after the conflict, including during the de-escalation period. Annual flight operations also fell sharply, from more than 50,400 flights per year before the conflict to just 1,782 flights afterward. Prior to the conflict, the airport operated commercial flights to more than 20 destinations through over 15 airlines, whereas after the blockade it has been limited to a single destination served by only one airline.
