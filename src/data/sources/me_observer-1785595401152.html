The representative of the Hamas movement in Tehran, Khaled Al-Qaddoumi, affirmed that the blood of the martyrs, especially the leader Ismail Haniyeh, represents a source for strengthening the steadfastness and continuity of the resistance front.
This came during a massive event organized in the Iranian capital, Tehran, to commemorate the second anniversary of the martyrdom of the head of the political bureau of the Hamas movement, Ismail Haniyeh.
Al-Qaddoumi said, during his speech, that Haniyeh, with his faith, sincerity, and jihad, embodied an eternal model for the Palestinian people and for all factions and forces of the resistance, and proved that martyrdom is not the end of the road, but rather the beginning of the continuation of the path of glory, freedom, and dignity.
He stressed that the assassination of leaders, as the martyr Haniyeh always affirmed, will not bring down the banner of resistance, and that this path will continue until victory is achieved.
He referred to the last meeting he had with the martyr Haniyeh in Tehran, recalling his faithful spirit and his constant readiness for martyrdom, adding that "Haniyeh viewed martyrdom as the wish of every mujahid in the path of God, until he finally attained what he desired."
Al-Qaddoumi reiterated the importance of the unity of the Islamic nation, strengthening coordination between resistance forces, and the necessity of continuing to support the Palestinian people.
