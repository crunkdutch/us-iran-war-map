⚡️ In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful

Statement from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs regarding the continued heroic defense of the Iranian entity by Iranians in the face of illegal American threats and attacks.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, with utmost appreciation and reverence, expresses its gratitude for the exceptional efforts made by the valiant armed forces in the honorable defense of the Iranian entity, and salutes the souls of the leader of the martyrs and all martyrs on the path of Iran's independence and glory, affirming the determination of the Islamic Republic of Iran to continue the path of resistance and steadfastness until the complete elimination of the enemies' evils.

In light of the continued American enemy's breach of its obligations stipulated in the memorandum of understanding for ending the war of the twenty-eighth of Khordad, and its continued imposition of a naval blockade on Iranian ports and commercial ships, and launching brutal attacks on various regions of the country, and intensifying economic pressures and illegal threats, and committing aggressive acts against Iran, the defensive strikes carried out by the brave armed forces in the country will continue with full force.

The continuation of the naval blockade, which, according to United Nations General Assembly Resolution No. 3314 issued in December 1974, is a clear example of an act of aggression, along with brutal attacks targeting military and non-military targets, civilian infrastructure, and Iranian citizens inside and outside the country, including in Iraq, constitutes a flagrant violation of paragraph two of Article Two of the United Nations Charter. Therefore, the Islamic Republic of Iran will use all available means to exercise its legitimate right to self-defense in the face of these aggressions.

The American aggressive actions against Iran during recent months have been characterized by all three descriptions contained in the United Nations Charter, namely: a threat to international peace and security, a breach of international peace and security, and an act of aggression. Under Article 39 of the Charter, the Security Council is obligated to intervene to stop and respond to these actions. However, the Council, as happened during the eight-year war imposed by Saddam's regime on Iran, has not yet taken any effective action to fulfill its legal responsibility in the face of American and Israeli aggressions. Unfortunately, the Secretary-General of the United Nations has only issued general and vague statements, without taking any effective step in this regard.
