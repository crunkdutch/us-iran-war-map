🇱🇧❌🇮🇱 NEW — Hezbollah:

Narrative of the targeting of the commander of the Israeli enemy’s 401st Armored Brigade, Colonel Meir Biderman, on the outskirts of the town of Dibl, southern Lebanon, on 20-05-2026.

After a series of attempted advances toward the town of Haddatha from several directions, the enemy failed to breach the resistance’s defenses at the town’s outskirts, where it suffered many losses in both soldiers and vehicles.

On the evening of 19/05/2026, the enemy launched a fierce attack on Haddatha from two axes, deploying a large number of vehicles and 34 soldiers (a force from the 601st Combat Engineering Battalion). Intense clashes took place on the town’s outskirts. At dawn, the advancing force retreated, leaving behind two destroyed bulldozers and four tanks that burned after being hit by drones and direct shells. As for the soldiers, 28 were seen withdrawing, while the remaining six were evacuated by a Namer vehicle after being wounded, among them the company commander, Captain M., and the operational documentation fighter, First Sergeant Sh., who sustained moderate and severe injuries.
This advance was directly overseen by the commander of the 401st Brigade, who was monitoring the progress and subsequent retreat of the forces from the brigade’s forward position in the town of Rshaf.

At 05:00 a.m., Brigadier General Meir Biderman returned to the newly established brigade operations headquarters on the outskirts of Dibl after the complete withdrawal of the force. All of this was under the watchful eye and surveillance of the resistance.

At 07:50 a.m., the first Ababil attack drone arrived at the headquarters, conducted a field reconnaissance of the site, and circled around it. When it detected enemy officers positioned behind camouflage on the top floor, and after they fled inside, the drone followed them into the headquarters, striking them and directly hitting the operations room. As a result of this strike, brigade commander Meir Biderman was wounded in the head, along with two other officers.

At 08:10, a medical team evacuated the wounded using a Hummer vehicle to the helicopter landing pad. The Hummer reached the pad at 08:23, where a helicopter transported two of the wounded, while the third was taken by ambulance.

At 08:45, the team returned to the headquarters on the outskirts of Dibl, where another drone was waiting and targeted the headquarters again. Another drone struck an ammunition truck, causing explosions and a fire near the headquarters.

At 09:00, as the force was preparing to leave the building, another Ababil drone targeted a Namer vehicle at the entrance to the headquarters.
