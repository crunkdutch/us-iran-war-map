🇮🇷/🏴| How can a pious reciter and lover of the Prophet and his family be killed, with his heart reportedly taken out while alive? Is this simply a crime, or does it reveal something far more disturbing? (1/3)

Six suspects have been arrested in connection with the martyrdom of Iranian religious reciter Hamidreza Rajabzadeh.

Hamidreza Rajabzadeh was a religious reciter who performed at the Hussayniyah of Imam Khomeini of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei. He was a simple religious man who focused on ethics, religious guidance, and the welfare of others.

A few days ago, a video circulated widely on anti-Islamic Republic channels, reportedly showing the perpetrators brutally attacking and martyring Rajabzadeh, desecrating his body, taking his heart out and subsequently setting it on fire.

According to the police investigation, Rajabzadeh had been communicating with a woman on social media and had advised her regarding hijab and religious conduct. Investigators say that the woman subsequently lured him to a secluded location after arranging an in-person meeting. According to the investigation, she first incapacitated him with poisoned food, after which several men allegedly attacked and martyred him.

Investigators further stated that the group had planned to record the incident and send the footage to the Mojahedin-e Khalq Organization (MEK), also known as the People’s Mojahedin Organization of Iran (PMOI), in exchange for money reportedly amounting to several thousand dollars.

Following the arrest of the main suspects in the Hamidreza Rajabzadeh case, investigators from the Criminal Investigation Department are continuing their inquiries to determine the motive and circumstances surrounding the incident.

According to the Police Spokesperson’s Office, the female suspect in the case, who was reportedly attempting to flee the country, was arrested in one of the northern cities and transferred to a police facility.

The Police Spokesperson’s Office stated that during the initial interrogations, the suspects confessed to their involvement in the killing, while further investigations into the various dimensions of the case remain underway.

Police also announced that, through the efforts of technical and specialist teams from the Tehran Police Criminal Investigation Department, the main suspect in the case was identified and arrested.

According to the Law Enforcement Command of the Islamic Republic of Iran, four other suspects had also been arrested during the preceding 48 hours, bringing the total number of arrested suspects to five at that stage of the investigation. Police later announced the arrest of the female suspect who had been attempting to flee, bringing the number of arrested suspects to six.

The head of the Tehran Criminal Prosecutor’s Office also announced that the body of Hamidreza Rajabzadeh had been discovered in the vicinity of Tehran and transferred to the Forensic Medicine Organization.

According to the report, Rajabzadeh had been missing for approximately 15 days. Several days ago, a video showing his severely injured body was published on an anti-government channel and subsequently circulated widely across social media.

His disappearance had previously been reported, with reports stating that the well-known religious reciter had not returned home after leaving his residence. After a case was opened at the Tehran Criminal Prosecutor’s Office, the investigation quickly entered a new phase.

Investigators from the Fourth Department of the Tehran Police Criminal Investigation Department, acting under orders from the criminal prosecutor, began investigating the circumstances surrounding the missing man and eventually identified a young female blogger and several young men as persons of interest.

No information concerning his whereabouts was initially obtained.

Contact with the female blogger on social media:

As the investigation continued, detectives determined that Rajabzadeh had met a young female blogger on social media some time earlier.

According to the investigation, he had repeatedly advised her regarding hijab and encouraged her to observe it on her Instagram page. He had also reportedly advised her not to discuss political issues about which she lacked sufficient knowledge.

On the day he disappeared, the woman reportedly requested an in-person meeting with him. Rajabzadeh, who had reportedly been advising her regarding hijab for approximately six months, agreed to meet her in person.

This lead redirected the investigation toward the young woman.

In another branch of the investigation, detectives identified the license plate number of a vehicle that had reportedly fled the area shortly after the meeting between the female blogger and Rajabzadeh. No further trace of Rajabzadeh was found in that area.

Police subsequently tracked the vehicle and identified a woman and several men connected to the case.

Following covert surveillance, the suspects were arrested in a series of operations.

During police interrogations, the suspects reportedly confessed to their involvement in Rajabzadeh’s martyrdom. According to the investigation, they stated that after the incident they transported his body to the city of Parand and set it on fire in the surrounding desert.

Investigators further stated that the suspects had recorded footage of the incident and the subsequent desecration of the deceased’s body and had sent the footage in exchange for money to the Mojahedin-e Khalq network. According to the investigation, they believed they could receive substantial payment for footage depicting the martyrdom of a Basij member.

Investigators determined that Rajabzadeh had sustained multiple stab wounds and that his body was subsequently set on fire after his martyrdom.

The suspects also reportedly provided investigators with information concerning the transportation of the body and the location where it was burned in the desert areas around Parand.

According to the investigation, the young woman had allegedly planned the meeting in advance and invited Rajabzadeh to a secluded location. After he arrived, she allegedly offered him poisoned food, causing him to lose consciousness. She then allegedly acted in coordination with several men, who attacked and martyred him.

Confession of the female blogger

During interrogation, the female blogger reportedly stated:

“I met the victim on social media. He repeatedly advised me about hijab and wanted me not to talk about political issues and to lead a proper life. I discussed this with my boyfriend, who suggested that I lure the young eulogist to a secluded area under some pretext so that he and his friends could kill him. He said that the Mojahedin-e Khalq groups paid money for the killing of Basij members. That is why, after the victim was killed, he filmed it so that he could sell the footage to them.”

The main suspect in the case remains at large

According to statements attributed to the arrested suspects, one individual identified as the main perpetrator allegedly inflicted the primary stab wounds, after which the other individuals also participated in the crime. However, the main suspect reportedly fled following the incident, and police efforts to locate and arrest him are continuing.

By order of the criminal prosecutor, the arrested suspects were placed in temporary detention on charges of intentional homicide and transferred to the custody of the Criminal Police. Investigations into the full circumstances of the case remain ongoing.

Forensic identification personnel and Criminal Investigation Department detectives are also expected to travel to the desert areas around Parand to search for and recover the victim’s remains.
