🖥 Pakistan PM invites Pezeshkian to visit Islamabad

📌 Islamabad, IRNA - Pakistan’s Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has formally invited Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian to pay an official visit to Islamabad at the earliest possible opportunity, while President Pezeshkian extended a reciprocal invitation for Sharif to attend the upcoming ECO summit in Tehran.


The invitations were extended during a bilateral meeting on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) Heads of State Council summit in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, according to a statement released by the Pakistani Prime Minister’s Office.
​
During the warm and cordial meeting, the two leaders underscored their deep and longstanding ties and called for further strengthening bilateral cooperation in all fields, in line with agreements made during Pezeshkian’s recent visit to Islamabad.
​
They also reviewed ongoing efforts aimed at expanding regional connectivity and people-to-people ties between the two neighboring countries.
​
In their exchange on the regional situation, Sharif emphasized the necessity of restraint and diplomatic engagement to achieve lasting peace in the region.
​
He reiterated Pakistan’s commitment to sincere efforts for regional peace, stating that the recent visit of Field Marshal Asim Munir to Tehran and his constructive engagement are a promising development.
​
The statement also quoted the Iranian president as expressing appreciation for Pakistan’s diplomatic efforts toward regional peace and security.

Baqaei wrote on his X account early Tuesday, “The #EU appears to be surrendering its sovereignty, its laws & regulations, its values and its ethics to American coercion. The spokesperson said, “The Union’s endorsement of America’s unlawful economic war constitutes a grave violation of international law, too. Every EU member state shall be held accountable for the consequences of its contribution to this wrongful act. The European Union cannot endorse Washington’s #economic_terrorism against Iran while claiming to seek de-escalation and regional stability. The two positions are mutually exclusive. This irresponsible stance is evidence of the EU’s moral decay and political incompetence.”

💬 EU compromises with its autonomy, reduces itself to executor of US orders: Baqaei

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson, Esmaeil Baqaei, in a message to the European Union, has deplored the 27-nation bloc for blindly following the dictate of the United States, by renouncing its own independence and proclaimed values.


In a post on his X network on Tuesday, Baqaei responded to the European Union's statement in support of the US sanctions regime and economic war against Iran, saying the bloc has reduced its role to that of “an executor of American orders.”
​
He explained the EU move, citing Discours de la servitude volontaire (Discourse on Voluntary Servitude), a 16th-century treatise of French philosopher Étienne de La Boétie, which he said “identifies a fundamental truth about the enablers of tyranny.”
​
“Domination is not sustained by force alone; it often endures through the acquiescence of those who are capable of acting independently,” he wrote, adding, “Europe can no longer speak of ‘strategic autonomy’ while merely executing Washington’s orders.”
​
“Strategic autonomy is not secured by grandiose declarations. It is realized through the capacity to decide independently and responsibly — even if those decisions diverge from the position of a greater power.”
​
Baqaei reminded Europe in his post that it had a choice between behaving “like an independent actor” or growing “so accustomed to echoing another’s policies that it mistakes that echo for autonomy.”
