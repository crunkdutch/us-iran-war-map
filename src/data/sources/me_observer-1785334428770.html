This approach reflects Abu Dhabi's effort to reduce security risks without abandoning its strategic partnerships.
⏺In the security sphere, the UAE seeks to strengthen air defense systems, develop intelligence cooperation, and increase deterrence capabilities alongside the US and Israel. ⏺This policy shows that Abu Dhabi, instead of choosing between Tehran and the US-Israel axis, seeks to create a balance between diplomatic engagement with Iran and maintaining the Western security umbrella.
