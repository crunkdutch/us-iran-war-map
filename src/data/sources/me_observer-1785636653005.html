⚡️ New Karakhana Information: Ambassador Simon Karam will head the Lebanese delegation to Rome, with the participation of Ambassador Nada Hamadeh and an expanded military delegation, alongside experts in surveying and economics.
⚡️ Scene from Al Jadeed:
An American delegation conducted a tour in Zawtar al-Gharbiyah, during which it was briefed on the security situation and the progress of military missions carried out by the army within the adopted coordination mechanism to monitor its work in the South.
-The Lebanese political delegation will not present specific demands, as the most important issues to be raised in the upcoming round will be brought by the military delegation.
-During the Rome round, the Lebanese delegation will present the tasks accomplished by the army in the South and North of the Litani, supported by maps documenting the field reality in the region, in addition to humanitarian and social missions.
-The Lebanese military delegation will present during the negotiations the file of Israeli violations and the bombings carried out by Israel in the southern villages, linking the pace of implementing some missions and progress in the plan to the extent of these field violations.
-Surveying experts and economic experts will discuss two separate files: surveying experts will discuss the land borders and outstanding points to complete their demarcation, while economic experts will handle the reconstruction and investment files in South Lebanon exclusively with the American side.
Diplomatic sources to Al Jadeed:
-The American delegation participating in the negotiations will be expanded to include, in addition to diplomats, experts in surveying and economics.
-The agenda of the Rome negotiations, extending over 3 days, will include a series of intensive meetings to discuss various political, security, military, and economic files.
