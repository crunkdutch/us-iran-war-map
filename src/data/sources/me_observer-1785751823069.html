🚫Suweihan Air Base, United Arab Emirates
🚫In recent hours, significant movement of C-17 transport aircraft has been observed at Suweihan Air Base in the United Arab Emirates.
🚫Since approximately 18:00 Zulu (UTC) last night (July 31), five C-17s with call signs MOOSE 54 to MOOSE 58 have arrived at Suweihan Air Base and departed shortly thereafter.
