📄 Iraqi communications minister visits Iran as two sides seek broader ICT cooperation

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran and Iraq are moving to expand their cooperation in information and communications technology (ICT), shifting from mainly operational coordination to a broader and more sustainable framework covering infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital economy and emerging technologies.


The latest step in the growing cooperation between the two countries is taken during the current visit of Iraq’s Communications Minister Mustafa Jabar Sanad who arrived in Tehran on Monday at the invitation of his Iranian counterpart Sattar Hashemi.
​
Sanad’s visit to Tehran is aimed at furthering discussions on communications and technology cooperation, and also participating in the 29th International Exhibition of Electronics, Computer and E-Commerce (ELECOMP), one of Iran’s major technology events.
​
A specialized Iran-Iraq forum is also scheduled on the sidelines of the exhibition to explore joint opportunities in ICT and facilitate direct engagement among technology companies, operators and digital-economy players from both countries.
​
In mid-July, Hashemi also paid a visit to Baghdad, where he and his Iraqi counterpart discussed improving telecommunications infrastructure, roaming services and connectivity along the Iran-Iraq border, particularly during the annual Arbaeen pilgrimage. They also agreed to provide free internet access for pilgrims along the Najaf-Karbala route.
​
Beyond Arbaeen-related services, the talks covered Iran’s experience in cybersecurity, digital economy, e-health, space technology, and telecommunications research. Tehran has also expressed readiness to share its expertise in network resilience, cybersecurity and fiber-optic infrastructure with Iraq.

🌍 India says PM Modi, Iran’s president held ‘fruitful’ talks on sidelines of SCO summit

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Indian Foreign Ministry has described as “fruitful” the latest meeting between the country’s Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian, saying they discussed various aspects of bilateral relations.


Pezeshkian and Modi held a bilateral meeting on Monday on the sidelines of the annual Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan.
​
“PM Narendra Modi had a fruitful meeting with President Masoud Pezeshkian of Iran on the sidelines of the SCO Summit in Bishkek. The two leaders discussed various aspects of bilateral ties,” Randhir Jaiswal, spokesperson of the Indian Foreign Ministry, said on his X account.
​
According to the spokesperson, the Iranian president and the Indian prime minister “reviewed recent developments in the West Asia region.”
​
“The Prime Minister reiterated India’s consistent position that all issues must be resolved through dialogue and diplomacy. Prime Minister also underlined the need for continued efforts to ensure peace and stability in the region, and for safeguarding freedom of navigation and commerce,” the Indian diplomat said.
​
President Pezeshkian and his accompanying delegation, including Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, arrived in the Kyrgyz capital earlier on Monday. In addition to attending the SCO summit, the president is scheduled to hold further bilateral meetings with participating heads of state on the sidelines of the gathering.

📡 PM Modi says India is ready to ‘expand and diversify’ trade relations with Iran

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi, emphasizing his country’s long-standing relations with Iran, has announced New Delhi’s readiness to expand and diversify trade ties with Tehran.


Modi made the remarks in a post on his X network, following a meeting with Iran’s President Masoud Pezeshkian, on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, on Monday.
​
Referring to his talks, Modi said he reaffirmed to Pezeshkian India’s “commitment to strengthening India’s long-standing friendship with Iran across diverse sectors”, especially diversifying the trade basket in the times to come.
​
According to the social media post, the Indian premier and the Iranian president also discussed the current situation in the region, with Modi ensuring “India will continue to support all efforts aimed at ensuring lasting peace.”
​
Indian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal also described the Modi–Pezeshkian meeting as productive and fruitful, saying the two leaders reviewed various aspects of bilateral relations and the latest developments in the region.
​
Jaiswal added that Modi, in his meeting with Pezeshkian, reiterated New Delhi’s position on the need to resolve all issues through dialogue and diplomacy.

🎙 Iran, Mongolia foreign ministers meet on sidelines of SCO Summit in Bishkek

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and his Mongolian counterpart Battsetseg Batmunkh met in the Kyrgyz capital of Bishkek on Monday on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) Summit.


During the meeting, the two top diplomats discussed recent developments in the region and the issues of mutual interest.
​
The 26th Summit of heads of state of the SCO kicked off in Bishkek on August 31 and will run through September 1 as Kyrgyzstan is the organization’s periodic chair in 2026.
​
This year’s summit slogan is “25 Years of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization: Together for Peace, Development, and Sustainable Prosperity.”
​
President Masoud Pezeshkian arrived in Bishkek on Monday to attend the Shanghai Cooperation Organization and Shanghai Plus Summit, where he is due to deliver a speech and hold bilateral meetings.
​
Upon his arrival, Pezeshkian was welcomed by Kyrgyzstan’s deputy cabinet chairman. His two-day visit will focus on the summit of heads of state from the Shanghai Cooperation Organization and Shanghai Plus, where he will speak and hold bilateral talks with a number of participating leaders and the presidents of Kyrgyzstan, Russia and Tajikistan on the sidelines of the summit.
​
At the invitation of the Kyrgyz president, the Iranian president will also attend the official opening ceremony of the sixth World Nomad Games during the trip.
