⚡️ Iran's anti-radar armaments:
1. Kh-58 anti-radar missile
This missile has a range of about 120 kilometers and a speed of 3.6 Mach.
Its guidance is inertial guidance to the middle of the path, then guidance by passive radar seeker.
Iran purchased about 50 units of this missile from Russia, and currently this missile is in the possession of the Army Air Force, and its launch platform is the Su-24 fighter jet, although it also has the capability to be installed on the IRGC's Su-22 fighter jets.
2. Hormuz anti-radar ballistic missile:
This missile is the only anti-radar ballistic missile in the world, with a range of 300 kilometers and a speed of about 5 Mach. The guidance of this missile is inertial in the initial phase and by passive radar seeker in the secondary phase.
3. Shahed 238 anti-radiation drone: In addition to other versions of this suicide drone, an anti-radiation version has also been developed that locks onto enemy radars and communication systems near the target using a passive radar seeker and destroys them.
