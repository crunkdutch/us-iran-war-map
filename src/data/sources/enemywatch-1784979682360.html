🇮🇶: 🇮🇷 | #IraqForImamKhamenei

• Popular Mobilization Forces (PMF) have deployed around 30,000 fighters to provide security for the funeral ceremonies of the martyred leader, Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei, in Najaf and Karbala.

• Commanders in the Euphrates (Furat al Awsat) region confirmed that PMF units will remain deployed until the conclusion of all funeral and farewell processions in both cities.

• Najaf International Airport has been temporarily closed following the issuance of a NOTAM by the Iraqi Civil Aviation Authority.

• All commercial flights scheduled for Najaf have been diverted to Baghdad International Airport.

• A total of 193 buses have been stationed at Imam Khomeini Airport City to provide transportation and services for pilgrims attending the funeral ceremonies.

• Nine Iraqi provinces have declared official holidays for the historic funeral procession:

- Najaf
- Karbala
- Baghdad
- Basra
- Dhi Qar
- Wasit
- Maysan
- Babil
- Al Muthanna

• Numerous Iraqi tribal and clan leaders have called upon the Iraqi people to participate in the funeral procession in honor of the martyred Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei.

• The funeral and farewell ceremonies for Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei are scheduled to take place in Najaf and Karbala on 8 July.
