🇾🇪 | Yemen battlefield update: Marib offensive, western coast advances, and strikes inside Saudi Arabia:

Marib front

Heavy fighting is underway across the Marib front as AnsarAllah launched limited offensive operations against Saudi proxy PLC positions around the city. Fighting is reported north, west, and northwest of Marib, including around Al-Uqdah, Atram Hill, Al-Falaj checkpoint, and the Harib axis. Reports stated that AnsarAllah fighters reached the gates and entrances of Marib city after advancing through the northern front, with heavy clashes and mutual artillery shelling reportedly continuing for around an hour on the northern outskirts before the fighting temporarily calmed. The current situation remains fluid, and it is unclear whether AnsarAllah has consolidated control over the defensive positions directly north of the city. Saudi proxy PLC trucks were reportedly seen outside Marib as forces evacuated the city, while part of the Saudi proxy PLC force was also reportedly withdrawn from Marib toward eastern Hadramawt. On the western side of Marib, AnsarAllah continued limited attacks while pushing eastward along both highways toward the western outskirts. AnsarAllah has not yet entered Marib from the western direction, and fighting there remains limited.

Northern Marib and the Al-Libnat front

AnsarAllah’s main effort has focused on the northern defensive belt around Marib. Reports stated that AnsarAllah advanced through desert trenches and fortifications and captured positions around the Labinah area, the An Nuddar Mountains, and Labinah Camp before advancing farther south. The Saudi proxy PLC had previously captured both Upper and Lower Al-Libnat, using their trenches and fortifications as part of the defensive line toward Jabal Dhana. AnsarAllah is now attempting to break through this defensive network and advance toward the city. Reports that Upper Al-Libnat, Lower Al-Libnat, and Kanayis Military Camp had fallen to AnsarAllah were later described as completely false, while the claim that AnsarAllah had entered Kanayis was specifically rejected. A separate local report claimed that Saudi proxy PLC forces had captured Mas Military Camp from AnsarAllah, but this remains unconfirmed.

Al-Uqdah, Atram Hill, and Al-Falaj

Heavy fighting continues around the Al-Uqdah defenses northwest of Marib. Clashes between the Yemeni Armed Forces and Saudi proxy PLC mercenaries were reported east of Atram Hill in the Al-Uqdah area, while heavy clashes involving heavy weapons were also reported on the Marib fronts as AnsarAllah fighters advanced toward the Al-Falaj checkpoint.

Harib front

The battle for the major town of Harib has begun. Harib is primarily defended by Saudi proxy PLC-aligned Shabwah Emergency Forces, and AnsarAllah reportedly carried out a limited incursion to probe the defenses before a wider operation.

Marib tribal participation

Reports state that Marib tribes are fighting alongside AnsarAllah in an effort to liberate the remaining parts of the governorate from Saudi proxy PLC forces and mercenaries.

Territorial gains around Marib

The current offensive has reportedly produced approximately 1,209 square kilometers of territorial gains in favor of AnsarAllah. The reported gains include positions north of Marib and advances along the western and northwestern approaches. However, Marib city itself remains under Saudi proxy PLC control, and several surrounding positions remain contested or unconfirmed.

Western coast offensive

AnsarAllah continued its major offensive along Yemen’s western coast, with reports describing rapid advances toward the Red Sea and Bab al-Mandab. Reports stated that AnsarAllah secured Hisn Murad and took control of large sections of Yemen’s western coastline from the Saudi proxy PLC within two days, while AnsarAllah forces also reached the coastal town of Dhubab on the Bab al-Mandab Strait. The offensive has also involved operations around Mokha, Ras al-Arah, Al-Wazi’iyah, Al-Barh, and other positions connecting the Taiz highlands to the coast.

Jabal Jardad

The Yemeni Armed Forces took control of Jabal Jardad in Taiz, overlooking Ras al-Arah, Al-Wazi’iyah, Al-Barh, and the coastal strip near Bab al-Mandab. The position gives AnsarAllah a strategic location connecting the Taiz highlands with the western coast and overlooking important coastal routes. Reports also described small AnsarAllah advances south of Taiz around Jabal Jardad.

Hifaan to Al-Turbah axis

Yemeni forces are continuing to advance eastward from the Hifaan front and westward from Jabal Jardad toward Al-Turbah. The reported objective is to cut the main supply route connecting Taiz with Aden, creating additional pressure on Saudi proxy PLC forces operating in southern Taiz and along the western approaches.

Taiz and Lahij fronts

Heavy fighting continues across Taiz and Lahij. Southern Giants mercenaries reportedly clashed with AnsarAllah fighters on the borders of Al-Mudaraba district in Lahij Governorate, while Saudi aggression also targeted the Al-Houban area in Taiz Governorate. Artillery shelling was reported between Beit Leif and Yater. The western Taiz battlefield remains connected to the wider coastal offensive, with the capture of Jabal Jardad strengthening AnsarAllah’s position between the highlands and the Bab al-Mandab coastline.

Al-Jawf and Al-Bayda fronts

Clashes continue on the Al-Jawf and Al-Bayda fronts alongside the Marib offensive. Ballistic missile shelling by AnsarAllah targeted Saudi proxy PLC force positions in Al-Jawf amid continuing field escalation. The Saudi proxy PLC is also attempting to capture Jabal Dhana in the Al-Hazm direction of Al-Jawf, with the position depending heavily on the trench and fortification network around the Al-Libnat base.

Saudi airstrikes against Yemen

Saudi aircraft continued striking Yemeni positions as AnsarAllah advanced across multiple fronts. Saudi airstrikes reportedly targeted Mokha Airport twice and later renewed attacks against Mokha Port, while Saudi aggression also targeted the Al-Houban area in Taiz Governorate. Additional Saudi airstrikes were reported against areas of Marib, Al-Jawf, Taiz, and Hodeidah as the Saudi proxy PLC attempted to contain the AnsarAllah offensive.

AnsarAllah attacks inside Saudi Arabia

AnsarAllah launched missile and drone attacks against targets inside Saudi Arabia as the escalation expanded beyond Yemen. Alerts were reported in Khamis Mushait and Abha, reportedly following the launch of AnsarAllah missiles and drones. Explosions were reported again in Khamis Mushait in southern Saudi Arabia. Reports also described missile attacks targeting King Khalid Air Base in Khamis Mushait and King Fahd Air Base in Taif. Ballistic missile strikes were reported against Taif, with explosions shaking the city, while additional explosions were reported in Sharurah Governorate in southern Saudi Arabia.

Reports stated that missiles targeted Sharurah Air Base, which is used for military cargo operations, including American military transport aircraft. Saudi Civil Defense issued an alert through the National Early Warning Platform in Sharurah Governorate, warning residents of potential danger. Additional alerts were reported in Sharurah Governorate in Najran Province.
