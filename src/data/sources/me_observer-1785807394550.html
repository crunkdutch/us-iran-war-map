⚡️ In the name of God, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate
Press News
Brother Mujahid Dr. Khalil Al-Hayya, head of the Political Bureau of the Hamas movement, received a phone call from His Excellency President Masoud Bazargan, President of the Islamic Republic of Iran, where they discussed the latest developments related to the Palestinian issue and the current situation in the West Bank and the Gaza Strip.
At the beginning of the call, His Excellency the President commemorated the martyr Commander Ismail Haniyeh, on the occasion of the anniversary of his assassination, may God have mercy on him.
He stressed the Islamic Republic's stance in support of the Palestinian people, their rights, and their just cause, and its continued support and backing, saying: "We are with you and support you in all the measures you take, despite the aggression we are facing."
He also congratulated the head of the Political Bureau on gaining the trust of his brothers and his election to lead the movement, expressing his pride in the continuous and direct communication.
He also stressed the importance of harmony and coordination between all components of the nation and its unity, especially with regard to the Palestinian issue.
For his part, the head of the movement expressed his appreciation for the President's call and the Iranian stance in supporting the Palestinian people and their fixed rights.
He reviewed the latest political developments in the file of negotiations, and the overall field and political situation in the West Bank, the Gaza Strip, and Jerusalem.
He stressed the Palestinian people's adherence to their full rights and the use of all legitimate means to achieve them.
He commemorated the martyrs who have risen since the start of the Al-Aqsa flood in various fields, on the road to liberating Palestine and Al-Aqsa.
He expressed his appreciation and gratitude for the Iranian positions and their constant support for the Palestinian people, expressing his hope and aspiration for an end to the aggression against the Islamic Republic and the return of security and stability to the entire region.
The head of the Hamas movement stressed the importance of the nation's unity, considering it a fundamental way to its strength and the restoration of our people's rights.
