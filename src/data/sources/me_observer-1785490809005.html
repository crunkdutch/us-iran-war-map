⚡️ ⏺A significant change in the IRGC's missile architecture.
🚫Images released from recent operations show an interesting sign of the IRGC moving towards sharing a common booster among several different warheads/payloads; it appears that the Haj Qasem family booster has been used as a propulsion platform to carry different warheads.
⏺This approach is entirely logical from an engineering perspective: when a solid-fuel booster, its engine, structure, separation system, and aerodynamic architecture have matured, there is no necessary need to design a complete missile from scratch for each warhead. The common booster can be maintained, and the upper section can be changed according to the mission.
The IRGC is moving from "one missile for one mission" towards a modular architecture; a common solid-fuel booster can carry different systems by changing the upper section.
⏺The advantages of this architecture are clear: reduced development time, reduced production cost, sharing of production lines and components, simplified logistics, and most importantly, increased speed of mass production.
