⚡️ Foreign Minister's Strong Reaction to British Approach: Condemnation of London's Action Against Armed Forces and Complicity Against Iran
🔹Ed Miliband, the British Foreign Secretary, spoke with Seyyed Abbas Aragchi, the Foreign Minister of the Islamic Republic of Iran, this Friday evening in a phone call, discussing regional and international developments and bilateral relations.
🔹The Foreign Minister of our country deemed Britain's inappropriate approach regarding Iran, including its recent resolution labeling the armed forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran, as well as Britain's complicity in the two imposed wars by the United States and the Zionist regime against Iran, unjustified and condemned it, emphasizing the necessity for the British government to reconsider its stance in this regard.
🔹The Foreign Minister of our country, recalling the military aggression of the United States and the Zionist regime against Iran as the main cause of the current situation in the region, stated: The Islamic Republic of Iran entered the diplomatic process in good faith and brought the understanding of ending the war to fruition on June 18, but again the United States, by breaching its commitment, prevented its implementation.
🔹The Foreign Minister emphasized Iran's determination to establish operational mechanisms in cooperation with Oman for managing shipping traffic in the Strait of Hormuz and stated that the obstruction by third parties in this process will only complicate the existing situation.
🔹The Foreign Minister of our country also considered any cooperation with aggressors, including under the pretext of previous defense agreements, unacceptable and noted that according to international law and the resolution defining aggression, allowing aggressive parties to use the territory and facilities of one country for an illegal attack on another country is considered an act of aggression and authorizes the attacked country to resort to legitimate defense.
🔹The British Foreign Secretary, explaining the approach of his country's new government, stated: We are not part of the war with Iran and believe in diplomacy for resolving issues. He emphasized the necessity of continuing diplomatic interactions with Iran to improve relations and reduce tension in the region.
