📣 | 20 hours Updates:

Lebanon

Israeli air activity and shelling continued across southern Lebanon. An Israeli warplane reportedly targeted the outskirts of Wadi al-Hujair, while another airstrike was reported on Marjayoun and an Israeli airstrike was reported in Deir Siryan. Israeli artillery shelling was reported in Wadi al-Hujair toward al-Qantara, the Ali al-Taher heights, the outskirts of Zawtar al-Sharqiya, Mushaa al-Mansouri, Kafr Tibnit, between Zebqine and Yater, and between Sorbine and Rashaf. Explosions were reported in Zawtar al-Sharqiya, Majdal Zoun, al-Qantara, Bani Hayyan and Hadatha. Machine-gun fire was reported around Zawtar al-Sharqiya, Hadatha, Sorbine, the outskirts of Ayta al-Jabal and Beit Yahoun, with enemy vehicles also reportedly moving inside Beit Yahoun during a combing operation. House-burning was reported in Khiam and Hadatha, while bulldozing of houses and roads was reported in Rab Tlathine. Sound bombs were reported around Kafra and Rashaf, and explosive materials were reportedly used in the Harash areas of Beit Yahoun and Ayta al-Jabal toward Beit Yahoun. An Israeli drone was also reported over Arnon.

Yemen

Fighting and military activity intensified across Yemen. The Yemeni Armed Forces said they would continue operations targeting Saudi mobilizations that they said were seeking to escalate the conflict and control Yemen. Brigadier General Yahya Sare’e announced that Yemeni forces had targeted Saudi concentrations, weapons depots and command centers near Al-Mokha and the Tadawin camp in Marib using ballistic missiles and drones, claiming the strikes caused dozens of casualties and injuries, including Saudis. Fierce clashes were also reported between Yemeni forces and Saudi-backed forces on the outskirts of Shabwah. Earlier, Ansarullah drones were reported over Aden, while a second attack in three days was reported against the port city of Mocha. Ansarullah also reportedly targeted a Saudi-managed commercial vessel in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, with UKMTO confirming the attack and reporting the deaths of two Pakistani and one Indonesian crew members. Saudi Arabia reportedly carried out two drone strikes against Ansarullah positions in Wadi al-Mashawwat in Sa’da’s Ghumar district. Large numbers of Ansarullah drones were also reported across eastern Yemen, including over Seiyun, Wadi Dawan, the al-Jawf desert, Shabwah, Marib and Bayhan al-Qisab, while evacuation orders were reportedly issued to residents in western Ta’izz.

Gaza / Palestine

Israeli military activity continued across the Gaza Strip. Artillery shelling was reported alongside flare bombs over the northwestern skies of Beit Lahia, while Israeli artillery targeted the eastern al-Zaytoun neighborhood of Gaza City and areas east of the al-Bureij refugee camp in central Gaza. Israeli Channel 14, citing a high-level diplomatic source, reported disagreement over authorization to open fire on individuals crossing the yellow line, with the source saying that withholding permission contradicted political-level instructions. Channel 14 also quoted Israel’s foreign minister as saying that the Israeli army was under significant pressure, with Israeli soldiers deployed in Gaza, Lebanon and Syria.

Syrian occupation

Syria’s Transitional Justice Commission told Al Hadath that Syria was continuing on the path of demanding the handover of former President Bashar al-Assad. The statement comes amid the ongoing transitional justice process and efforts by the Syrian authorities to pursue accountability for figures from the former government.

To make it easier for them to recognize the East, Westerners divided the "East" into three parts : the Near East, the Middle East and the Far East. These foreign terms were imposed on our minds and unfortunately, through the generation we have accepted it — maybe because we feel it is not that important. The truth is, it is this important to our societies. It is important to not feel the East of something, to not feel an inferiority in front of a foreign civilization. So how can we decolonize our minds ?

A better term would be "West Asia", for our region is located in the Asian Continent as we all know. Moreover geographically, we are at the edge of it, close to Africa and Europe. By using the term "West Asia", we reaffirm our true position on the map and remove this inferiority's mindset to truly express what we are. Arabs, Assyrians, Kurds, Persians, Turks and all other ethnicities of the "Middle East" are as Asian as Chineses, Koreans, Japaneses.

It is a hard work on our own mind to change such habits to use the term "Middle East", sometimes it may feel ridiculous to debate on such matters but in the end, we can all agree that it is quiet the opposite. Through education, we may reclaim the societies we are. And perhaps, we can finally extract ourselves from this position of second-zone region in the world.
