⚡️ 🔺Secretary of the Supervisory Council on IRIB: IRNA news was an inaccurate interpretation by the reporter/No official warning or promise of correction was raised at the Supervisory Council meeting
🔹The Secretary of the Supervisory Council on the Islamic Republic of Iran Broadcasting (IRIB) emphasized that no official warning or promise of correction was raised at the recent meeting of this council with the organization's political deputy, stating: The news published in this regard was an inaccurate interpretation by the reporter, and I have also warned him about it.
🔹Mohammad Mirzaei, Secretary of the Supervisory Council on IRIB, in an exclusive interview with an IRIB News Agency reporter, regarding the news published quoting him in one of the media outlets about the Supervisory Council's meeting with the IRIB's political deputy, stated: The recent meeting was held in a completely constructive, interactive, logical, and expert atmosphere, and questions that had been previously sent to the organization's political deputy were raised, and the necessary and convincing answers were provided.
🔹He stated that he was personally convinced by the answers provided, adding: At that same meeting, I explicitly announced that Mr. Abedini's explanations were convincing to me, and the atmosphere of the meeting was one of interaction, cooperation, and expertise, not confrontation.
🔹Mirzaei, referring to the news published about "the Supervisory Council's warning to IRIB and the organization officials' promise of correction," clarified: Such an interpretation was not my intention. The reporter asked me if he should write that the Supervisory Council had issued a warning and IRIB had promised to correct it, to which I explicitly told him not to write it that way and to reflect the matter in a way that would not lead to misunderstanding.
🔹The Secretary of the Supervisory Council on IRIB emphasized: No resolution or official warning has been issued by the Supervisory Council in this regard, and what was discussed at the meeting were expert discussions and exchanges of views between the council members and the organization's officials.
🔹He continued: The term "warning" in the context of expert discussions may mean raising a point or a view, but to say that the Supervisory Council officially issued a warning and IRIB promised to correct it is not an accurate interpretation of the meeting's discussions.
🔹Mirzaei, referring to the relations between the Supervisory Council and IRIB, said: Today, perhaps more than ever, there is an effective and constructive interaction between the Supervisory Council and IRIB. If a point is raised in some cases, it is examined in a friendly and interactive atmosphere, and so far there has been no particular point of contention between the two entities.
🔹He also praised the performance of the national media, noting: At that same meeting, I also thanked IRIB for its performance, especially in covering the developments and the 12-day imposed war. The fact that the national media's broadcast was not interrupted for even a moment despite enemy attacks showed the organization's readiness and proper planning.
🔹The Secretary of the Supervisory Council on IRIB stated that after the news was published, he contacted the relevant reporter, saying: I also emphasized to him that this part of the news did not match what I had said, and he even promised that the text of the news would be corrected, but later announced that the news had been published.
🔹Mirzaei finally emphasized: My position is that the recent meeting was a constructive and dialogue-based meeting, and the news published about "the official warning of the Supervisory Council" and "the promise of correction by IRIB" does not reflect my position.
