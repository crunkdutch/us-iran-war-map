🇾🇪 ❌ 🇸🇦 🇦🇪 | Current Update:

AnsarAllah forces have confirmed the capture of Jabal al-Umari, the final strategic high ground overlooking the Bab el-Mandeb Strait on the Mocha axis in southwest Yemen. The position lies just east of Dhubab and south of the 17th Brigade Military Camp, placing one of the most important elevated positions overlooking the strait under AnsarAllah’s control.

Following the fall of Jabal al-Umari and Jabal Dhubab, AnsarAllah forces are preparing to enter the coastal city of Dhubab as military operations continue along the Bab el-Mandeb front.

AnsarAllah forces have confirmed the capture of the major islands in the southern Red Sea, including Abu Ali, Hanish al-Kabir, Hanish as-Saghir, Suyul Hanish, al-Mamalih, and Jabal Zuqar. PLC sources also confirm ongoing clashes at sea as AnsarAllah’s naval forces attempt a landing operation on Mayun Island in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait.

Mayun Island is one of the most strategically important islands in the Bab el-Mandeb chokepoint. Before the collapse of PLC positions, the island reportedly hosted a secret Emirati air base and had allegedly provided Israeli access in the past.

Former Southern Transitional Council (STC) formations are preventing Saudi proxy-PLC fighters withdrawing from the Mocha front from entering southern governorates, including Aden and Lahij. STC forces are demanding that withdrawing PLC units fully disarm before being allowed entry.

STC forces are also mobilizing around Aden to prevent the city from falling to AnsarAllah as the military situation rapidly develops across southern Yemen.

Three full brigades of Saudi-backed forces have surrendered to the Yemeni Armed Forces, according to AnsarAllah officials, along with their light and heavy weapons and military equipment across the fronts of Hays, Al-Mokha, Al-Khokha, Taiz, and Al-Jawf.

Thousands of Saudi-backed fighters in the strategic port city of Al-Mukha have been captured following the collapse of PLC positions and the advance of Yemeni Armed Forces (AnsarAllah) units along the western coast.

Al-Mukha is one of the most strategically important cities on Yemen’s western coast. Located about 70 km north of the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, it overlooks one of the world’s busiest maritime chokepoints connecting the Gulf of Aden, the Red Sea, and the Suez Canal. Control of Al-Mukha and Dhubab significantly alters the balance of power along the Bab el-Mandeb Strait and the security of regional and international shipping routes.

Sources confirm continued AnsarAllah advances across Dhubab District as the offensive progresses toward the Bab el-Mandeb coastline.
