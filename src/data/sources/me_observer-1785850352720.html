⚡️ 🔺Free food basket credit charged for 262,000 children in need of supplementary nutrition
Deputy Minister of Welfare and Economic Affairs at the Ministry of Cooperatives, Labor, and Social Welfare:
🔹 As of today, the free food basket for over 262,000 children aged 5 to 59 months in need of supplementary nutrition has been charged.
🔹 The credit for purchasing food baskets for malnourished children, with a 50% increase, was deposited into the accounts of household heads via the electronic coupon system.
🔹 The amount of the Ministry of Welfare's support basket is 2 million tomans for needy households and 1.2 million tomans for households with average means.
🔹 Families with children suspected of malnutrition should visit health centers for height and weight monitoring and registration in the system.

We renew our pledge that the rule of law is the only guarantee that this tragedy will not be repeated, and that Lebanon, which has known resilience in the darkest circumstances, is capable of emerging from its ordeal stronger, once justice is fully achieved.
