🇮🇷🇺🇸🇮🇱🇱🇧 New details about a 14-article draft memorandum of understanding between Iran and the United States:

1- A permanent and immediate cessation of war on all fronts, including Lebanon.

2- The United States commits to non-interference in Iran’s internal affairs and to respecting the sovereignty of the Islamic Republic of Iran.

3- The complete lifting of the naval blockade within 30 days.

4- The US commits to withdrawing its forces from the vicinity of Iran.

5- The reopening of the Strait of Hormuz within 30 days according to Iranian arrangements.

6- Suspension of sanctions on the sale of oil, petrochemical products, and their derivatives, and granting Iran full access to its financial revenues.

7- The US and its allies commit to presenting plans for the reconstruction of Iran worth no less than $300 billion.

8- Sixty days of negotiations to reach a final agreement on the nuclear file and the complete lifting of US and UN sanctions, as well as Security Council and IAEA Board of Governors resolutions.

9- Iran reaffirms its commitment to the Non-Proliferation Treaty by not producing nuclear weapons.

10- During the negotiation period, the US pledges not to increase its forces in the region and not to impose new sanctions.

11- The release of $24 billion of Iran’s frozen funds during the negotiation period, with half made available before the start of negotiations.

12- The establishment of a monitoring mechanism to implement the agreement.

13- Ratification of the final agreement through a UN Security Council resolution.

14- Final negotiations will not begin before the implementation of certain articles related to frozen funds and lifting the blockade. Final negotiations will be limited to the issue of nuclear material enrichment and lifting sanctions, with the missile program and support for “resistance fronts” permanently excluded from negotiations.
