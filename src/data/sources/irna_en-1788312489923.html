📡 EU support for US anti-Iran sanctions violates international law: Foreign Ministry spokesperson

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has criticized the European Union’s position on new US sanctions against Iran, saying the bloc’s support for Washington’s economic pressure violates international law, contradicts EU regulations and runs counter to Europe’s professed values.


Baqaei wrote on his X account early Tuesday, “The #EU appears to be surrendering its sovereignty, its laws & regulations, its values and its ethics to American coercion. Under its own legislation, the European Union shall not recognise the extraterritorial application of laws adopted by third countries and regards such effects as contrary to international law. The #EU_Blocking_Statute expressly prohibits EU operators from complying with any requirement or prohibition based on the specified foreign statutes.”
​
He noted, “How, then, can the Union now support the most predatory and unlawful unilateral coercive measures (#UCMs) against Iran? Contrast this with 1996, when the EU resolved to act independently in defence of its sovereignty against the American sanctions regime and adopted the Blocking Statute. Today it has become an accessory to Washington’s lawless conduct.”
​
The spokesperson said, “The Union’s endorsement of America’s unlawful economic war constitutes a grave violation of international law, too. Every EU member state shall be held accountable for the consequences of its contribution to this wrongful act. The European Union cannot endorse Washington’s #economic_terrorism against Iran while claiming to seek de-escalation and regional stability. The two positions are mutually exclusive. This irresponsible stance is evidence of the EU’s moral decay and political incompetence.”

💬 EU compromises with its autonomy, reduces itself to executor of US orders: Baqaei

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson, Esmaeil Baqaei, in a message to the European Union, has deplored the 27-nation bloc for blindly following the dictate of the United States, by renouncing its own independence and proclaimed values.


In a post on his X network on Tuesday, Baqaei responded to the European Union's statement in support of the US sanctions regime and economic war against Iran, saying the bloc has reduced its role to that of “an executor of American orders.”
​
He explained the EU move, citing Discours de la servitude volontaire (Discourse on Voluntary Servitude), a 16th-century treatise of French philosopher Étienne de La Boétie, which he said “identifies a fundamental truth about the enablers of tyranny.”
​
“Domination is not sustained by force alone; it often endures through the acquiescence of those who are capable of acting independently,” he wrote, adding, “Europe can no longer speak of ‘strategic autonomy’ while merely executing Washington’s orders.”
​
“Strategic autonomy is not secured by grandiose declarations. It is realized through the capacity to decide independently and responsibly — even if those decisions diverge from the position of a greater power.”
​
Baqaei reminded Europe in his post that it had a choice between behaving “like an independent actor” or growing “so accustomed to echoing another’s policies that it mistakes that echo for autonomy.”

🌍 President Pezeshkian proposes three initiatives to strengthen SCO role

📌 Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has called for simultaneous strengthening of security and economic cooperation within the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), presenting three initiatives to deepen financial and banking cooperation, support trade and investment, and enhance energy security.


Addressing the 26th SCO Summit, President Pezeshkian highlighted major security and economic challenges facing the region and stressed the need for greater cooperation among member states to consolidate stability and build a shared future.
​
He said trust-building, respect for national sovereignty, non-interference in internal affairs and intergovernmental cooperation were essential to achieving sustainable security, drawing on the SCO’s more than two decades of experience.
​
President Pezeshkian identified terrorism, violent extremism, drug trafficking, transnational organized crime and cybercrime, along with growing geopolitical rivalries, unilateral policies and the use of military force, as major threats to regional stability and emerging economies.
​
Referring to recent developments in West Asia and US and Israeli attacks against Iran, he described such actions as violations of the fundamental principles of the UN Charter and international law. He also outlined the course of the conflict and developments afterward, reaffirming Iran’s position regarding its commitments under the Islamabad memorandum of understanding.
​
The Iranian president emphasized that security and development were interconnected and inseparable, urging the SCO to address economic and security dimensions simultaneously.
​
He identified intra-SCO trade, customs facilitation, transport corridors, connectivity between ports and logistics centers, expanded transit capacity, and stronger supply-chain resilience as key areas for economic cooperation.
​
President Pezeshkian proposed three economic initiatives: establishing a bank to deepen financial and banking cooperation, creating an export and investment insurance union, and forming an energy consortium.
​
In the security field, he also proposed establishing a strategic security studies center within the organization to identify emerging threats and contribute to regional stability.

🎙 Islamic Unity Conference calls for practical solidarity, independent regional security

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Participants in the 40th International Islamic Unity Conference have called for turning unity into a practical approach among Muslim nations and governments, urging Islamic countries to strengthen cooperation.


In its final statement, the conference stressed that Islamic unity is a Quranic and civilizational principle. It called for managing religious differences through dialogue, mutual respect and reason, while expanding political, economic and cultural cooperation among Muslim countries.
​
The participants described Palestine as a test of Islamic solidarity, condemning Israeli atrocities in Gaza, Palestine, and Lebanon, and emphasizing support for legitimate resistance against occupation and aggression. They said the Palestinian issue has moved beyond sectarian divisions, becoming a point of convergence for Muslims.
​
Participants described the martyrdom of Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei as a major loss, honoring his legacy and affirming that his resistance-oriented leadership continues through Ayatollah Seyyed Mojtaba Khamenei.
​

📡 Iran Army chief: Recent war highlights vital role of air defense

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Chief Commander of the Islamic Republic of Iran Army Major General Amir Hatami has described air defense as a symbol of Iran’s defensive power and self-reliance, saying the experience of recent imposed wars has once again demonstrated its vital and strategic importance in securing the country and protecting its airspace.


In a message marking Air Defense Day and the anniversary of the establishment of the Khatam al-Anbiya Joint Air Defense Headquarters on Tuesday, Hatami congratulated Brigadier General Alireza Elhami, commander of the headquarters and the Army’s Air Defense Force, as well as its commanders, personnel, and families of fallen service members.
​
He said the establishment of the joint headquarters reflected the “valuable and farsighted initiative” of the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, to make integrated air defense a top priority in the country’s defense strategy.
​
He said the measure created coordination and unity among the air defense capabilities of Iran’s Armed Forces, providing a strong foundation for protecting the country’s airspace.
​
The Army chief highlighted the role of air defense personnel during the eight-year Iran-Iraq war, saying they demonstrated resistance and sacrifice on the front lines of the country’s defense.
​
He added that in subsequent years, Iranian specialists and young experts accelerated efforts toward self-sufficiency, indigenization, and upgrading air defense systems.
​
“The experience of recent imposed wars once again revealed the vital and strategic importance of air defense in ensuring security and protecting the country’s airspace,” Hatami said.
​
He described the use of advanced indigenous equipment and systems, alongside the knowledge, faith, and round-the-clock readiness of air defense personnel, as a symbol of Iran’s defensive power and self-reliance.
​
General Hatami also paid tribute to air defense personnel who were killed while defending Iran’s skies, saying they “stood by the systems and in the trenches of defending Iran’s airspace until their last moments” and sacrificed their lives for the nation’s security and dignity.

⚡️ Iran’s air defense vigilant, courageous in safeguarding skies from enemies: Navy commander

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Navy Commander, Rear Admiral Shahram Irani, says the country’s Air Defense Force stood against the aggressive American-Zionist enemy and defended the skies with vigilance and courage, relying on domestic know-how and capabilities.


Admiral Irani made the remarks in a message addressed to Brigadier General Alireza Elhami, commander of the Iranian Army's Air Defense Force, congratulating the establishment anniversary of Khatam al-Anbiya Air Defense Base and National Air Defense Day that falls this year on September 1.
​
He called the 10th of Shahrivar (the sixth month of the Solar Hijri calendar) a stark reminder of the struggle and sincerity of the country's brave warriors in safeguarding the skies over the Islamic Republic, with courage and sacrifice.
​
Stating that the Iranian Air Defense Force had been at the forefront of defense in all areas – from the eight-year Sacred Defense to the US-Israeli-imposed wars, the navy commander expressed his gratitude and honored the martyrs, who sacrificed their lives for the sake of the country and in the course of fulfilling their responsibility.
​
During the recent war, the role of air defense fighters was spectacular; relying on local knowledge and capabilities and utilizing domestically manufactured systems, they stood against the criminal American-Zionist enemy, he added.
​
Admiral Irani attributed the combat readiness and enhanced defensive capabilities of the Iranian Armed Forces to their synergy based on the character and school of the martyred Imam, and following the guidelines of the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, aimed at safeguarding the security and national interests.
​
He concluded his message by remembering the precious martyrs of the air defense and extending his warm wishes, on behalf of the Navy of the Islamic Republic of Iran, to all air defense commanders, personnel, staff and their esteemed families on the anniversary of the establishment of the Khatam al-Anbiya Air Defense Base.
