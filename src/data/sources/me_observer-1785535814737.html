Information obtained by Bint Jbeil website indicates that seismic fault observatories are concerned about the violent explosions in the South. The information warned that the current and recent tremors are activating the Roum Fault and the Yammouneh Fault segment, which extend from the far South towards northern occupied Palestine. This threat places populated coastal cities such as Sidon, Tyre, Haifa, and Nahariya in direct danger due to their proximity to the fault lines. Intense explosions could act as a trigger, accelerating the release of tectonic pressure stored in these active faults. Historically, the Roum Fault fueled the devastating 1956 earthquake that struck the South, with its damage extending to Haifa and Nahariya. The current concern lies in the possibility of a repeat earthquake scenario threatening residential and coastal areas on both sides of the border.
