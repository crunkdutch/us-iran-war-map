🇮🇷/ 🇴🇲 | Joint Iran-Oman statement on the Strait of Hormuz:

Iran and Oman issued a joint statement following talks between the foreign ministers of the two countries regarding the current situation in the Strait of Hormuz, which they said has resulted from the recent war and its catastrophic consequences.

The two sides discussed a phased framework that includes the establishment of a temporary joint navigation corridor through the Strait of Hormuz, as well as an agreement to implement a joint project to clear the strait of naval mines.

The statement said technical negotiations will continue with the aim of reaching an agreement on a permanent navigation corridor and the future management of the strait, along with mechanisms for information sharing, traffic management, and the provision of maritime and security services.

Both sides also emphasized the importance of holding joint discussions with the regional countries bordering the waters of the Persian Gulf. They reaffirmed the need to comply with applicable international law and to respect the sovereign rights of the coastal states.
