📄 Foreign Ministry condemns US attack on Larak Island, reaffirms Iran’s right to self-defense

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry has strongly condemned a US military attack on parts of Larak Island, saying the Iranian Armed Forces targeted American military bases in Jordan in exercise of the country’s inherent right to legitimate defense.


The ministry said in a statement early Monday that the US military violated Iran’s national sovereignty and territorial integrity by attacking parts of Larak Island late on Sunday, leaving several Iranian servicemen and civilians killed or wounded and causing damage to public and private property.
​
“In response to this military aggression and in accordance with its inherent right to legitimate defense, the powerful Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran delivered defensive and crushing strikes against US military bases located in Jordan, which served as the launchpad and support for the US military aggression against Iran,” the statement said.
​
The Foreign Ministry strongly condemned the US attack on Larak Island as a clear violation of Article 2(4) of the United Nations Charter and called on the UN Security Council and the secretary-general to fulfill their responsibilities to safeguard international peace and security and hold the aggressor accountable.
​
It also reminded the UN secretary-general, the president of the Security Council and all governments that have expressed concern over continued insecurity in the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz that the sole cause of insecurity affecting maritime navigation in the region has been the illegal and aggressive actions of the United States and Israel.
​
The ministry said those actions began with the war on Iran on February 28, and had continued over the past six months in various forms, including a naval blockade and other provocative and interventionist measures.
​
The Foreign Ministry stressed that Iran’s Armed Forces would not hesitate to exercise the country’s inherent right to legitimate defense and would respond decisively and appropriately to any further military aggression.
​
“The full responsibility for the consequences of the US’s new acts of aggression, as well as the continued naval blockade and economic war against Iran, rests with the US regime and all parties that, in any way, collude in or participate in preparing and carrying out the US’s illegal and unlawful measures,” the statement concluded.

⚡️ Iran Army pounds US air base in UAE with retaliatory drone strikes

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Republic of Iran Army says it has carried out retaliatory drone strikes against US troops and helicopters at Al Minhad Air Base in the United Arab Emirates.


In a statement on Monday, the Army's public relations department announced that the American base was targeted in response to the killing of members of the Islamic Revolution Guard Corps (IRGC) and civilians in Sunday’s US attack on Iran’s Larak Island.
​
“Since early morning, the Islamic Republic of Iran Army has targeted locations housing US military helicopters and personnel at Al Minhad base in the UAE with dozens of explosive drones,” the statement said.
​
Reiterating to avenge the blood of all martyrs of the US-Israeli-imposed war, the army said it remained vigilant to ensure sustainable security and territorial integrity until the enemy's threat from the region is eliminated.
​
The Al Minhad Air Base is a military installation located south of Dubai in the United Arab Emirates, and is known as an important logistics and air transport hub for foreign forces.
​
As part of its ongoing terror activities against Iran and elsewhere in the region, the American military attacked Larak Island on Sunday, prompting the Iranian Armed Forces to target the hideout of the terrorist enemy personnel and their war equipment.

🌍 Pezeshkian arrives in Kyrgyzstan

📌 Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has arrived in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, where he will deliver a speech at the annual summit of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO).


The president, who is visiting Kyrgyzstan to participate in the SCO summit 2026, was welcomed upon arrival in Bishkek by the country’s deputy cabinet chief on Monday.
​
During the two‑day trip, Pezeshkian will hold meetings with Kyrgyzstan’s president, Sadyr Japarov, as well as the president of Tajikistan and the prime ministers of India and Pakistan.
​
He will also take part in the official opening ceremony of the 6th World Nomad Games at the invitation of the Kyrgyz president.
​
On the second day of the visit, he will address the summit of SCO and SCO Plus leaders. Moreover, holding a bilateral meeting with the president of Russia is on the agenda of Pezeshkian’s trip.

📄 Iraqi communications minister visits Iran as two sides seek broader ICT cooperation

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran and Iraq are moving to expand their cooperation in information and communications technology (ICT), shifting from mainly operational coordination to a broader and more sustainable framework covering infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital economy and emerging technologies.


The latest step in the growing cooperation between the two countries is taken during the current visit of Iraq’s Communications Minister Mustafa Jabar Sanad who arrived in Tehran on Monday at the invitation of his Iranian counterpart Sattar Hashemi.
​
Sanad’s visit to Tehran is aimed at furthering discussions on communications and technology cooperation, and also participating in the 29th International Exhibition of Electronics, Computer and E-Commerce (ELECOMP), one of Iran’s major technology events.
​
A specialized Iran-Iraq forum is also scheduled on the sidelines of the exhibition to explore joint opportunities in ICT and facilitate direct engagement among technology companies, operators and digital-economy players from both countries.
​
In mid-July, Hashemi also paid a visit to Baghdad, where he and his Iraqi counterpart discussed improving telecommunications infrastructure, roaming services and connectivity along the Iran-Iraq border, particularly during the annual Arbaeen pilgrimage. They also agreed to provide free internet access for pilgrims along the Najaf-Karbala route.
​
Beyond Arbaeen-related services, the talks covered Iran’s experience in cybersecurity, digital economy, e-health, space technology, and telecommunications research. Tehran has also expressed readiness to share its expertise in network resilience, cybersecurity and fiber-optic infrastructure with Iraq.
