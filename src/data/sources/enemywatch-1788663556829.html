🇾🇪 | Yemen Battlefield Report | September 6, 2026

The battlefield in western and southern Taiz Governorate has entered a decisive phase as the Yemeni Armed Forces continue a rapid offensive against Saudi and Emirati-backed forces, while expanding control over strategic terrain and tightening pressure on Taiz’s remaining supply routes.

🇾🇪 Ansarallah advances toward Al-Burain and Al-Masrakh

Ansarallah forces are now less than 4 kilometers from Al-Burain Market, continuing their advance south of Taiz after moving around Jabal Habashi. The offensive is now pushing toward Al-Burain and Al-Masrakh, with the objective of cutting the remaining supply corridors connecting Taiz to Al-Mokha.

If this spearhead succeeds, the Taiz-Mokha road will effectively be severed, placing Saudi and Emirati-backed forces in Taiz under increasing logistical pressure.

🎯 New territorial gains across the Taiz front

The Yemeni Armed Forces have made rapid gains across western Taiz, taking control of several rural villages along the Maqbanah District frontline.

According to the latest battlefield updates, Yemeni forces have:

• Freed and taken control of the Al-Makazma area.

• Advanced into the Al-Akkad front.

• Captured the Sherajah Junction.
• Freed the town of Najd al-Nashamah.

• Advanced south toward the Moazza’a Junction, located at the entrance to the strategically important Jabal al-Rawa.

The advances place Ansarallah on dominant high ground around Jabal Habashi, bringing Taiz’s western supply route under direct fire control.

🚀 Missile and artillery operations intensify

The Yemeni Armed Forces launched violent missile bombardments against Saudi-backed militia positions across Taiz Governorate.

Operations included:

• Ballistic missile strikes targeting Saudi-backed positions in Taiz.

• Initial reports of five Yemeni ballistic missiles launched toward Al-Mokha.

• Continued missile attacks on Saudi-backed positions along the western coast.

• Heavy artillery shelling targeting Saudi mercenary positions across multiple fronts.

The bombardments coincided with renewed ground offensives across the Taiz-Hodeidah axis.

⚔️ Fierce battles continue in western Taiz

Heavy battles remain underway between the Yemeni Armed Forces and Saudi-backed mercenaries across the Al-Kadha, Al-Barh, Maqbanah, and western Taiz fronts.

Reports from the battlefield indicate:

• Fierce clashes continue throughout western Taiz.

• Saudi-backed militia positions are coming under sustained pressure.

• Yemeni forces continue advancing despite ongoing resistance.

🇦🇪 Heavy losses among Emirati-backed forces

The Yemeni Armed Forces reported killing a number of commanders and fighters affiliated with Emirati-backed Giant Brigades and Saudi-backed militias during their recent operations.

The deaths come after the UAE-backed forces entered the battle in support of Saudi-backed formations attempting to halt the Yemeni advance in Taiz. In Taiz, Yemeni forces inflicted heavy losses on the UAE-backed Giant Brigades, which entered the battle in support of Saudi-backed forces.

The commander of the Rapid Response Company, Mohammed Seif Al-Muhwili, was killed along with several other fighters during the fighting. Following violent battles, the Yemeni Armed Forces announced they had freed and taken full control of the strategic Al-Kadaha area in Taiz, expelling Saudi-backed forces from the area and consolidating their advances on the Taiz front.

Separately, Hodeidah Governor Abdullah Qatifi held Saudi-backed forces and their mercenaries responsible for the artillery attack on a civilian family in Al-Jarrahi District, which martyred an elderly man and his daughter and seriously injured his wife and another relative.
