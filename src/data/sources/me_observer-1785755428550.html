⚡️ Medical Referrals Committee Issues Updated Statistics for Referrals File Until August 1, 2026

The Medical Referrals Committee at the Ministry of Health issued updated statistics clarifying the workflow in the medical referrals file until August 1, 2026, where the total number of medical referrals issued in hospitals reached 31,101 referrals.

The statistics showed that 20,644 referrals were approved and are still on the waiting list, while the number of nominated and priority cases reached 5,369 cases, distributed according to priority levels as follows: 155 cases (A+), 2,221 cases (A), 9,947 cases (B), and 8,321 cases (C).

Regarding the status of referrals, the data showed that:
* 1,459 referrals are still under process.
* 676 referrals are under review for completion of required documents.
* 805 cases were decided for local treatment.
* 35 cases were treated locally.
* 4,736 patients were able to travel for treatment.
* 1,831 cases were classified as deceased.

The committee also clarified that the total number of patients who left the Gaza Strip for treatment during the period from February 2, 2026, to August 1, 2026, reached 1,856 patients, including 1,641 patients via the Rafah crossing, and 215 patients via the Kerem Shalom crossing.

The Medical Referrals Committee affirmed its continued follow-up of all patient files according to approved medical and professional standards, and its work to complete the necessary procedures to ensure patient service according to medical priorities and available capabilities.
