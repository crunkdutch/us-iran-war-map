⚡️ #Important_Announcement | Ministry of Interior and Bar Association Announce Start of Receiving Passport Applications for High School and Postgraduate Students in Gaza Strip
The Palestinian Ministry of Interior, in cooperation with the Palestinian Bar Association, announces the start of receiving passport applications at the Association's headquarters in the governorates of the Gaza Strip, as part of efforts to facilitate procedures for citizens and organize the process of receiving applications to ensure rapid completion and quality follow-up.
The Ministry confirms that receiving applications includes high school students for the academic year 2025/2026, and the Palestinian Bar Association has been approved as an official body to receive applications according to a mechanism that begins with obtaining the application form from accredited service offices for only 20 shekels, then completing the data and attaching the required documents and submitting them at the Association's headquarters, while the passport issuance fee is only 230 shekels per application.
The Ministry also announces the reception of applications from postgraduate students who have obtained external scholarships, provided that the applications are supported by an official letter issued by the Palestinian embassy in the country where the student has been accepted, proving their acceptance of the scholarship and the completion of travel procedures.
The Bar Association indicates that applications will be received at its headquarters in Gaza Governorate, Central Governorate, and Khan Yunis Governorate.
The Ministry of Interior calls on students to adhere to the announced instructions and complete all required documents before heading to the Association's headquarters and not to pay any unauthorized fees, stressing that applications are received during official working days, excluding official holidays.
The Palestinian Ministry of Interior appreciates the fruitful cooperation with the Palestinian Bar Association, stressing that this partnership comes within the framework of developing services provided to citizens and providing the necessary facilities for high school and postgraduate students, enabling them to complete their educational and academic journey and reflecting the integration of efforts between state institutions and professional associations to serve the Palestinian people.
