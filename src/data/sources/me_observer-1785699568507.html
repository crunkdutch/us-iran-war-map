⚡️ ✈️ United States Air Force (USAF) – Middle East Activities
Suweihan Air Base, United Arab Emirates
⏺ Over the past hours, significant movement of C-17 transport aircraft has been observed from Suweihan Air Base in the United Arab Emirates.
⏺ Since approximately 18:00 Zulu (UTC) on the evening of July 31, five C-17 aircraft with call signs MOOSE 54 to MOOSE 58 have arrived at Suweihan Air Base and departed shortly thereafter. With the exception of one aircraft, all of these planes have flown to King Hussein International Airport in Jordan. Aircraft MOOSE 54 has proceeded to Ramstein Air Base in Germany.
⏺ A few days prior, similar movements involving several C-17s were observed in Erbil. Reports subsequently emerged that a Patriot missile defense system had been removed from Iraq.
⏺ It is not yet clear what cargo these aircraft are transporting, but ACARS data indicates that they first consolidate at Suweihan Air Base and then proceed towards Jordan.
