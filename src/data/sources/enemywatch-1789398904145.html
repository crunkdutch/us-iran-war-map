The Yemeni Armed Forces, with the help of Almighty Allah and with broad participation from our people and their free tribes, launched the large-scale and special military operation “Allah is Stronger in Might and More Severe in Punishment” on 3 September from multiple axes to expel Saudi military mobilizations from several districts along Yemen’s western coast.

These Saudi mobilizations have committed the most horrific crimes against civilians and seek to subjugate Yemen’s geography to Saudi occupation and control.

By the grace and success of Allah Almighty, the operation was crowned with victory. Despite the heavy Saudi air cover provided in support of its forces, the enemy failed, by Allah’s will, to influence the course of the operation.

First:

Saudi military mobilizations were expelled from six districts across Taiz and Hodeidah governorates, covering a total area of 5,400 square kilometers, which have now become secure and stable by the grace of Allah.

The operation struck seven military formations belonging to the Saudi mobilizations, comprising 38 military brigades, killing, capturing, and wounding hundreds of their personnel.

Third:

A number of our prisoners, who had been held for years by Saudi mercenaries on the western coast, were liberated.

Fourth:

By the grace of Allah, the Yemeni Armed Forces successfully carried out 32 air defense operations against Saudi warplanes and shot down nine aircraft.
