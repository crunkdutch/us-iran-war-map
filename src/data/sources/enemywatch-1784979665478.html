🇮🇷 | Breaking — American strikes intensify across southern Iran as regional war escalates:

Over the past two hours, southern Iran has come under intense American airstrikes, with the attacks still continuing. The strikes followed Iran’s reported targeting of commercial shipping in the Strait of Hormuz. According to reports, four commercial vessels were attacked over the past several hours, including the Qatari owned LPG tanker AL REKAYYAT, the Saudi owned crude oil tanker WEDYAN, the UAE owned LPG tanker AL MARYAH, while another vessel targeted this afternoon has not yet been identified.

Another unreported maritime incident is also said to have occurred during the night of July 5 to 6. Reports claim that one cruise missile and four Razi or Raad 3 loitering drones were used in these operations. In response, America launched a large scale wave of strikes against southern Iran, with U.S. Central Command stating that the attacks were carried out in response to Iran’s reported targeting of commercial ships transiting the Strait of Hormuz.

Heavy aerial activity has since been reported across Hormozgan Province and surrounding areas. Multiple waves of strikes have reportedly targeted Sirik, Qeshm Island, Bandar Abbas, Kharg Island, Bushehr Province, Hormozgan Airport, Dayyer Port, Khormuj, Taheroui village, and the commercial pier at Ziarat village in Sirik. Reports indicate around ten American airstrikes have been carried out so far, while numerous secondary explosions continue to be reported across the affected areas. Several civilians were reportedly injured by shrapnel following strikes near the commercial wharf in Sirik and were transferred to Minab Hospital. Press TV has also confirmed reports of fresh explosions at both Qeshm Island and Kharg Island, indicating that the scope of the operation continues to expand across southern Iran.

The military situation is currently ongoing and continues to escalate. Fresh reports indicate another violent wave of attacks against Kharg Island and Qeshm Island, while additional explosions have been reported in Bushehr Province. At the same time, reports indicate that Iran has launched anti ship missiles toward U.S. Navy vessels operating in the Persian Gulf, while other reports suggest an anti ship missile has also been fired toward the Strait of Hormuz. Further exchanges between Iranian and American forces are being reported, with additional strikes and missile launches continuing to emerge as the situation develops.
