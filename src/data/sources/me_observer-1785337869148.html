⚡️ IRIB's explanation regarding the interview with a member of the Supreme Council for National Media
IRIB will remain committed to legal and professional frameworks
🔹Following the interview of one of the esteemed members of the Supreme Council for IRIB concerning the recent meeting of the council with the political deputy of the organization, it is hereby announced: The duties of the esteemed Supreme Council are clearly stated in the law. According to the law, the Supreme Council for IRIB can, while overseeing the proper conduct of the organization's affairs, raise any necessary warnings.
🔹Accordingly, in the current period, all senior officials, including the head and deputies of the organization, have repeatedly attended meetings with the members of this council to present reports and discuss important issues of the national media, keeping the council informed of the organization's activities. They have also requested assistance from the three branches of government through their representatives in this council for the national media to properly carry out its missions.
🔹Fortunately, in the current period, the relationship between the council and the organization has been at a very desirable level, accompanied by mutual respect. The organization's officials have always, in cordial interaction with the Supreme Council, responded to the questions of the esteemed council members while presenting their demands from the branches of government, and have prepared and submitted the necessary reports to the council. In yesterday's meeting, as is customary for the presence of organization officials in the Supreme Council's session, the esteemed political deputy of the organization presented a comprehensive report on the organization's performance in the relevant area, listened to the opinions and views of the esteemed council members, and welcomed their suggestions.
🔹It is evident that in a vast organization like the national media, with hundreds of diverse missions and more than 150 radio, television, and internet channels at various local, national, and international levels, the process of carrying out missions, due to diverse and numerous external challenges, requires continuous review, care, and oversight to ensure that media services to audiences and the performance of duties are carried out in the best possible way. For this reason, IRIB has always welcomed and will continue to welcome any legal oversight, especially by the Supreme Council for the Organization.
🔹Finally, for clarification, it is announced: The headline chosen by the state news agency IRNA for the statements of one of the esteemed council members, titled "Necessary warnings were given to IRIB / They promised to reform," contains ambiguity and is not necessarily related to the false and unfair accusations attributed to the national media in recent days under the title of censoring the statements of the country's officials. The necessary explanations have already been brought to the attention of the public in the public relations announcement of the national media.
🔹It is reiterated that regarding the aforementioned issue, the national media has acted precisely according to its legal duties and professional mission, and from now on, as in the past, it will remain committed to legal and professional frameworks. The Public Relations Department of the Political Deputy of IRIB, while announcing the aforementioned points to remove ambiguity, emphasizes the necessity of observing ethical and professional principles in drafting and highlighting news by media colleagues.
