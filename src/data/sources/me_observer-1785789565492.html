"Today, I arrived in the holy city of Najaf and am on my way to holy Karbala to participate in the Arbaeen ceremonies.

The Arbaeen ceremonies are a grand procession that embodies the unity of the Iraqi and Iranian peoples. Pilgrims from both countries constitute the largest percentage of participants in this event, reflecting the strength of the religious and ideological ties between the two peoples.
This unity was also previously evident during the funeral ceremonies for the martyr Sayyed Ali Khamenei, where it showed the depth of feelings and the strong, established relations between the two peoples.
Fortunately, our relations with the Iraqi government are currently at their best stages, and the recent visit of the Iraqi Prime Minister to Tehran witnessed very constructive and fruitful discussions.
Cooperation between Iran and Iraq is close and extensive in various fields, starting from political coordination and adopting common positions on issues of mutual concern, to economic and security cooperation, securing borders, regulating cross-border trade, and developing joint transport and transit routes."
