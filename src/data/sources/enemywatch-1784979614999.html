🇮🇷 | Various statements by the IRGC and the Iranian Armed Forces during the funeral of Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei:

1. Iranian Army statement

Following the aggression launched by U.S. forces against areas inside the country and units of the Iranian Army, the Iranian Army stated that it had targeted Patriot systems in Kuwait, satellite early warning reception sites in Qatar, and fuel depots belonging to U.S. forces in Bahrain using a large number of attack drones.

The statement added that the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran would continue, under the guidance of the Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces, to prevent what it described as the objectives of the United States and would continue defending the ideals of the Islamic Revolution.

2. Regional strikes

The Iranian Army stated that it targeted the following with drones:

- A Patriot air defense system in Kuwait.

- An early warning satellite antenna site in Qatar.

- Fuel storage facilities used by U.S. forces in Bahrain.

The IRGC stated that it targeted:

1) Arifjan Base, Kuwait.

2) Ali al Salem Air Base, Kuwait.

3) The U.S. Navy’s Fifth Fleet headquarters in Juffair, Bahrain.

4) Sheikh Isa Air Base, Bahrain.

The accompanying reports claimed to show heavy smoke and explosions at military facilities in Bahrain.

3. IRGC Navy

The IRGC Navy stated that, over the previous two weeks, it had established control over the Strait of Hormuz, secured the waterway, and gradually reopened it to shipping.

According to the statement, vessel transit has reached approximately 50 percent of pre-war traffic, with capacity continuing to increase for ships that receive permission from the IRGC Navy and comply with the security regulations established by the Islamic Republic.

The statement further declared that foreign powers have no share in the Strait of Hormuz and warned that any further U.S. military interference or attempts to influence navigation would face a response and could disrupt the reopening process.

5. IRGC statement

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) stated that, following its earlier warning that any renewed attacks would expand the scope of Iran’s response, it had launched what it described as the second phase of its retaliation.

According to the IRGC, at 2:20 p.m., its Aerospace Force struck what it described as the enemy’s command and control center in West Asia and Al Azraq Air Base in Jordan using ten ballistic missiles.

The statement warned that, if further U.S. attacks occur, all American military bases in the region would remain within the range of Iranian strikes.
