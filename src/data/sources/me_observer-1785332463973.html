⚡️ Washington's Sensitive Secret: How Much of America's Missile Inventory Remains?
The Wall Street Journal, in a report, has examined the sensitive state of America's missile inventory after approximately five months of engagement in the war with Iran. The report, published yesterday, focuses on the internal concerns of the U.S. government regarding the severe depletion of precision ammunition stockpiles, especially offensive and air-defense missiles.
According to analyses by the Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS), the stockpiles of some of these missiles have been severely depleted: for example, about 30% of Tomahawks, nearly half of Patriot and THAAD, and a significant portion of JASSM have been reduced.
A full replenishment of these stockpiles could take 3 to 6 years, even with increased production.
