⚡️ Abu Ali Express blog: Recently, we have seen several cases where the killing of Hamas operatives, who were targets of raids, was announced on social media in Gaza and by the Israeli army spokesperson, while they were in fact alive (seriously wounded) and died later. For example, policeman Yaman Obaid, whose killing was announced by the Israeli army on the 14th of the month (among a group of 7-8 Hamas police officers who were killed in the northern Gaza Strip), actually died of his wounds last Saturday, 11 days after his killing was announced. The same applies to Hammam Eid, who was targeted on Saturday. His killing was announced on social media in Gaza, while the Israeli army announced his killing yesterday, but he actually died of his wounds only this morning, after his killing was announced. These are just two cases that have emerged and are easy for us to point out, but they indicate a broader phenomenon, where Hamas tries to deceive the Israeli army into thinking that the assassination operation has succeeded and that the target has been removed from the wanted list, and can continue his life without significant intelligence surveillance. Hamas recently announced that it had arrested a number of Israeli agents, who confirmed to its operatives the results of the assassination operations from the hospital morgue. It seems that Hamas has decided to focus on this issue to deceive Israeli intelligence and preserve the lives of its operatives. It is advisable to exercise more caution in verifying the deaths of targets, now that it is known that Hamas is also working to mislead in this area.
