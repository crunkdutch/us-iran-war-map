🎙 US military cannot call its ‘barbaric crimes’ against civilians a ‘mistake’: Iran’s OIC mission

📌 Tehran, IRNA –Iran’s mission to the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) has denounced the US military for proudly acknowledging yet another “cowardly and illegal attack” on an Iranian oil tanker, while evading responsibility for the killing of civilians.


The Iranian mission reacted on Sunday to a statement issued by the US Central Command (CENTCOM) claiming “precision and professionalism” of American forces in the attack on the Iranian vessel.
​
The message mocked CENTCOM for describing its act of aggression as “precision and professionalism,” noting that “a cowardly attack” on a commercial oil tanker was not something to be proud of or praise.
​
“So how can attacks on an elementary school in Minab, a sports club in Lamerd, a wedding ceremony in Kuhestak, and other civilian sites be dismissed merely as a ‘mistake’ or ‘targeting error’?” the OIC mission deplored the US military for evading responsibility for these attacks.
​
It noted that the CENTCOM statement was a clear acknowledgment of “all the barbaric crimes committed by the US terrorist forces during the aggressive and illegal war against Iran.”
​
You cannot invoke “precision” whenever it suits you and call it a “mistake” when civilians pay the price; the Iranian mission further reminded the United States and its military in the West Asia region.

⚡️ Mexico’s ambassador welcomes joint trade cooperation committee with Iran

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Mexico’s ambassador to Tehran, Guillermo Puente Ordorica, has welcomed the formation of a joint Iran-Mexico committee to expand trade relations, identifying health tourism, the automotive industry and auto parts manufacturing as potential areas of cooperation.


Speaking at a meeting with Samad Hassanzadeh, president of the Iran Chamber of Commerce, Industries, Mines and Agriculture, Guillermo Puente Ordorica said the committee could help expand trade and investment ties between the two countries.
​
“I welcome the idea of forming a joint committee and believe this method will have a positive effect on expanding relations,” he said.
​
The Mexican ambassador said Mexico’s new government has developed an economic program for cooperation with countries around the world, incorporating various factors to expand trade relations.
​
Puente Ordorica also stressed the need for the Iran Chamber of Commerce to establish relations with Mexican private companies within the agreed framework and welcomed the exchange of trade delegations between the two countries.
​
He identified health tourism as an area with potential for cooperation, while noting Mexico’s capabilities in automobiles, auto parts and heavy industries.
​
He proposed first establishing the joint committee, followed by exchanges of trade delegations, allowing the two sides to become familiar with each other’s capabilities before taking further steps.
​
Hassanzadeh also stressed the importance of exchanging trade delegations to help economic actors become more familiar with the capabilities of each country, as well as the need to establish a joint committee to improve coordination in expanding trade and investment relations.
​
He also highlighted the importance of exchanging economic information through the joint committee, saying the mechanism could help the two countries benefit from their respective relations with other countries.
​
Hassanzadeh said Iran is ready to cooperate with Mexico in areas including new agricultural technologies, artificial intelligence, technical and engineering services, medical equipment and large-scale projects.
​
He also highlighted export opportunities in petrochemicals, minerals, agricultural products, industrial equipment, technical and engineering services, tourism and health tourism, as well as potential for joint investment.
​
He expressed hope that resolving transport and financial transfer obstacles would help create better conditions for expanding economic ties between Iran and Mexico.

💬 Pezeshkian: Expansion of comprehensive cooperation with Tajikistan on Iran’s foreign policy agenda

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated Tajikistan on its Independence Day, saying Tehran has made the expansion of comprehensive cooperation with Dushanbe a foreign policy priority.


In a message to President Emomali Rahmon on Monday, Pezeshkian congratulated him and the Tajik people on the 35th anniversary of their country’s independence, which falls on September 9.
​
He described the occasion as the “manifestation of national sovereignty and the beginning of fundamental transformations” on the path toward Tajikistan’s development and prosperity.
​
“This auspicious occasion is a reminder of the Tajik people’s firm determination and strong resolve in their pursuit of independence, dignity and pride. Undoubtedly, this great achievement is a source of joy and pride for all those around the world who cherish our shared culture and civilization,” said Pezeshkian.
​
The Iranian president described the long-standing historical, cultural, and linguistic bonds as well as civilizational commonalities between Iran and Tajikistan as a great asset for deepening and expanding “brotherly relations” between the two countries.
​
“The Islamic Republic of Iran has made the expansion of comprehensive cooperation with the Republic of Tajikistan a priority of its foreign policy and reaffirms its determination to further strengthen bilateral relations in pursuit of mutual interests, regional stability and security, and the advancement of our shared culture and literature,” President Pezeshkian said in his message.

💬 Iran unveils new domestically-developed antivirus software

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Simultaneously with ELECOMP 2026, the country's largest exhibition for electronics, computer technologies, and e-commerce, Iran unveiled its second homegrown antivirus software, dubbed Ayyza, using artificial intelligence and machine learning to detect an unknown malware and unidentified cyber threats.


A group of senior officials from security, defense, cyberspace, and knowledge-based firms attended the unveiling ceremony of the new national antivirus software on the sidelines of the 29th edition of the ELECOMP Exhibition, held from 31 August to 3 September in Tehran.
​
Ayyza combines XDR, EDR, and antivirus technologies in a single security ecosystem and is designed to provide a local, specialized solution to threats against the country's infrastructure and digital assets.
​
The software is another piece of evidence of Iran’s growing scientific know-how and its progressive path to building the country’s cyber resilience and self-reliance, in a situation where digital-based economic, banking, industrial, communication, and service activities necessitate the protection of cyber infrastructure more than ever.
​
Antivirus, as one of the basic layers of cyber defense, is at the forefront of identifying and combating malware, and Ayyza is part of Iran’s new strategic documents in the field of passive defense that emphasize reliance on domestic capabilities, the development of indigenous products, and reducing dependence on foreign technologies.
​
The Ayyza software is the outcome of investing in knowledge, specialized human resources, and domestic engineering capabilities, and its launch can be seen as a sign of the country’s cybersecurity industry moving from a consumer to designer and developer of strategic products.
​
ELECOMP 2026 was held with the presence of more than 350 participants in 14 exhibition halls and covered areas such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, software and hardware, digital services and innovative businesses.

📡 Iran warns South Korea over military presence in Persian Gulf

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Iran has warned South Korea against any military participation in the Persian Gulf and Strait of Hormuz, saying such involvement would amount to direct support for an aggressor and could have serious consequences.


Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei made the remarks on Monday in a Korean-language post on X, stressing the long history of relations between Iran and South Korea.
​
Baqaei said the two countries have maintained relations for more than 64 years based on mutual respect, adding that Iran values its friendly ties with Seoul.
​
However, he said that under current circumstances, when Iran is defending itself against US acts of aggression and war crimes against Iranian civilians, any operational participation or military presence by other countries in the Persian Gulf or Strait of Hormuz would constitute direct support for the aggressor.
​
He also said no responsible sovereign state would submit to US pressure or coercion to participate in aggression against Iran.
