⚡️ MP Abu Hamdan: Resistance's Weapon to be Discussed within a Defensive Strategy After Achievement
Rami Abu Hamdan, a member of the Loyalty to the Resistance Bloc, confirmed that the issue of the resistance's weapon cannot be approached before achieving a set of national entitlements, stressing that any discussion about it must take place within an internal framework and through a national defensive strategy.
Abu Hamdan criticized the performance of the authority in Lebanon, asserting that it is "subject to American pressure" and is moving towards making concessions that affect national sovereignty, according to his expression.
He believed that the officials' performance contributed to deepening internal division, accusing some parties of attacking symbols and sanctities and belittling the sacrifices of the resistance fighters.
He touched upon the President's recent visit to Washington, clarifying that its results were "non-existent," according to his description, and directed criticism at his stance on the statements issued by US President Donald Trump during the visit.
He questioned the nature of American support for the Lebanese army, calling for it to be provided with defensive weapons capable of confronting "Israeli" aggressions.
Abu Hamdan said: "The United States does not play the role of a mediator," accusing it of supporting "Israel," and affirming that the goal of some Western armament projects is to confront the resistance, not to protect Lebanon. He touched upon the issue of the displaced and reconstruction, believing that the authority agreed to link the return of the displaced to their villages and the reconstruction of affected areas with the disarmament of the resistance, stressing that this issue must be discussed within an internal national framework, in line with what was stated in the presidential speech and the ministerial statement regarding the defensive strategy.
He said: "Any discussion about the resistance's weapon must be preceded by the achievement of five basic demands: the complete "Israeli" withdrawal from Lebanese territories, the cessation of "Israeli" aggressions, the return of the displaced to their areas, reconstruction, and the release of Lebanese prisoners."
Abu Hamdan addressed the "Israeli" shelling that targeted the Marqaba water station, clarifying that targeting vital installations in the south raises questions about how the official handling of "Israeli" aggressions and the concept of national sovereignty.
He stressed that "Israel" remains the permanent enemy for the resistance," clarifying that some political forces seek to change this equation.
In another context, Abu Hamdan criticized the recent statements of the Lebanese Foreign Minister regarding the resistance's weapon, explaining that they exceed the existing national understandings regarding addressing this issue.
