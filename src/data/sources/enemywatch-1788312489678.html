🇮🇷 Iran

- Tensions continue to escalate in and around the Strait of Hormuz after a Saudi VLCC (Very Large Crude Carrier) identified as CEDAR was disabled while attempting to transit the southern corridor of the strait through Omani waters.

- Iranian media said the supertanker, capable of carrying up to 2 million barrels of oil, stopped after suddenly activating its Automatic Identification System (AIS) while passing through the waterway. Iranian reports also said the vessel entered the strait with its AIS switched off and without prior coordination with the Persian Gulf Strait Authority, adding that Iranian naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz have not yet been cleared.

- Reports also said three cruise missiles were launched from Sirik toward targets in the Strait of Hormuz, further raising tensions in the strategic waterway.

- The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) first reported a security incident involving an oil tanker near Oman. The British maritime authority later said the tanker was struck by three projectiles while sailing outside the Strait of Hormuz, approximately 17 nautical miles east of Khasab, Oman. No injuries were reported.

🇸🇾 Occupied Syria

Syrian authorities said four security personnel were injured during clashes with armed outlaw groups in the countryside of As-Suwayda, where internal security positions came under mortar and heavy machine gun fire, causing casualties among both civilians and security personnel.

Israeli occupation forces continued military incursions into southern Syria. Local sources reported repeated raids in Jamla in western Daraa countryside, with occupation patrols consisting of up to 10 military vehicles entering the village, searching homes, and later re-entering the area in the Yarmouk Basin.

Syrian sources also reported 95 Israeli incursions and violations across southern Syria during the month of August.

🇵🇸 Palestine

🇮🇷 Iran

Police said the suspect was identified and arrested in Rask County, while efforts to apprehend the remaining suspects are ongoing.

The US State Department has approved a possible $800 million military sale to Iraq involving Bell 412EPX and Bell 407M helicopters and related equipment.
