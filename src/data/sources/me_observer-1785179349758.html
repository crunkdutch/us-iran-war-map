⚡️ Romanian Ministry of Energy:
One of the reactors at the Cernavodă Nuclear Power Plant will be temporarily shut down starting July 28 as a precautionary measure due to low water flow in the Danube River.
This measure is preventive and necessary to protect equipment associated with three pumps of the cooling system.
Any potential malfunction in one of the pumps would require complex repairs lasting several months and could extend up to a year.
Peak consumption this week is estimated at approximately 7,300 megawatts, which is about 700 megawatts less than the peak values recorded in previous weeks due to the holiday period.
The estimated volume of domestic production will range between 4,000 and 4,300 megawatts, and the difference needed to cover consumption will be provided through imports under commercial terms.
