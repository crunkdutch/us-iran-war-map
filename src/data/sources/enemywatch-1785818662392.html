❤️ | 🇮🇶 🌎| The 1347th Arbaeen is being commemorated today in Karbala and across the globe:

Arbaeen marks the 40th day after the martyrdom of Imam Husayn (A), the grandson of Prophet Muhammad (S), who was martyred in the Battle of Karbala in 680 CE (61 AH) along with his family members and companions after refusing to pledge allegiance to Yazid I.

The first Arbaeen was observed 1,346 years ago, when Sayyidah Zaynab (A), the sister of Imam Husayn (A), together with Imam Ali ibn al Husayn Zayn al Abidin (A) and the surviving members of the Prophet’s family, returned to Karbala after being released from captivity under the Umayyad ruler Yazid I. They visited the graves of the martyrs, mourned their sacrifice, and ensured that the message of Karbala would endure for future generations.

Today, Arbaeen is regarded as one of the largest annual Islamic gatherings in the world, with millions of pilgrims traveling to Karbala, Iraq, to visit the shrine of Imam Husayn (A). At the same time, millions more commemorate Arbaeen across the globe through mourning processions, religious gatherings, lectures, charitable initiatives, and acts of service. The commemoration stands as a global expression of faith, remembrance, justice, sacrifice, and loyalty to the values for which Imam Husayn (A) gave his life.

The commemoration stands as a global expression of freedom, justice, dignity, and sacrifice in the path of resistance. It symbolizes standing for Islam, rejecting humiliation and oppression, and upholding the principles for which Imam Husayn (A) gave his life: speaking the truth without fear, resisting tyranny, defending the oppressed, and remaining steadfast in the face of injustice regardless of the consequences.
