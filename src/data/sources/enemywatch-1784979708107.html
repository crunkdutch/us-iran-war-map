5) Popular Mobilization Forces: Preparations Complete for the Funeral Ceremony

The Deputy Chief of Staff for Operations of Iraq’s Popular Mobilization Forces (PMF), Yasser Hussein Al Issawi, announced the completion of organizational preparations for the funeral ceremony of Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Ali Khamenei (Quddisa Sirruh), scheduled to take place next Wednesday in the provinces of Najaf and Karbala.

Speaking to the media, Al Issawi said that his visit to Najaf was aimed at reviewing the organizational and service plan prepared by the Middle Euphrates Operations Command. The plan involves the participation of various PMF formations, including command units, directorates, intelligence and security agencies, medical teams, support units, and several brigades assigned with organizational and logistical responsibilities.

He added that the plans cover both Najaf and Karbala and were developed in coordination with PMF formations, security forces, and the relevant authorities. Alternative organizational and service plans have also been prepared to ensure the smooth conduct of the funeral ceremonies. He emphasized that these preparations reflect a high level of readiness and the extensive resources allocated to ensure the event’s success.

Al Issawi further stated that the funeral ceremonies represent a message of loyalty to the blood of those martyred as a result of what he described as the Zionist-American aggression. He added that Iraq is honored to host the event and welcome participants, given what he described as the significant religious and Islamic standing of the late Sayyid Ali Khamenei.
