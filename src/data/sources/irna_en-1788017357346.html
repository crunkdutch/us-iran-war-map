🔻 Exclusive | Iran envoy: Europe must accept reality of a strong Iran

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Ambassador to Germany Majid Nili says Europe must accept the reality of a strong Iran, stressing that Tehran will continue pursuing dialogue and engagement based on mutual respect while firmly defending its national interests.


In an exclusive interview with IRNA, Nili said Iran’s resistance to the recent US-Israeli military aggression has further demonstrated the country’s decisive position, defense capabilities, and resilience.
​
“Despite the existing political atmosphere and pressures, the path of dialogue and engagement based on mutual respect is still being pursued,” he said.
​
On February 28, the United States and Israel initiated a war of aggression against Iran with the assassination of Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei and several top military commanders. They also targeted a school in Minab, southern Iran, killing scores of elementary school children.
​
In response, the Iranian Armed Forces carried out retaliatory attacks during the 40 days of the war, targeting US and Israeli military assets, which resulted in significant damage.
​
Nili also criticized Europe’s response to the war against Iran, saying the country’s regional position, defense capabilities and resilience has attracted growing attention among political and expert circles.
​
He said even Iran’s critics have realized that the country could not be excluded from the region’s political and security equations.
​
The ambassador also said the embassy’s main priorities over the past year included addressing the affairs of Iranians living in Germany, maintaining channels of communication with German officials, and defending Iran’s national interests.
​
He identified supporting Iranians abroad, expanding economic and trade relations, strengthening public diplomacy and engaging with German society as other priorities for the embassy.

🔻 Culture minister urges Muslims to promote the Prophet’s message of humanity

📌 TEHRAN, Iran — Iranian Culture Minister Abbas Salehi urges Islamic countries to strengthen cultural cooperation and introduce the human and civilizational message of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) to the world, describing the Prophet’s teachings as a response to serious contemporary human challenges.


In a message to the culture ministers of Islamic countries marking the 1,500th anniversary of the birth of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), Salehi said the occasion provided an opportunity to reflect on the shared heritage of the Islamic world and expand cultural cooperation among Muslim nations.
​
He stressed that revisiting and presenting the life and teachings of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) is a response to a pressing need in the contemporary world, where parts of the globe are facing war, violence, extremism, injustice and the suffering of innocent people.
​
“The blessed name of Prophet Muhammad Mustafa, throughout Islamic history and civilization, has always been a source of unity among hearts and nations, transcending geographical, linguistic and ethnic boundaries,” Salehi said.
​
Muslims across a wide range of cultures and lands, inspired by the Prophet’s message and conduct, have created a shared heritage of faith, morality, knowledge, justice and human dignity, he added.
​
According to Salehi, this heritage has played a role in shaping Islamic culture and civilization for centuries and continues to remain alive and inspiring in the hearts and minds of Muslim societies.
​
Against this backdrop, he described the 1,500th anniversary of the Prophet’s birth as a unique opportunity to reflect anew on the shared heritage and its inherent potential.
​
“In a world where distances and differences sometimes overshadow common human values, remembrance of and devotion to the Messenger of Islam can once again return Muslim nations to a source in which mercy, morality, justice and respect for human beings are the fundamental pillars of individual and social life,” he said.
​
Salehi said that revisiting and presenting the Prophet’s life and conduct was not merely an act of respect for a historical heritage.
​
“It is a response to a pressing need in the contemporary world,” he said.
​
“Prophet Muhammad was a messenger of mercy to all worlds, and revisiting this truth can make an effective contribution to strengthening a culture of dialogue, coexistence and mutual respect among nations and cultures.”
​
Turning to the role of cultural institutions, Salehi said cultural bodies in Islamic countries carried an important and shared responsibility.
​
“Culture and art are among the few languages capable of carrying profound religious and human concepts beyond official borders and speaking to new generations,” he said.
​
He said the occasion should pave the way for expanded cultural cooperation among Islamic countries, the exchange of cultural and artistic experiences and works, and the development of joint programs and initiatives.
​
Such efforts, he added, could introduce the world to the true image of Islam and the human and civilizational message of Prophet Muhammad.
​
Salehi extended his congratulations to his counterparts, the culture ministers of Islamic countries, as well as the cultural and artistic community and Muslims around the world.
​
He expressed hope that the occasion would not only renew Muslims’ spiritual bond with the conduct of the Prophet of Mercy but also mark the beginning of a new chapter of understanding, interaction and cultural cooperation in the Islamic world.
