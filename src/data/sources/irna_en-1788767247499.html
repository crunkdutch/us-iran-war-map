🎙 Iran embassy in Beirut clarifies official position on Lebanon developments

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Embassy in Beirut has stressed that the official positions of the Islamic Republic and the embassy are communicated only through authorized institutions and representatives.


In a statement issued Sunday, the embassy reacted to reports by some Lebanese media outlets concerning developments in southern Lebanon that were attributed to Iran’s official position.
​
The embassy said comments by individuals cited in Lebanese media as representing Iran’s stance should be regarded solely as their personal views.
​
The embassy emphasized that such statements do not constitute official positions of the Islamic Republic of Iran or its diplomatic mission in Beirut.
​
It urged media outlets to rely on authorized Iranian sources when reporting Tehran’s positions on regional developments.

📄 Iran Expo 2026 to bring together 1,500 foreign traders, business representatives

📌 Tehran, IRNA – More than 1,500 foreign traders are expected to attend the eighth Iran Expo, as organizers seek to expand commercial ties and connect Iranian exporters with target markets.


Ali Tolouei, executive director of the exhibition, said on Saturday that the event will be held from November 27 to December 1, 2026, across all halls of the Tehran International Exhibition Center.
​
He said exporters across sectors will participate, with coordination established with specialized associations, the Iran Chamber of Commerce, Tehran Chamber of Commerce, and provincial export bodies to identify target markets and invite relevant economic players.
​
Tolouei added that arrangements have also been made with major economic groupings, including the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), BRICS, and the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU).
​
A new B2B Management System is another key feature of this edition. Scheduled to be unveiled in September, the platform will organize business meetings between Iranian companies and foreign traders according to product categories, business fields and target markets.
​
Major exhibition sectors will include oil and petrochemicals, construction, medical products, cosmetics, textiles, industrial equipment, automobiles and spare parts, and food.
​
Organizers have also coordinated hotels, foreign affairs services, interpreters and protocol arrangements for international guests.

🎙 US military cannot call its ‘barbaric crimes’ against civilians a ‘mistake’: Iran’s OIC mission

📌 Tehran, IRNA –Iran’s mission to the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) has denounced the US military for proudly acknowledging yet another “cowardly and illegal attack” on an Iranian oil tanker, while evading responsibility for the killing of civilians.


The Iranian mission reacted on Sunday to a statement issued by the US Central Command (CENTCOM) claiming “precision and professionalism” of American forces in the attack on the Iranian vessel.
​
The message mocked CENTCOM for describing its act of aggression as “precision and professionalism,” noting that “a cowardly attack” on a commercial oil tanker was not something to be proud of or praise.
​
“So how can attacks on an elementary school in Minab, a sports club in Lamerd, a wedding ceremony in Kuhestak, and other civilian sites be dismissed merely as a ‘mistake’ or ‘targeting error’?” the OIC mission deplored the US military for evading responsibility for these attacks.
​
It noted that the CENTCOM statement was a clear acknowledgment of “all the barbaric crimes committed by the US terrorist forces during the aggressive and illegal war against Iran.”
​
You cannot invoke “precision” whenever it suits you and call it a “mistake” when civilians pay the price; the Iranian mission further reminded the United States and its military in the West Asia region.

⚡️ Mexico’s ambassador welcomes joint trade cooperation committee with Iran

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Mexico’s ambassador to Tehran, Guillermo Puente Ordorica, has welcomed the formation of a joint Iran-Mexico committee to expand trade relations, identifying health tourism, the automotive industry and auto parts manufacturing as potential areas of cooperation.


Speaking at a meeting with Samad Hassanzadeh, president of the Iran Chamber of Commerce, Industries, Mines and Agriculture, Guillermo Puente Ordorica said the committee could help expand trade and investment ties between the two countries.
​
“I welcome the idea of forming a joint committee and believe this method will have a positive effect on expanding relations,” he said.
​
The Mexican ambassador said Mexico’s new government has developed an economic program for cooperation with countries around the world, incorporating various factors to expand trade relations.
​
Puente Ordorica also stressed the need for the Iran Chamber of Commerce to establish relations with Mexican private companies within the agreed framework and welcomed the exchange of trade delegations between the two countries.
​
He identified health tourism as an area with potential for cooperation, while noting Mexico’s capabilities in automobiles, auto parts and heavy industries.
​
He proposed first establishing the joint committee, followed by exchanges of trade delegations, allowing the two sides to become familiar with each other’s capabilities before taking further steps.
​
Hassanzadeh also stressed the importance of exchanging trade delegations to help economic actors become more familiar with the capabilities of each country, as well as the need to establish a joint committee to improve coordination in expanding trade and investment relations.
​
He also highlighted the importance of exchanging economic information through the joint committee, saying the mechanism could help the two countries benefit from their respective relations with other countries.
​
Hassanzadeh said Iran is ready to cooperate with Mexico in areas including new agricultural technologies, artificial intelligence, technical and engineering services, medical equipment and large-scale projects.
​
He also highlighted export opportunities in petrochemicals, minerals, agricultural products, industrial equipment, technical and engineering services, tourism and health tourism, as well as potential for joint investment.
​
He expressed hope that resolving transport and financial transfer obstacles would help create better conditions for expanding economic ties between Iran and Mexico.
