The manuscript was calligraphed by the renowned Persian master Mir Ali Heravi in 939 AH (1532-33 CE) and is considered a valuable example of Islamic calligraphy and devotional literature. The work is preserved within the manuscript collections of the Astan Quds Razavi Library, one of the most important repositories of Islamic manuscripts in the Muslim world.
