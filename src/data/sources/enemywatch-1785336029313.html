🇸🇦 + 🏴‍☠️ : 🇮🇶 | Saudi Arabia joins America in attacks across Iraq:

As anticipated earlier, the accusations repeated by the Saudi Ministry against the Iraqi Resistance for allegedly targeting Saudi territory now appear to have served as a pretext for Saudi Arabia to join military operations alongside the United States in Iraq. Over the past few hours, multiple airstrikes reportedly targeted PMF positions and other sites across several Iraqi governorates. Initial reports indicate the martyrdom of at least seven PMF members, primarily from the 30th PMF Brigade in the Nineveh Plains, while one civilian was reportedly killed and six others injured in the attack on the Al-Deir area of Basra.

• Nineveh Governorate / Nineveh Plains / Bartella: Targeted 9 times. Reportedly struck repeatedly, including six airstrikes against the 30th PMF Brigade (4th Regiment, Sheikh Hassan Battalion). Fires and heavy smoke were reported, and at least 7 PMF fighters were reported martyred.

• Diyala Governorate: Targeted 6 times. Camp Ashraf, the Martyr Abu Muntadhar al-Hamidawi Camp, a prayer hall, a water purification plant, and the Saadiya al-Jabal area were reportedly struck.

• Wasit Governorate (Suwaira): Targeted 4 times. A PMF site near Suwaira was reportedly hit, with surveillance footage said to show the strike.

• Basra Governorate: Targeted 4 times. The PMF Operations Command headquarters and the Al-Deir area were reportedly struck. Initial reports indicate one person was killed and six others were injured in the Al-Deir attack.

• Karbala Governorate: Targeted 3 times. Strikes were reported north of Karbala, including the Husseiniya area, where an Arbaeen Mowkeb (pilgrim service guesthouse) was reportedly targeted.

• Kirkuk Governorate (Al-Zab/Dibis): Targeted 3 times. Airstrikes and explosions were reported, with three PMF fighters reportedly wounded in Dibis.

• Saladin Governorate (Amirli): Targeted 2 times. The 52nd PMF Brigade was reportedly struck.

• Iraqi sources also reported that a mosque and a water purification facility inside the Martyr Abu Muntadhar al-Hamidawi Camp in Diyala were struck.

• Baghdad Governorate (Madain): Targeted 1 time moments ago. Reports indicate an attack on the Madain area of the Iraqi capital, with columns of smoke seen rising from the targeted location.

• Currently, the Nineveh Plains are under attack.
