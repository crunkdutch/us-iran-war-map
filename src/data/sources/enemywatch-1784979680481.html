🇮🇷: 🇮🇷 | The millions funeral procession of Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei has concluded in Tehran after more than eleven hours. The procession brought together an estimated 15 million participants, while several million others were unable to reach the vehicle carrying the sacred coffins due to the extraordinary congestion and the overwhelming size of the crowds.

The total participation may have reached around 20 million people throughout the day, although the final official figures will be confirmed later. The funeral procession advanced only slowly, as mourners continuously surrounded the sacred coffins, with many waiting for hours to bid their final farewell. Despite the immense crowds, almost everyone who remained near the procession was eventually able to approach the sacred coffins.

The scale of the gathering transformed much of Tehran into a single mourning procession, with people filling streets, intersections, rooftops, bridges, and surrounding avenues. Thousands continued to join the procession long after it had begun, while countless others lined the route simply to witness the passing of the sacred coffins.
