Yemen

The Yemeni Armed Forces launched an attack on Ma’rib with two ballistic missiles and two drones. There are also indications that attacks on the UAE were carried out by Ansar Allah in Yemen, although this has not yet been confirmed.

Iran

The Iranian Foreign Ministry informed two employees of the French Embassy in Tehran that they had been declared persona non grata and banned from entering Iran. France’s Foreign Minister said that two Iranian diplomats would be expelled in the coming days.

The Iranian Armed Forces reportedly carried out an operation against the cargo ship Minoan Dignity, owned by the Greek company Modion Maritime and operating under the Liberian flag since 2004. One crew member was reportedly killed and several others injured. The vessel was reportedly rendered completely inoperable after being struck by a missile, while two attempts by U.S. aircraft to evacuate the trapped crew members were reportedly unsuccessful. Assistance was requested from the Omani Coast Guard.

According to the UK Maritime Trade Operations center, the Iranian Armed Forces also struck another vessel the previous night. The vessel was reportedly attempting to pass through the Oman Corridor south of the Strait of Hormuz with support from U.S. military aircraft, allegedly in violation of the recent understanding. The attack reportedly severely damaged the vessel’s engine room and killed one crew member. Omani naval forces were reportedly attempting to assist and evacuate the crew.

Bab al-Mandab - Yemen

Strait of Hormuz - Iran
