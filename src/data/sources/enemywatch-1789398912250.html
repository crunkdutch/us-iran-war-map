🇾🇪 | What is the situation in Yemen now?

The war is ongoing in more than seven locations, although the two main fronts are in the governorates of Taiz and Al-Jawf. In Taiz, where the Saudi proxy-Yemeni Presidential Leadership Council made good advances on the first day of the war, the tide has now largely turned. Ansar Allah has retaken the areas it had lost, and fighting is currently underway in the suburbs of the port of Mocha in this governorate, where Ansar Allah forces intend to enter the city.

One of the reasons for Ansar Allah’s victories on this front is the support of the tribes in this region for Ansar Allah. They were previously alongside the UAE-backed Southern Transitional Government, which was destroyed by the Saudi proxy-Presidential Leadership Council last March. For this reason, they are not pleased with the Presidential Leadership Council, and many tribes have decided to fight for Ansar Allah. It must be said that despite living in the 21st century, Yemen is still governed by tribalism. If you are looking for soldiers there, you must persuade the tribal chiefs to send their men to war.

On the Al-Jawf front, however, since Monday, the Saudi proxy-Yemeni Presidential Leadership Council has made advances in this governorate, including the capture of Labnat. The tribes in that region mostly hold extremist Sunni Wahabi beliefs and support the Saudis and the Presidential Leadership Council. Also, the geography of this governorate facilitates superior air operations.

According to the latest reports, the Saudi proxy-Presidential Leadership Council intends to attack Al-Hazm, the capital of Al-Jawf governorate. Ansar Allah has also sent its best forces to this area, and they seem to intend to bring the war to the streets of Al-Hazm and use urban ambushes by abandoning open positions, which are difficult, if not impossible, to defend against air superiority. In urban areas, the effectiveness of air superiority is reduced because effective identification of enemy positions is difficult.

This battle plan seems tactically correct for Ansar Allah, and if they gather all their forces in the region to defend Al-Hazm, the probability of victory will increase, and it may even give Ansar Allah the capability for a counterattack. Defending Al-Hazm is also very important because if the enemy passes Al-Hazm, Ansar Allah’s next stronghold is the city of Sanaa itself.
