Such a heavy weapons load, which is equivalent to ships of 6,000 tons and above, has been achieved by sacrificing a large part of the fuel tanks and storage facilities, which means that these ships have almost no oceanic operational capability. It should be noted that almost all of the Israeli Navy's assets, including submarines and ships, are German-made.
In fact, Israel did not need an oceanic navy. Its only threat was its Egyptian and Syrian neighbors with Soviet-made Osa missile boats, which were easy prey for the Israeli air and naval forces, just as these boats in the service of the Ba'athist Iraqi Navy were destroyed in Operation Morvarid by Iranian air and naval forces with heavier Alvand frigates and Phantom fighters.

For this reason, they have ordered second-hand P-8 Poseidon maritime patrol aircraft and SH-60 anti-submarine helicopters from the United States and are scheduled to receive newer German submarines.
