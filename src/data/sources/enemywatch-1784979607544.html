In the Name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful

Peace be upon you, O Avenger of Allah and the son of His Avenger, the forsaken one whose blood remains unavenged. Peace be upon you, and upon your grandfather, your father, your mother, your brother, and the infallible ones from among your descendants.

Peace be upon the Imam whose life giving uprising carried the resounding echo of the Prophetic mission into the farthest depths of history, from which emerged the Islamic Revolution in Iran, a revolution that was Husayni from its very foundation and was built and elevated upon the slogan and path of Imam Husayn (A). The martyr of Iran was likewise raised upon this path. He was Husayni, he thought in the spirit of Husayn, he acted in the spirit of Husayn, he struggled and resisted in the spirit of Husayn, he lived in the spirit of Husayn, and he offered his blood in the spirit of Husayn for the sake of the school of Imam Husayn, thereby attaining martyrdom.

Among the followers of Husayn are men whose blood, when unjustly shed for the sake of Imam Husayn and for his school and path (peace be upon him), causes the Islamic Ummah to rise, joining that time with Ashura and that place with Karbala. Today, that very Husayni epic has awakened our people and bestowed a new manifestation upon the school of the great Imam Khomeini and the martyred Imam Khamenei. This is the very epic that breathes life. It is the echo of the oppressed cry of Imam Husayn (peace be upon him), the call of, "Is there anyone to help me?" which resounds first in Iran, then in Iraq and throughout the rest of the countries, shaking falsehood to its foundations.

On this occasion, I must also express my deepest gratitude and appreciation to the tens of millions of people who made their astonishing, enemy breaking, and historic presence known throughout the cities and villages of Iran and Iraq, especially in Tehran, Qom, Najaf, Karbala, and Mashhad.

Our people call for vengeance for the blood of Imam Husayn (A). For many years this great nation has offered its sons in sacrifice for the sake of Imam Husayn (A), in the war against the enemies of Husayn and against those hostile to Husayni honor. Today as well, it demands vengeance for his blood and for the blood of the Husaynis of this age.

Now I address our martyred Imam and say:

O oppressed martyr, O noble oppressed one, O righteous servant of Allah, as we bid farewell to your body with tearful eyes and broken hearts, we pledge to preserve your school, to steadfastly follow the straight path that you drew for us, never to fear the hardships of this path, and to bind our hearts, as you did, to the divine glad tidings and promises.

We also pledge that we shall avenge your pure blood and the blood of all the martyrs of these two wars from the disgraceful criminal murderers. This vengeance is the demand of our people, and it will inevitably be fulfilled. Those criminals, whose names are fully known from the first to the last, will carry with them to their graves the wish that they might die peacefully in their beds. They must know that this matter does not depend upon my existence or upon the existence of any other officials. Whether we remain or not, this promise will be fulfilled, and soon the free people of the world will each carry out a portion of this divine mission.

O martyred father of the nation, blessed are you for finally tasting the sweetness of martyrdom that you had long desired throughout your life. Blessed are you for being clothed in the garment of martyrdom with a body bearing the marks of your mother, al Zahra al Tahira, your grandfather Aba Abdillah al Husayn, and Abal Fadl al Abbas (peace be upon them all). And to your oppressed companions who were struck by the enemy in a treacherous attack and attained martyrdom, glad tidings to you, for you are now guests of that Master whose mercy and kindness you perhaps experienced time and again. That Sayyid who is the gate of Allah's mercy for all, especially for the people of these lands, is now your host, and his secure presence has become your dwelling place.

O exalted Master, O noble one, O compassionate Imam, O Aba al Hasan al Ridha al Murtadha, upon you be the finest blessings of Allah. Into this sacred soil is now laid the torn body of one of the servants of your holy presence and of the Pure Household, after years of tireless effort, struggle, and continuous jihad. Alongside him are the bodies of martyrs from his own family, each reminding us of one of the martyrs of the plains of Karbala. Here they shall rest until the day when, by the command of Allah, the Remaining Proof of Allah (may Allah hasten his noble reappearance) emerges like the radiant sun from behind the clouds of occultation, spreading the light of divine mercy across the inhabitants of the earth. On that day, which we pray is very near, he, may Allah hasten his reappearance, will be accompanied by stars among the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous. We hope that our martyred Sayyid will be among them, once again portraying scenes filled with jihad and faithfulness to the covenant of "Am I not your Lord?" Perhaps these very companions will accompany him on that day as well.

O my compassionate Master, we entrust to you our Sayyid who gave everything he possessed for your sake, together with his martyred companions, so that they may be honored by your kindness and care. Just as they enjoyed the blessings of your grace during their worldly lives, may they from this moment onward receive it in a far more complete and exalted manner.

In conclusion, we once again offer our condolences to our Master, the Remaining Proof of Allah (may Allah hasten his noble reappearance). We pray to that Merciful Master that he include the Chief Martyr of Iran, his martyred companions, and all the martyrs within his blessed supplications, that he ask the Almighty (Glorious and Exalted is He) to grant them the highest ranks, to bestow patience and reward upon their families, and to grant the oppressed Iranian people a decisive and near victory, Allah willing.

- Sayyid Mujtaba Husayni Khamenei
