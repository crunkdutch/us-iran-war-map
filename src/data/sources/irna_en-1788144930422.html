🔊 Iran’s diplomacy supports national interests alongside military front: Ex-CBI governor

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry, under the administration of President Masoud Pezeshkian, has kept the country’s diplomatic front active under exceptionally difficult conditions, while building an effective negotiation network and helping shape the Islamabad memorandum of understanding between Tehran and Washington, according to former Central Bank of Iran Governor Mohammad-Hossein Adeli.


In an exclusive Persian-language article for IRNA, published on Sunday that marked the last say of the National Government Week, Adeli said the Foreign Ministry had played a significant role alongside the military front in protecting Iran’s national interests and security during a period marked by intense military and political tensions.
​
He said maintaining diplomacy during wartime was particularly difficult, as negotiations faced pressure at home and abroad. Despite these challenges, Iran’s diplomatic apparatus continued consultations, meetings and negotiations with various parties before, during and after recent military confrontations, the expert noted.
​
Adeli also highlighted the establishment of an active negotiation network through Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi’s contacts with counterparts in countries including Oman, Pakistan, Turkey, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, and other regional states, as well as Iran’s allies and members of the Axis of Resistance.
​
He said maintaining these channels of communication had helped preserve opportunities for dialogue and could contribute to a path toward peace and Iran’s strategic objectives.
​
Adeli further described the Islamabad memorandum as a significant diplomatic achievement, arguing that its provisions reflected Iran’s position in negotiations with its adversaries.
​
He said the document could serve as a basis for future negotiations and praised the Foreign Ministry for helping create the conditions that led to the agreement.
​
Overall, Adeli said, Iran’s diplomacy had remained active despite severe political and military pressures.

🎙 Expediency Council chief calls for preserving national unity

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Head of Iran’s Expediency Council Ayatollah Sadegh Amoli Larijani has called for preserving national unity and cohesion to overcome challenges and achieve national progress.


In a message on Sunday marking the birth anniversary of the Prophet Mohammad (PBUH), Larijani said unity and cohesion, grounded in the teachings of the Quran and the tradition of the Prophet, are both a rational and religious duty for overcoming obstacles and reaching the heights of honor and greatness.
​
He also referred to a message issued by Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei, saying that the message will protect Iran against plots hatched by the global arrogance.
​
In his message, the Leader described the United States and the Israeli regime as “sworn enemies” of Muslim unity, stressing the need for greater solidarity and cooperation across the Islamic world.

🎙 President Pezeshkian to visit Kyrgyzstan on Monday

📌 Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian will travel to Kyrgyzstan on Monday, his office’s Public Relations says.


Pezeshkian will participate in a two-day Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit in Bishkek, the capital of Kyrgyzstan.
​
Habibollah Abbasi, head of the presidential Public Relations, told IRNA that Pezeshkian will deliver a speech at the SCO summit and engage in bilateral meetings with leaders and senior officials from SCO member and partner countries.
​
Pezeshkian will also attend the official opening ceremony of the sixth World Nomad Games, scheduled for August 31 to September 6, 2026, in Kyrgyzstan.
​

🎙 Mexican lawmaker pays tribute to Iranian people’s resistance

📌 Tehran, IRNA – A member of Mexico’s Chamber of Deputies has paid tribute to the resistance of the Iranian people, calling for global solidarity among resistance nations.


A ceremony marking the launch of the second edition of the book “Fidel in the Mexican imagination” was held at Mexico’s Chamber of Deputies, attended by ambassadors and diplomats from Iran, Nicaragua, Venezuela, China, Palestine and Vietnam, as well as cultural figures.
​
María Magdalena Rosales Cruz, the book’s author and a lawmaker from the ruling Morena party, paid tribute to Iran and nations engaged in struggle, highlighting Fidel Castro’s legacy of defending resistance, independence, national sovereignty and the right of nations to self-determination, while stressing the need to combat poverty, inequality and domination.
​
The speakers concluded by emphasizing solidarity among nations, the defense of national independence and sovereignty, and preservation of the legacy of liberation movements.
