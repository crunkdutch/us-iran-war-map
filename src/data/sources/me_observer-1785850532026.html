⚡️ Turkish Aerospace Industries announced that the KAAN fighter jet successfully taxied using its own engine. This phase involves the final engine and airframe tests before takeoff and limited-speed flight tests. Subsequent phases will include supersonic speed and weapons testing.

It appears that this dear aircraft, which resembles the F-22 from the front, the F-35 from above, and the Su-57 from the rear, has reached a good level of maturity and could enter service with the Turkish Air Force and its allies in the future.

There was also talk of a naval version, but now, with Turkey on the path to rejoining the F-35 project, the need for such a version has become ambiguous.

The initial version of the KAAN fighter uses the General Electric F-110 engine, which is also installed on the F-16, providing approximately 29,000 pounds of thrust with afterburner. Turkey is also developing a domestic engine with 40,000 pounds of thrust in afterburner mode.

It can be said that this fighter has the potential to eventually become a numerous and more affordable complement to the F-35 in Turkey as it matures. Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, and the UAE are potential customers.
