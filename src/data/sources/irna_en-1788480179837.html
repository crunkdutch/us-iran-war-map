​
Asked about the ministry’s “approach and view regarding the current status of negotiations with the United States,” Baqaei said that the ministry assesses developments and expresses its views based on the country’s interests and national considerations, and ultimately will act in accordance with whatever is decided by the competent authorities.
​
Baqaei acknowledged that the US officials are confused in their approaches and statements, but said this would not cause Iran to neglect its own capabilities.
​
“We will use the tools of diplomacy wherever necessary to explain our positions and secure the rights of the Iranian nation,” he said.
​
Baqaei added that Pakistan and several other countries are continuing efforts to de-escalate tensions and end the war, while Pakistan’s military chief, Field Marshal Asim Munir, visited Iran as part of Islamabad’s role as a friendly and neighboring country and presented his views. He stressed that Iran’s constant criterion is safeguarding its national interests.
​
The spokesperson said that the US attack was not something new for Iran, noting that Washington has repeatedly carried out acts of aggression under various pretexts and received the necessary response.
​
He further noted that these actions reflect US ill intent and confusion, stemming from its excessive demands and its mistaken belief that negotiations mean dictating terms—an approach that will not work with Iran. “We must remain fully vigilant against enemy moves, and any aggression will be met with a response,” he added.

He also warned the international community against inaction in the face of such brutality, saying that no one will remain immune from the consequences, as the ongoing silence will only embolden the perpetrators.
​
“Still, those governments and international institutions that remain silent in the face of such barbarism, or seek to justify such acts, must understand that their silence is not neutrality. When unlawful attacks and brutal crimes are whitewashed for political convenience, the line between condemning a crime and normalizing it disappears. Those who remain silent today will not be immune to its consequences tomorrow. Turning a blind eye to injustice does not contain it; it only emboldens the perpetrators,” said the spokesperson.
