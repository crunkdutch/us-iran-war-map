🇮🇷/🏴| How can a pious reciter and lover of the Prophet and his family be killed, with his heart reportedly taken out while alive? Is this simply a crime, or does it reveal something far more disturbing? (1/3)

Six suspects have been arrested in connection with the martyrdom of Iranian religious reciter Hamidreza Rajabzadeh.

Hamidreza Rajabzadeh was a religious reciter who performed at the Hussayniyah of Imam Khomeini of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei. He was a simple religious man who focused on ethics, religious guidance, and the welfare of others.

A few days ago, a video circulated widely on anti-Islamic Republic channels, reportedly showing the perpetrators brutally attacking and martyring Rajabzadeh, desecrating his body, taking his heart out and subsequently setting it on fire.

According to the police investigation, Rajabzadeh had been communicating with a woman on social media and had advised her regarding hijab and religious conduct. Investigators say that the woman subsequently lured him to a secluded location after arranging an in-person meeting. According to the investigation, she first incapacitated him with poisoned food, after which several men allegedly attacked and martyred him.

Investigators further stated that the group had planned to record the incident and send the footage to the Mojahedin-e Khalq Organization (MEK), also known as the People’s Mojahedin Organization of Iran (PMOI), in exchange for money reportedly amounting to several thousand dollars.

Following the arrest of the main suspects in the Hamidreza Rajabzadeh case, investigators from the Criminal Investigation Department are continuing their inquiries to determine the motive and circumstances surrounding the incident.

According to the Police Spokesperson’s Office, the female suspect in the case, who was reportedly attempting to flee the country, was arrested in one of the northern cities and transferred to a police facility.

The Police Spokesperson’s Office stated that during the initial interrogations, the suspects confessed to their involvement in the killing, while further investigations into the various dimensions of the case remain underway.

Police also announced that, through the efforts of technical and specialist teams from the Tehran Police Criminal Investigation Department, the main suspect in the case was identified and arrested.

According to the Law Enforcement Command of the Islamic Republic of Iran, four other suspects had also been arrested during the preceding 48 hours, bringing the total number of arrested suspects to five at that stage of the investigation. Police later announced the arrest of the female suspect who had been attempting to flee, bringing the number of arrested suspects to six.

The head of the Tehran Criminal Prosecutor’s Office also announced that the body of Hamidreza Rajabzadeh had been discovered in the vicinity of Tehran and transferred to the Forensic Medicine Organization.

According to the report, Rajabzadeh had been missing for approximately 15 days. Several days ago, a video showing his severely injured body was published on an anti-government channel and subsequently circulated widely across social media.

His disappearance had previously been reported, with reports stating that the well-known religious reciter had not returned home after leaving his residence. After a case was opened at the Tehran Criminal Prosecutor’s Office, the investigation quickly entered a new phase.

Investigators from the Fourth Department of the Tehran Police Criminal Investigation Department, acting under orders from the criminal prosecutor, began investigating the circumstances surrounding the missing man and eventually identified a young female blogger and several young men as persons of interest.

No information concerning his whereabouts was initially obtained.
