The AGM-154 JSOW is a long-range precision-guided stand-off weapon designed to strike targets from a significant distance. The available evidence is described as being most consistent with an AGM-154 JSOW, although a definitive distinction between the C and C-1 variants cannot be made without identifying a part number, CAGE code, or datalink-specific components.
