🖥 Iran reaffirms China ties, defends diplomacy, warns Europe

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmail Baqaei has said the Islamic Republic maintains very good and friendly relations with China, stressing that contacts between the two countries continue at various levels.


Opening his weekly press conference in Tehran on Monday, Baqaei said the upcoming BRICS summit would provide another opportunity for the Islamic Republic of Iran to hold talks with China and other member states. Baqaei noted that many countries are members of both BRICS and the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), adding that the absence of a meeting at one particular gathering should not be considered significant.
​
Negotiation is neither absolutely good nor bad, Baqaei said, describing diplomacy as a tool for advancing national interests and safeguarding national security. He added that Iran’s Foreign Ministry would continue to follow decisions made by relevant authorities, including the Supreme National Security Council.
​
Baqaei also criticized efforts by the United States, France, Britain, and Germany for their efforts to pass a resolution at the International Atomic Energy Agency’s Board of Governors that could pave the way for referring Iran’s nuclear issue to the UN Security Council.
​
He argued that such an initiative was legally contradictory, noting that the European countries and the United States had previously claimed that the so-called snapback mechanism had restored earlier UN Security Council resolutions.
​
Reintroducing Iran’s nuclear issue to the council, he said, undermines that claim and supports the Iranian, Chinese, and Russian position that snapback was unlawful and inapplicable.
​
The spokesman further criticized the European Trio (Britain, France, and Germany) for undermining their own credibility by repeating a strategy that had previously failed to produce results and had instead complicated the nuclear issue.
​
Baqaei also blamed US and Israeli attacks on Iranian nuclear facilities for preventing the IAEA from accessing certain sites. He said it was contradictory to create obstacles to inspections and then criticize Iran over the resulting lack of access.
​
He warned that any reckless action by Washington and the three European countries would prompt Iran to take necessary measures, urging Europe to learn from the Iranian proverb: It is wrong to test the already tested.
​
Baqaei referred to developments in Iran and the West Asia region. He condemned a US attack on Sirik and offered condolences to the families of those killed.
​
He said that the United States has been maintaining military and economic pressure on Iran for six months. The spokesperson argued that Washington’s economic campaign targets not only Iran but also international trade and the UN Charter.
​
Addressing the situation in Palestine, Baqaei condemned Israeli attacks in Gaza and the West Bank. He described the deaths of Palestinian civilians, including children, as war crimes and genocide. He said the discovery of bodies beneath the rubble underscores the human cost of the conflict and warned that such violations would leave a stain on human history.
​
Responding to questions about the absence of a bilateral meeting between the Iranian and Chinese presidents during the SCO summit, Baqaei said President Masoud Pezeshkian held meetings with leaders from several countries, while a brief meeting also took place with China’s premier.

📡 Baqaei: Iran’s actions in Strait of Hormuz purely defensive

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has said Iran’s actions in the Strait of Hormuz are quite defensive and are being carried out in response to the ongoing military aggression of the US and the Israeli regime.


Speaking to reporters on Monday, Baqaei said that “the war of tankers” may not be an accurate term to describe what we are witnessing in the region.
​
Since February 28, Iran has been countering the United States and the Zionist regime’s military aggression, and from that point onward, the Strait of Hormuz and the Persian Gulf have been rendered insecure, not by any action of Iran, but as a direct result of US—Israeli belligerence, he said.
​
The situation has continued ever since, taking different forms, including a naval blockade, he added.
​
It was the United States that first carried out attacks on vessels, both within Iranian regional waters and on the high seas, he reiterated.
​
Attacks on commercial vessels are contrary to international law and constitute a terrorist act, he stated.
​
The US Secretary of Defense’s taking pride in this operation effectively amounts to an acknowledgment of having committed a war crime against commercial vessels, Baqaei said.
​
As long as the US economic war and naval blockade continue, Iran retains full legal and operational rights to self-defense, he stressed.
​
In response to a question regarding threats by Israeli officials against regional countries and their hostile rhetoric, Baqaei said the Zionist regime harbors ambitions of dominating the entire region and humiliating all regional countries.
​
Such overt threats by the regime’s officials against regional countries are not new, he emphasized.
​
They have previously spoken of the dream of a “Greater Israel” and have also threatened Turkey in various ways, he said.
​
This demonstrates the Zionist regime’s expansionist mindset and should remind regional countries of the true danger to regional peace and security, he noted.
​
Regional countries need to be careful that other concerns do not embolden the main enemy of peace and security, he stressed.

🔊 Iran dismantles terrorist cells in southeast, arrests nine

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Ministry of Intelligence has announced that three operational cells belonging to a Takfiri terrorist group had been disbanded in the country’s southeast, with nine terrorists arrested and three others killed during clashes with security forces.


According to a statement issued by the ministry on Monday, its forces in Sistan and Baluchestan Province identified and disrupted the three organized cells as they were entering Iran, before they could carry out any terrorist or sabotage operations.
​
The ministry said the suspects had received military, bomb-making and assassination training abroad and were planning to create insecurity in the southern parts of the province. A significant quantity of weapons and military ammunition was seized from those arrested.
​
The statement also said intelligence forces discovered and confiscated a hidden cache containing light and medium-heavy weapons that had been smuggled into the country.
​
The ministry thanked citizens for providing information and urged them to report suspicious activities through its official channels.
