I’ll give you a TL;DR but make sure to read the article on Al Jazeera yourself.

The analysis writes about Iran’s strategic "blinding" campaign aimed at dismantling the United States' integrated missile-defense network across West Asia.

By shifting from mixed-drone tactics to concentrated ballistic missile strikes, Iran has systematically targeted the sophisticated AN/FPS-132 and AN/TPY-2 radar systems in Qatar, Jordan, the UAE, and Saudi Arabia.

These high-value sensors, costing upwards of $500 million each, serve as the "eyes" of the defense architecture, providing the early detection and tracking necessary for THAAD, Patriot, and Aegis systems to intercept attacks.

By disabling these specific assets, Iran seeks to degrade regional detection capabilities, shorten the reaction window for US/Israel, and significantly increase the probability that its ballistic arsenal can penetrate the multi-layered defenses protecting US assets and Israeli territory.
