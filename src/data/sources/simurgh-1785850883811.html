🇮🇷 The Sunni head Cleric in Iran: Iran-U.S. Interests Should Not Be Tied to Israel and Lebanon

Full statement:
The leading Sunni cleric in Zahedan, Iran, Molavi Abdolhamid, called for giving priority to Iran's national interests and improving the living conditions of Iranians in any negotiations between Tehran and Washington.
During his Friday sermon, he said that Iran is facing "numerous problems, particularly in the areas of the economy and people's livelihoods," stressing that "the national interests of Iran and improving the lives of its people should be the foremost priority in all negotiations, taking precedence over the interests of any other country or people."
Molavi Abdolhamid welcomed the resumption of negotiations between Iran and the United States, adding, "I do not believe it is right for the national interests of the United States and Iran to be tied to Israel and Lebanon."
He added that once Tehran and Washington resolve their differences and establish diplomatic relations, the two sides would be able to "address the issues of Israel, Lebanon, and Palestine more easily through diplomacy."
In another part of his sermon, he called for an end to chanting "death to" slogans against any country or people, saying that "it is not appropriate to continue slogans that date back 47 years, because circumstances have changed."
He also called for an end to repression, the release of political prisoners, and respect for the rights of Iran's ethnic and religious minorities. He urged the Iranian authorities to uphold the rights and freedoms guaranteed by Islam, international law, and the Iranian Constitution.
