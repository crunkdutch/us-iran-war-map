⚡️ Occupation postpones wide-scale attack on fourth camp in West Bank due to troop shortage.

Nablus - The Hebrew newspaper Yedioth Ahronoth revealed that the Zionist military establishment decided to postpone the implementation of a wide-scale military operation against a fourth camp in the West Bank, likely to be Balata Camp east of Nablus, due to a shortage of troops and resources and the army's preoccupation with other fronts.

According to the newspaper, the occupation army considers its previous operations in Jenin, Tulkarm, and Nur Shams camps "successful" and is studying applying the same model in other camps by storming the camp, evacuating its residents, destroying infrastructure, and then maintaining a military presence within it.

The newspaper quoted the head of the occupation government, Benjamin Netanyahu, as saying that instructions call for continuing to storm Palestinian towns, carry out search campaigns, confiscate weapons, and make arrests, stressing the security establishment's readiness to expand its military operations in the West Bank.

It indicated that the army is considering targeting Balata, Qalandia, and Jalazoun camps, with Balata Camp being the likely first destination for any wide-scale operation. However, implementing the plan requires large forces and additional resources, especially since the operation is not limited to storming the camp but includes remaining in it for a subsequent period.

The newspaper added that the postponement of the operation comes amid the continued preoccupation of the occupation army with the Gaza Strip and southern Lebanon fronts, in addition to the tension with Iran, which places the West Bank in a secondary position within the military establishment's priorities.

It pointed out that the statements of Zionist War Minister Yisrael Katz aim to enhance deterrence in the West Bank and reassure settlers, while plans to expand military operations remain contingent on developments in the coming stage.
