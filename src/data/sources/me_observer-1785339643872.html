🔹The First Vice President announced the Cabinet's resolution regarding the price of wheat for guilds and industry for the year 1405.
🔹Based on this resolution, the price of each kilogram of wheat for industrial uses has been set at 53,000 tomans.
🔹The government is set to withdraw from the industrial wheat market. According to this directive, the Ministry of Agriculture Jihad must transfer the process of purchasing wheat for this sector to the private sector within the next two weeks.
🔹The import of wheat required by this sector will also be carried out using the exchange center's currency.
🔹To prevent price shock, for the next three months, wheat required for the production of traditional breads, free-baking breads, and pasta products will be supplied at a subsidized rate with a 55% discount compared to the guild and industry price.
