🎙 Iran urges all countries to denounce extraterritorial application of unilateral coercive measures by US

📌 New York, IRNA – Ambassador and Deputy Permanent Representative of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the United Nations, Gholamhossein Darzi, has “called on all States to reject and denounce the extraterritorial application of unilateral coercive measures by the United States regime aimed at compelling Member States to subordinate their sovereign rights and lawful policies to those of another State.”


“The Security Council must categorically condemn and reject these unilateral coercive measures aimed at compelling sovereign States to alter their lawful economic interests and foreign relations,” Darzi said in a statement addressing the United Nations Security Council Open Debate on “The Security Council’s Role in Mitigating Global and Local Food Crises” on Tuesday.
​
The full text of Darzi’s statement is as follows:
​
Armed conflict remains a major driver of hunger and food insecurity, which cannot be addressed without confronting its root causes, including aggression, occupation, unlawful coercive measures, and the deliberate destruction of civilian infrastructure.
​
Security Council resolution 2417 (2018) condemns starvation of civilians as a method of warfare and denial of humanitarian access.
​
In Gaza, Israel’s genocidal war against the Palestinian people has involved grave violations of international humanitarian law, including the deliberate targeting of civilians and civilian infrastructure and the weaponization of hunger through starvation and systematic obstruction of humanitarian assistance.
​
Yet, despite the scale of these crimes, the Security Council has repeatedly failed to uphold its Charter responsibilities and ensure accountability, due to the political protection and obstruction by the United States.
​
The root cause of instability and disruptions to maritime trade and navigation through the Strait of Hormuz is the US-Israeli regime war of aggression against Iran, in flagrant violation of the UN Charter and international law.
​
Prior to this aggression, commercial navigation through the Strait proceeded without disruption.
​
The United States and the Israeli regime launched an unlawful war of aggression against Iran on 28 February 2026, with grave and far-reaching consequences for regional and international peace and security.
​
Their acts of aggression, in flagrant violation of the UN Charter and the peremptory norm of general international law, resulted in the assassination and martyrdom of the Supreme Leader of the Islamic Republic of Iran, the highest official of the State, together with several other senior Iranian officials, as well as the martyrdom of more than 3,400 innocent civilians, including hundreds of women and children.
​
The Islamabad MoU signed on 17 June expressly requires the United States to refrain from any threat or use of force against Iran.
​
Once again, the United States materially breached the MoU by launching further military aggression against Iran, which started on 27 June 2026, killing more than 60 civilians, injuring over 630, and deliberately targeting airports, bridges, transportation infrastructure, vessels, search-and-rescue facilities, and meteorological installations.
​
The responsibility does not rest solely with the main aggressors.
​
States that facilitate unlawful military action by providing bases, logistical or intelligence support, or access to airspace must also bear responsibility under international law, as such assistance contributes to regional instability and undermines maritime security around the Strait of Hormuz.
​
The United States regime has demonstrated utter disregard for international law.
