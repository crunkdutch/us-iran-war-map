🇮🇷 🔻🏴‍☠️ | Iranian retaliation expands across Bahrain, Kuwait and Erbil:

In the past hours, Explosions were reported in northern Kuwait, followed later by additional explosions shaking other parts of the country. Reports also emerged of explosions in Erbil, Iraq, where renewed strikes reportedly targeted bases and headquarters of Kurdish separatist terrorist groups.

Meanwhile, Bahrain came under another wave of Iranian ballistic missile and drone attacks. Videos reportedly captured the moment an Iranian missile struck its target, while additional footage showed columns of smoke rising from the vicinity of the american base in Juffair. Reports stated that armored vehicles belonging to the american military moved through Manama following the impacts. At least one ballistic missile impact was reported, although some sources suggested that a malfunctioning Patriot interceptor may also have fallen inside Bahraini territory. Later reports claimed that a Patriot interceptor struck the Al Juffair military base, followed by further explosions and the detonation of american Patriot air defense missiles over Bahrain. Additional explosions were reported across Bahrain before sources declared that the latest phase of the Iranian attack had concluded.

Reports also stated that ambulances were dispatched to the headquarters of the american Fifth Fleet after the facility was reportedly struck by Iranian missiles. According to further reports, an Iranian Fateh-110 short-range ballistic missile (in photo) directly hit the Fifth Fleet headquarters in Bahrain, with claims that there was no interception by american Patriot air defense systems.

🇮🇶 | Iraqi military deployments raise concerns over mounting pressure on the Resistance:

Hours ago, heavy Iraqi military deployments have been reported in Baghdad and the nearby city of Baqubah. Reports claim that forces from the Baghdad Operations Command stormed the headquarters of the Special Operations Command affiliated with Shaykh Akram al-Kaabi, leader of the Harakat al-Nujaba movement, in the Jurf al-Nasr area.

This reportedly comes after several warnings from Iraqi Prime Minister Ali Al-Zaidi directed at the PMF and Iraqi Resistance factions, demanding that all their weapons be handed over to the state. According to the same alleged claims, Iraqi security forces are moving to prevent the Resistance from continuing to launch drones or rockets toward the Kurdistan Region of Iraq and neighboring Persian Gulf states.

Reports also describe a large Iraqi military convoy moving through the streets of the capital, particularly in Sadr City, Jamila, and Talbiyah, with the city reportedly resembling a military barracks. An Iraqi security source claimed that, this morning, a force belonging to the Baghdad Operations Command, accompanied by members of the Second Division of the Ministry of Defense, moved toward one of the headquarters of the Islamic Resistance in the Jurf al-Naddaf area after receiving information alleging the presence of drones inside the facility. The force was reportedly unable to enter after those inside objected, and eventually withdrew from the site after some time.

The event was described as:

At approximately 5:30 PM yesterday, a joint security force consisting of elements from the Iraqi Army, Popular Mobilization Forces (PMF) Security, and the Federal Police arrived at the headquarters of the Nujaba Movement’s Special Missions Regiment in the Jurf al-Nadaf area, southeast of Baghdad, to conduct a search operation. Upon the force’s arrival, officials at the headquarters refused them entry and informed them that they must leave the premises. As the security force remained positioned outside the gate, armed personnel emerged from inside the headquarters and fired warning shots into the air, leading to the disarming of members of the approaching force. Shortly afterward, calls were received from the PMF leadership, and official orders were issued directing the withdrawal of the PMF Security and Iraqi Army units. It was later clarified that the deployment had been part of a broader security plan and was not specifically intended to search the Nujaba headquarters. Following this clarification, all security forces withdrew from the area without conducting the search operation.

Whatever the real reason may be, it is clear that american pressure on Iraq is mounting once again against the Islamic Resistance and its capabilities, which is indeed a concerning issue. We now have to wait for official statements from the Resistance factions...

🇮🇶 | Trump increases pressure on Iraqi Kurdistan as regional energy agreements deepen American influence:

According to Al Mayadeen’s correspondent in Sulaymaniyah, the Trump administration has reportedly warned officials in the Iraqi Kurdistan Region that if they refuse to participate in a war against Iran, the region’s current autonomous status could be abolished or fundamentally altered. In connection with these claims, the leader of the separatist group Kurdistan Organization of Iran reportedly stated that american intermediaries had contacted the group and that it is now seeking military assistance from america, including weapons, explosives, and advanced equipment. Iran, which has repeatedly warned Kurdish authorities against allowing such a conflict to emerge from their territory, has continued carrying out drone and missile strikes against the positions of separatist armed groups in the Kurdistan Region.

At the same time, another development has drawn attention. About a week ago, the Syrian occupation signed two memoranda of understanding with Iraq and a coalition of international companies to rehabilitate and revive the Kirkuk-Baniyas crude oil pipeline. Notably, the agreements were signed at the american Chamber of Commerce in Washington, rather than in Damascus or Baghdad. Even if international companies are involved, the fact that the agreements were concluded under Washington’s auspices raises broader questions about american influence over regional economic projects. Critics argue that Washington seeks to position itself as the indispensable gatekeeper through which major political and economic arrangements in the region must pass, and that it aspires to impose a similar framework on Iran. However, this approach has failed with Iran because Iran refuses to bow. For Tehran, dignity and sovereignty matter more than submission, and that has remained a defining feature of its policy despite sustained deadliest military, economic, and political pressure.

🇾🇪 ❌ 🇸🇦 | Yemen launches missile and drone attack on Saudi Arabia after Saudi strikes on Hodeidah port:

Reports of a missile and drone attack by Yemen targeting Saudi Arabia following Saudi airstrikes on Hodeidah port in Yemen. The reported response comes after the strikes on the strategic Red Sea port, with regional sources reporting that Saudi cities and facilities were targeted in a combined missile and drone operation by Yemeni forces.

The Saudi city of Jizan (Jazan) was reportedly targeted, with media sources reporting more than five explosions across the city. Yemeni sources reported that the industrial area of Jizan was targeted in the attack. Reports indicate that at least five ballistic missiles targeted ARAMCO oil facilities in Jizan, Saudi Arabia.

Following geolocation analysis, reports indicate that the impacts were not at the marine terminal but at the Jizan ARAMCO refinery itself, with the reported impact location near (17.2814398, 42.3685122), although the exact impact points remain undetermined. Powerful explosions were also reported in the Saudi Arabian port city of Yanbu, along with reports of explosions in Samad/Zamd and severe explosions in Dhahran, Saudi Arabia.

Following reports of air defense system failures, Saudi authorities called for restrictions on publishing footage showing casualties and explosion scenes. Regional media continue reporting explosions and developments across multiple locations in Saudi Arabia.
