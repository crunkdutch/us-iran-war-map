🇮🇷 | In the past 5+ hours, the largest Iranian response to American aggression occurred across the region: (1)

Iran launched one of its largest ballistic-missile and drone responses of the battle after America carried out fresh airstrikes against southern Iran. Ballistic missile launches were reported from Darab, Khomeyn, Yazd, Kermanshah, Lorestan, Tabriz, Arak, Karaj and the Bidganeh missile site. At least three missiles were reported launched from Khomeyn in one wave, followed by additional launches from Khomeyn and Yazd, while another massive wave was reported from across Iran, including Kermanshah, Lorestan, Tabriz, Arak and Yazd. America carried out airstrikes against Ahvaz in southwestern Iran. Iranian air defenses also engaged hostile aerial objects over southern Iran, with Iranian sources reporting the successful targeting and destruction of a hostile object over Bandar Abbas. The IRGC announced that its new aerospace air-defense system had shot down the 50th advanced American MQ-9 Reaper, with the drone reported downed over Khomeyn. The IRGC said the MQ-9 was destroyed as part of its response to the latest American attacks. Iranian state-linked media described the night’s response as one of the largest missile attacks against American bases compared with previous rounds. The latest exchange followed American strikes on Iranian targets near the Strait of Hormuz, which america said were carried out in response to Iran’s alleged attempt to deploy naval mines and earlier Iranian missile attacks on American military sites in Jordan.

Jordan became the main target of the Iranian ballistic-missile response. Muwaffaq Salti Air Base was targeted in multiple successive waves, with reports of direct impacts, interceptions and renewed explosions around the American base. Reports said two waves of missiles specifically targeted Muwaffaq Salti, while subsequent reports said the base was being struck by dozens of Iranian missiles. The base is also referred to as Al-Azraq Air Base in reports about American military operations in Jordan. Prince Hassan Air Base in eastern Jordan was also targeted by ballistic missiles. The IRGC said its attack on Prince Hassan destroyed several American drones and killed a number of pilots and technical flight personnel. Iranian statements said ballistic missiles struck the hangars housing long-range RQ-4 and MQ-9 unmanned aircraft, destroying several drones and setting several technical facilities on fire. Camp Titin, described by the IRGC as an American Marine base near the Persian Gulf of Aqaba, was also targeted by heavy ballistic missiles. Iranian sources claimed that the attack destroyed several important installations and attack helicopters and killed a large number of American personnel. Visual material was circulated claiming to show a missile impact near Camp Titin, while evacuation helicopters were reportedly seen over Jordan. Jordanian authorities, however, said that 13 Iranian ballistic missiles entered Jordanian airspace, 10 were intercepted and three landed in remote areas, while American officials said there were no U.S. casualties from the Camp Titin attack.

The Iranian response also spread into Iraq’s Kurdistan Region, occupied Palestine and Bahrain. A large wave of Iranian drones entered the airspace of the Kurdistan Region, followed by reports of multiple interceptions and impacts in Erbil which is still ongoing. A second wave of drones was subsequently reported targeting American objectives in Erbil, northern Iraq. Several explosions were reported in Erbil, while another huge explosion was reported in the Soran district. Fires also broke out at the headquarters of a Kurdish opposition group in Erbil that Iranian sources consider a terrorist organization. In occupied Palestine, footage was circulated showing Israeli interceptor missiles being launched over Eilat after Iranian missiles were launched toward sites in Jordan near the border with occupied Palestine.
