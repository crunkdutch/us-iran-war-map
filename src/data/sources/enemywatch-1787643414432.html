📢 | Regional updates | 23 August 2026

🇾🇪 Yemen

A Yemeni civilian was reportedly martyred after Saudi-affiliated mercenaries shelled the city of Damt in Al-Dhalea Governorate.

The Yemeni Ministry of Foreign Affairs condemned new American sanctions targeting Hezbollah and the Lebanese resistance, describing them as direct assistance to the Israeli enemy. The ministry said that targeting Hezbollah and the Lebanese resistance at this stage amounts to explicit support for Israel and participation in its crimes. It added that when Israel fails to achieve a decisive military victory, the United States steps in to complete the mission through sanctions and political pressure. The ministry also warned Lebanese authorities that siding with the enemy leads only to loss.

🇮🇷 Iran

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi held a phone call with Pakistan’s Army Chief, Field Marshal Asim Munir. The two discussed the latest regional developments and ways to strengthen peace, stability, and security across West Asia.

Mehdi Rahimi said Iran had received an invitation to join the Mecca trilateral agreement and that the proposal is currently under consideration.

Rahimi also stated that negotiations between Iran and Oman over the Strait of Hormuz are approaching a final agreement. Recent regional reporting has also indicated that Tehran and Muscat are nearing a maritime management arrangement for the waterway, although wider political issues remain unresolved.

According to local sources, anti-ship missiles were launched toward the Strait of Hormuz. At the same time, three to four U.S. Air Force aerial refueling aircraft were reportedly operating over the Gulf, suggesting that an operation involving oil tankers was underway. Local reports further claimed that tankers attempting to transit through the southern corridor were not visible on the AIS tracking system, fueling speculation that the vessels were moving under restricted or concealed tracking conditions.

Strait of Hormuz

Maritime tensions remain high around the Strait of Hormuz, where shipping traffic has been significantly reduced following recent attacks on commercial vessels and continuing military escalation. International shipping data has shown a sharp decline in vessel transits through the strait in recent days.

🇸🇾 Occupied Syria

Israeli occupation forces carried out raids in several areas of the occupied West Bank overnight.

Occupation forces entered the town of Allar north of Tulkarm, while separate raids were launched in Saida, north of Tulkarm, and Beit Dajan, east of Nablus.

According to local Palestinian sources, occupation forces arrested Palestinian prisoner Sohaib Abu Thabet after raiding his home in Saida.

Israeli forces also raided homes near the village of Tal, southwest of Nablus, and reportedly seized several Palestinian civilian vehicles.

🇮🇶 Iraq

Iraq’s National Security Service announced the launch of a nationwide security operation codenamed “Shadow of Justice.”

The operation will cover all Iraqi provinces. Authorities have not yet released further details regarding its objectives or the groups being targeted.

Later, preliminary Iraqi reports indicated an RPG and gunfire attack on a Popular Mobilization Forces position behind the Tuz Khurmatu mountains, with PMF forces responding to the attack.

🇪🇬 🇨🇳 Egypt & China

Egypt and China have begun a joint aerial military exercise in Egypt titled “Eagles of Civilization 2026.”

The exercise marks a new phase of military cooperation between Cairo and Beijing and involves joint air operations and training activities.

🇱🇧 Lebanon

Local reports documented a day of extensive Israeli military activity across southern Lebanon.

Airstrikes were reported in Wadi Sarbin, Al-Mansouri, the outskirts of Sarbin, Ali al-Taher, Douhat Kafr Rumman, and the outskirts of Deir Sirian.

Artillery shelling targeted Ali al-Taher, the vicinity of Al-Dabsha with phosphorus munitions, Douhat Kafr Rumman, Mays al-Jabal, Houla, and Al-Mansouri.

🇵🇸 Palestine

Al-Qabbanji also referred to the arrest of Mahdi Zuhair after he publicly recited the Ziyarat of Imam Husayn (peace be upon him), calling it another example of religious persecution.

No organization has claimed responsibility for the attack, and police said the perpetrators have not yet been identified.

Democratic Republic of the Congo
