Hungary's only nuclear power plant shut down due to water shortage

Hungarian Prime Minister Péter Magyar announced the closure of the country's nuclear power plant due to a severe drop in the water level of the Danube River, and warned of electricity restrictions and possible penalties for consumers.

The Paks nuclear power plant, located 120 kilometers south of the capital Budapest, uses water from the Danube to cool its reactors. This power plant provides more than 40% of Hungary's electricity production.

The second objection concerns the mechanism for monitoring the implementation of the agreement; Israel refuses to involve Qatar and Turkey in the verification committee that will evaluate the implementation of each stage before moving to the next stage, and ensure the commitment of all parties to what they have pledged.
The third objection concerns security responsibility in the areas that will be transferred to the supervision of the international stabilization force and the Palestinian police affiliated with the National Committee for the Administration of Gaza.
