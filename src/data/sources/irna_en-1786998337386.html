🔻 New US sanctions will not dampen Iran’s resolve: FM spox

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has said that the proposed new sanctions by the United States will not weaken Iran’s determination.


Washington has long been entangled in a self-defeating pattern of conflict, coercion, and punitive measures—a cycle that has persisted since the 1953 coup and over the past five decades, Baqaei told reporters at his regular press conference on Monday.
​
During the Democrats’ tenure, the so‑called crippling sanctions were introduced, and later, during US President Donald Trump’s first term, the maximum pressure campaign was launched—both of which laid bare the inhumane character of US policy, he said.
​
The spokesperson explained that Iran has long anticipated various scenarios in which the US might persist in its hostile actions. Accordingly, the necessary measures have already been put in place, and Iran will not hesitate to deploy all available resources to confront any emerging situation.
​
Bahrain should study UN Security Council Resolution
​
In response to a question regarding Bahrain’s recent letter of protest to the United Nations concerning the spokesperson’s X post—which referred to respect for the national sovereignty of all regional countries, including the newly established governments that have seceded from Iranian territory—and Manama’s demand for respect for its sovereignty, Baqaei said he did not name any country in that message.
​
Iran respects the national sovereignty and territorial integrity of all regional countries, he added.
​
He also reminded the Bahraini side, as well as Iranian historians and those of the region, that a resolution was adopted by the UN Security Council on May 11, 1970, under Resolution 278, based on which a decision was made—on the basis of the UN Secretary‑General’s Report No. 9772—to resolve the dispute between the two governments at the time, Britain and Iran.
​
He reiterated that Iran respects the sovereignty of all countries in the region, in line with the principle of good neighborliness.
​
These issues have been raised mainly because some countries in the region have failed to uphold good neighborly conduct and have overlooked historical and geographic realities, Baqaei noted.
​
“How can any nation claim to uphold the principle of good neighborliness when it places its soil and resources at the service of the Zionist regime and America to strike a neighboring Muslim country? Is that what respect for a neighbor looks like?” he asked.

📡 New flight route launched between Iran and Pakistan

📌 Islamabad, IRNA – Iran's Kish Air has launched a new weekly flight route between Mashhad and Karachi, operating every Monday as a round-trip service.


The flight departed from the holy city of Mashhad on Monday morning, August 17, with 140 passengers onboard, arriving at Karachi’s Jinnah International Airport. Later in the day, almost the same number of passengers were transported back from Karachi to Mashhad.
​
Karachi, the capital of Sindh province, is Pakistan’s most populous city and has long maintained strong trade, cultural, tourism, political and people-to-people ties with various Iranian provinces.
​
In recent years, Iran’s Taban Air has also operated flights between Tehran and Karachi. Last year, direct Tehran-Islamabad flights were officially launched by Iranian airlines Mahan Air and Iran Airtour.

🎙 Yemeni forces target Saudi military ship, four boats in Red Sea

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Yemeni armed forces spokesman Brigadier General Yahya Saree says Yemeni forces targeted a Saudi-affiliated military ship and four accompanying military boats off the coast of Mokha in the Red Sea with ballistic missiles.


Quoted by Yemeni Al-Masirah television on Monday, Saree said the military ship and four boats accompanying it were struck by several ballistic missiles, adding that the strikes were “precise and direct.”
​
He said the military ship was completely set ablaze following the attack, while several of the accompanying boats sank and the remaining boats caught fire.
​
Saree also said Yemeni armed forces were closely monitoring the movements and concentrations of Saudi forces.
​
He warned that any movement or concentration of Saudi forces, whether on land or at sea, will be targeted.
​
He further said Yemeni armed forces would continue implementing the “siege-for-siege equation” and would target Saudi forces’ positions and concentrations wherever they were located.
​
Separately, Saree said Yemeni forces carried out a drone strike against a weapons depot belonging to Saudi-affiliated forces at the Sahn al-Jan camp in Marib province.
​
He said the drone attack was carried out accurately and caused the weapons depot to catch fire.
​
The attacks followed a renewed escalation in fighting in recent days between Yemeni armed forces and forces affiliated with the Saudi-backed regime across several fronts in Yemen.
​
Yemeni armed forces have carried out missile and drone attacks against positions and camps belonging to Saudi-affiliated forces in Marib and Hadramout provinces.

⚡️ Aref says enemies seek to destabilize Iran through economic, social pressure

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Vice President Mohammad-Reza Aref says the enemies aim to weaken national unity through economic pressure and social divisions.


During a meeting with media representatives on Monday, Aref said that, in light of the recent military defeats suffered by adversaries, their strategy now focuses on undermining the country’s unity, adding that the enemies are attempting to achieve this by applying economic pressure and exploiting social divisions.
​
He emphasized that the government has prioritized enhancing national resilience in response to these challenges.
​
The vice president also added that the media bear a responsibility to counter psychological warfare and present the “first narrative”, stressing that rising prices, which are now a reality, must not become a source of social instability.
​
He described reforms to economic policies, including subsidies and gasoline prices, as necessary, saying that these changes should be implemented gradually, guided by expert assessments, and accompanied by effective public communication to avoid increasing economic pressure on citizens.
​
Aref stressed that the government is prepared for difficult circumstances, including the possibility of war, adding that the priorities for the next two years are maintaining relative market stability, ensuring the people’s livelihoods, and enhancing the country’s resilience.

🔻 IRGC vows decisive response to any threat or aggression

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has vowed to use all of its capabilities to defend Iran’s territorial integrity, independence, and national interests, saying it will respond decisively to any threat or act of aggression.


In a statement issued on Monday to mark the anniversary of the return of Iranian prisoners of war (POWs) following the 1980–1988 Iran–Iraq War, the IRGC described them as symbols of patience, resistance and steadfastness.
​
It emphasized that the memories and sacrifices of POWs create a lasting legacy and serve as an inspiration for future generations, as well as a testament to the injustices endured and the legitimacy of the Islamic Republic of Iran.

🖥 Exclusive | Foreign Ministry Spokesperson calls drone attack on Iraqi Kurdistan PM’s office ‘highly suspicious’

📌 Tehran, IRNA — The Foreign Ministry spokesperson has labeled the recent drone attack on the office of Masrour Barzani, Prime Minister of the Iraqi Kurdistan Region, a “highly suspicious development,” urging regional vigilance to thwart attempts to destabilize ties between Iran and Iraq.


In response to a query from IRNA on Monday regarding media reports of the attack, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Ismaeil Baqaei condemned the incident, characterizing the strike as a deliberate provocation that requires careful investigation.
​
“This development is highly suspicious and requires all sides to exercise vigilance,” Baqaei said. He emphasized that the incident appears intended to harm the “brotherly relations” between Iran and Iraq, noting that both nations must cooperate to neutralize the “sinister plots” of mutual adversaries seeking to drive a wedge between them.
​
The spokesperson further pointed to recent warnings issued by Iraqi officials — including the country’s national security advisor — regarding the presence of foreign-affiliated cells in the region.
​
According to Iraqi intelligence, these groups have been involved in planning and executing “false-flag” operations, he said.
