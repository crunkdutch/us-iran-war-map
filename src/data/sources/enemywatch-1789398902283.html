🇾🇪 ❌ 🇸🇦 🇦🇪 | Current updates: (Ongoing)

🇾🇪 Yemen

- The Royal Saudi Air Force reportedly carried out repeated airstrikes across southwestern Yemen, targeting Mokha, Jabal al-Nar, and the Mokha-Dhubab road in areas under Ansar Allah control. Additional strikes were also reported on the city of Mokha itself as the aerial campaign continued.

- Yemen’s Ministry of Health stated that the death toll from the Saudi strike on the Central Correctional Facility in Al-Jawf Governorate has risen to 35 people killed and 7 injured. The ministry described the victims as casualties of the Saudi attack on the prison facility.

- Ansar Allah announced that its forces seized additional military vehicles and equipment belonging to Saudi-backed forces after their withdrawal from frontline positions and the capture of coastal areas. The group said the captured equipment included several military vehicles and anti-tank missiles.

- Yemeni forces also reportedly launched missiles toward positions held by Saudi-backed militias, while a loud explosion was reported in the city of Taiz later in the evening.

🇾🇪 Marib Front

- Heavy fighting was reported across the Marib front as Ansar Allah continued offensive operations against Saudi-backed forces. Clashes were reported north, west, and northwest of Marib, including in the al-Uqdat area east of Fay’a Hill.

- Reports from the battlefield claimed Ansar Allah forces advanced toward the Atran Heights, with some suggesting they had moved roughly 9 miles beyond their previously reported positions near Tabat Alam. However, control of the heights remained unconfirmed at the time of reporting.

- Further fighting was reported around the Al-Balaq Heights and the approaches to the Great Marib Dam. Separate reports said Ansar Allah launched repeated assaults from the town of Yara toward Balaq al-Gharbi, while other analysts described the attacks as aggressive probing operations intended to identify weak points in Saudi-backed defensive lines.

- Several reports also claimed that dozens of trucks belonging to the Saudi-backed Presidential Leadership Council (PLC) were seen evacuating areas outside Marib as clashes intensified.

🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

Saudi-backed gatherings east of Al-Jawf were reportedly targeted, with claims that dozens of military vehicles were destroyed by fire. These claims were circulated by pro-Ansar Allah sources and had not been independently verified.

Saudi Arabia also condemned the drone strike on its East-West oil pipeline, stating that the attack originated from Iraq. Earlier reports indicated that the pipeline had been targeted on Thursday, although Iraqi responsibility has not been independently confirmed.

🌊 Maritime Situation

Reports circulating in regional media claimed that shipping routes through the Red Sea have been severely disrupted by the Yemeni blockade, forcing some tankers that previously reached the Arabian Sea in about 4 days to reroute around the Cape of Good Hope, extending voyages to roughly 34 days. The reports also linked the disruption to recent attacks on commercial vessels near the southern Strait of Hormuz, though the broader shipping impact remains contested and varies by operator.
