Hungary's only nuclear power plant shut down due to water shortage

Hungarian Prime Minister Péter Magyar announced the closure of the country's nuclear power plant due to a severe drop in the water level of the Danube River, and warned of electricity restrictions and possible penalties for consumers.

The Paks nuclear power plant, located 120 kilometers south of the capital Budapest, uses water from the Danube to cool its reactors. This power plant provides more than 40% of Hungary's electricity production.
