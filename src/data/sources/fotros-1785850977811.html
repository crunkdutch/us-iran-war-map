✍ For all the non-military knowledged people:

A ballistic missile is a bit like a delivery truck and its package.

• The booster is the rocket part. Its job is to push the missile up and forward at very high speed.

• The warhead is the actual payload (with explosives) at the front. That is the part meant to hit the target.

What usually happens is:

1️⃣ The booster launches the missile and gives it speed and altitude.

2️⃣ The booster separates mid air

3️⃣ The warhead keeps going toward the target on its own path.

-> Israel intercepted the booster part which is useless and does not affect the missile’s target.
