⚡️ 🔺Musk's Claim of Restoring Eyesight to "Superhuman Vision"
🔹Elon Musk has announced that Neuralink plans to test its first chip designed to restore eyesight in humans within the next 6 to 12 months. According to him, this chip could even help people who have been blind since birth, as it sends visual information directly to the visual cortex of the brain instead of repairing the eye or optic nerve.
🔹Although experts say that restoring eyesight to all people who have been blind since birth is not that simple.
🔹Musk has also spoken about the idea of "superhuman vision," meaning the ability to see infrared, ultraviolet light, or information that the human eye is not naturally capable of observing.
🔹Theoretically, special sensors could capture such information and convert it into signals that the brain learns to interpret, but so far there is no credible clinical evidence to suggest that humans can achieve such abilities using brain chips.
