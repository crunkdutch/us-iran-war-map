🇲🇲: 🏴| Muharram commemorations in Myanmar draw thousands of participants:

In Myanmar, Muharram observances are organized primarily by the country’s small Twelver Shi’a Muslim community, with major gatherings taking place in Yangon and Mandalay. The historic Panja Mosque in Yangon serves as a focal point for commemorations, where thousands of devotees gather for mourning ceremonies, religious sermons, marsiya recitations, matam, and communal Niyaz gatherings.
