🇮🇷: 🇮🇷 | Funeral prayer for Imam Khamenei to be held Sunday morning:

The official Media Office of the Funeral Committee for the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r), has announced that the funeral prayer over the pure body of the martyred Imam and his family will be held on Sunday at 6:00 a.m. (local time) at Imam Khomeini Musalla in Tehran.

The Central Committee for Commemorating the Martyrdom of Grand Ayatollah Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r) had previously announced that farewell ceremonies would take place on Saturday, July 4, and Sunday, July 5 (corresponding to 19 and 20 Muharram) at Imam Khomeini Musalla in Tehran.

The funeral procession will begin in Tehran on Monday, July 6, continue in Qom on Tuesday, July 7, and conclude in Mashhad on Thursday, July 9 (24 Muharram), where the pure body will be laid to rest in the shrine of Imam Ali al Ridha (A).

Farewell ceremonies for the martyred leader will also be held in Najaf and Karbala, Iraq, on Wednesday, July 8.
