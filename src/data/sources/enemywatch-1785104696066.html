🥀 | Mothers of Minab

They wake before the dawn,
not because the morning calls,
but because sleep
no longer remembers them.

The beds remain untouched,
the schoolbags still waiting,
tiny shoes rest by the door
as if small feet
might return at any moment.

They set another place
at tables that have forgotten laughter,
listening for voices
the wind can never bring back.

The world keeps turning,
yet time has stopped
inside their homes.

No court speaks their children’s names.
No nation carries their grief.
No procession marches
for the little hands
that slipped away too soon.

Only the mothers remember.

They remember
the first cry,
the first smile,
the first day of school,
the last embrace
that neither they nor their children
knew would be the last.

The powerful bargain.
The diplomats speak.
The headlines fade.

But a mother’s memory
does not negotiate.

It does not surrender.

Every tear
is a witness.

Every prayer
is an indictment.

Every silence
calls the names
the world chose not to hear.

The mothers of Minab
do not ask
for sympathy.

They ask
that their children
not be buried twice:

once beneath the earth,

and once
beneath the silence
of the world.


(Illustration by Iranian artist Marzieh Asadpour)

— Saudi Arabia’s major blunder for the umpteenth time!

The downing of the heavy Saudi Bayraktar Akıncı drone over the skies of Al Jawf is considered a significant and, of course, relatively straightforward success for Yemen’s air defenses. The Akıncı, with a wingspan of approximately 20 meters and a multi-ton takeoff weight, is a strategic combat drone designed for long range reconnaissance, electro-optical surveillance, and the deployment of guided munitions.

Its large dimensions, greater radar cross section, and operation at relatively high altitudes make it more vulnerable to radar detection, optical tracking, and surface-to-air missile systems. This incident also demonstrates that Saudi intelligence and offensive operations deep inside Al Jawf, without effective electronic warfare support and suppression of enemy air defenses, can come at a very high cost.
