📌 Tehran, IRNA — The High Council for Human Rights of the Islamic Republic of Iran has, in a statement, described the Islamic Human Rights and Human Dignity Day as a symbol of resistance by Islamic nations against Western hegemony and cultural and political domination.

It also holds that the recent hostile actions by global powers against Islam only further confirm the truth of the Islamic faith.

🎙 Iran, Pakistan reaffirm $10bn trade goal, advance free trade agreement

📌 Islamabad, IRNA – Iran and Pakistan have signed a memorandum of understanding to expand bilateral trade, reaffirming their commitment to raising annual trade to $10 billion and advancing negotiations toward a free trade agreement.


The memorandum was signed during the closing session of the 10th Joint Trade Committee in Islamabad on Wednesday, attended by Iran’s Minister of Industry, Mine and Trade, Mohammad Atabak, and Pakistan’s Commerce Minister, Jam Kamal Khan.
​
The two sides discussed expanding commercial cooperation, particularly in shared border regions, developing maritime and shipping activities, coordinating steps toward a free trade agreement, establishing joint free trade zones, and promoting barter trade.
​
Atabak said negotiations over the past two days had focused on increasing commercial exchanges, adding that some areas required facilitation and the removal of obstacles, and that the Pakistani side agreed to pursue further follow-up.
​
He said key decisions included activating border regions, expanding cross-border trade, strengthening private-sector participation, exchanging technical delegations, and immediately implementing bilateral agreements.
​
“The Islamic Republic has no limitation on increasing the volume of trade with Pakistan,” Atabak said, calling on business communities in both countries to play a leading role.
​
A separate cooperation memorandum was also signed between the Iran Chamber of Commerce, Industries, Mines and Agriculture and the Federation of Pakistan Chambers of Commerce and Industry.
​
Atabak is also scheduled to meet Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif during his visit.

📡 Official urges Iran, Pakistan to move beyond goods trade toward economic partnership

📌 Tehran, IRNA — Vice President of the Iran Chamber of Commerce Hossein Pir-Moazzen has called on Tehran and Islamabad to progress from commodity trade to a broader economic partnership.


Speaking at the Iran-Pakistan Business Conference in Islamabad on Wednesday, Pir-Moazzen said Tehran and Islamabad must move beyond mere trade and, by strengthening cooperation, develop a suitable model to enhance their economic relations.
​
He also highlighted the importance of utilizing the port capacities of Chabahar and Karachi in trade.
​
He called for reducing clearance times and facilitating the release of goods, reducing stoppages at border areas, developing cooperation in transportation and corridors, creating a database of private-sector actors from both sides, providing a list of goods that both countries can trade, and ultimately removing tariff and non-tariff barriers.
​
He lauded Iran’s unique industrial position in the region, and stressed the possibility of joint production and investment between Iran and Pakistan.
​
The Iranian and Pakistani private sectors are ready to multiply trade; however, to achieve this, barriers that have existed for years on trade between the two countries must be removed.
​
Pir-Moazzen reiterated the need to resolve banking and money transfer issues.
​
Elsewhere in his remarks, he emphasized the importance of removing barriers in transportation and logistics.
​
Cargo routes between the two countries must become faster, cheaper, and more predictable, he said.
​
Accordingly, developing cooperation between ports, railways, road transport, and especially utilizing the capacities of Chabahar and Karachi, can develop Iran-Pakistan trade, he stated.
​
Pir-Moazzen proposed establishing a dispute resolution and arbitration mechanism between the chambers of commerce of the two countries.
​
Iran can offer significant capacities to Pakistan’s private sector in energy, petrochemicals, food industries, agriculture, technology, and industrial equipment fields, he said.
​
Pakistan also has a large market and can serve as a gateway for Iranian companies to South Asian markets, he stressed.
​
He highlighted Iran’s geographical position to provide access routes for Pakistani companies to the markets of Central Asia, the Caucasus, Iraq, and the Persian Gulf countries.

🖥 Russian reporter describes Arbaeen as “amazing and incredible” ritual

📌 Moscow, IRNA – A Russian television reporter, who is visiting Iraq to cover the Arbaeen procession, has described the event as “amazing and incredible”, saying that despite being a mourning ritual, Arbaeen has a truly amazing atmosphere.


While reporting from among the crowd of Arbaeen pilgrims, Sarkar Magomedov, Russia’s Channel One reporter, said that every year millions of Muslims from across the world gather in the holy city of Karbala on the occasion of Arbaeen.
​
Despite the scorching heat of Iraq, people from more than 80 countries – a huge number of the elderly, women, and children among them – have traveled to this country to take part in the Arbaeen ritual, and in the extremely hot weather, they walk dozens of kilometers on foot along the roads leading to Karbala, he reported.
​
According to statistics provided, about 25 million people participate in the Arbaeen march in Iraq, which has turned it into one of the largest marches in the world, Magomedov added.
​
Arbaeen is one of the most sacred observances for Shia Muslims worldwide. It commemorates the sacrifice of Imam Hussein (PBUH), who was martyred in the 680 AD Battle of Karbala alongside 17 of his family members and more than 70 loyal companions, after refusing to pledge allegiance to Yazid, the Umayyad ruler.
