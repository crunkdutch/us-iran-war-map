In the longer term, planners envision extending the corridor toward Oman and the Indian Ocean, creating an alternative trade route that could reduce dependence on maritime chokepoints such as the Strait of Hormuz. However, many observers argue that this project is unlikely to achieve its broader strategic objectives. While it may improve regional connectivity on paper, it does not fundamentally address the economic, political, and security realities required for such a corridor to operate successfully over the long term.
