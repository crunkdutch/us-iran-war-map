⚡️ Media Office of the Presidency of the Republic:
The President of the Republic, General Joseph Aoun, congratulated the army, its command, officers, non-commissioned officers, and individuals on Army Day, which falls today, August 1st.

President Aoun said on the occasion:
The Lebanese Army is not an institution like other institutions; rather, it is the sole guarantor of Lebanon's unity, sovereignty, and independence.
The time has come for all of us to solidify this constant constitutional and national truth:
There is no legitimacy for any weapon outside the legitimacy of the state, and there is no protection for this homeland except through its inclusive national army, which represents all Lebanese, regardless of their regions, sects, and affiliations.
The army alone possesses the decision, power, and legitimacy to be the exclusive military arm of the state.
This is what we are working on steadily and persistently, step by step, to ensure the safety of coexistence and the stability of the country.
President Aoun added:

In the South, where our army wrote the most wonderful pages of sacrifice and steadfastness...
Today, the army's responsibility doubles with every step of Israeli withdrawal from Lebanese territory.
The next phase requires a full deployment of military units along the entire southern border and the exclusive extension of state authority over its territories...
This confirms to the world that Lebanon, with its army alone, is capable of protecting its sovereignty and controlling the security of this sensitive region, in preparation for the safe return of its people to their villages and towns.

If the army is carrying out this enormous role, then the Lebanese rallying around it is not a luxury but an existential necessity.
A strong army is not built by equipment alone, but by the trust of its people and their moral and material support.
Therefore, any questioning of the army institution or attempt to isolate it from its popular base weakens the entire state and threatens what remains of the elements of stability.
Therefore, I call on all Lebanese, at home and in the diaspora, to make the army their unifying red line, above all political or partisan differences, because the homeland should not be stripped of an army that defends it.

I reiterate, with full confidence and pride, full trust in the army command, which has proven in the most delicate and difficult circumstances that it is at a high level of responsibility, professionalism, and impartiality.
It managed sensitive files with wisdom and concern for the supreme national interest, far from any bidding or bias, and this strengthens the position of this institution in the conscience of all Lebanese.

As for the military personnel who guard our borders and our security under extremely difficult economic and living conditions...
I assure them that the Lebanese state, the presidency, the parliament, and the government, will continue with all its determination to work to improve their living and social conditions.
And to provide the necessary support for them and their families, because the dignity of the military is part of the dignity of the homeland.
And it is not permissible to ask them to sacrifice while they and their families are left vulnerable to destitution and deprivation.
We will continue to strive, in coordination with sisterly and friendly countries, to provide the support, equipment, and training befitting an ancient institution that has made and continues to make the most precious sacrifices.

Long live the army, long live Lebanon, and glory and eternity to our righteous martyrs.
