🇮🇶: 🇮🇷 | The funeral ceremonies of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r) concluded in Najaf and Karbala, with more than 25 million mourners participating as the sacred coffins departed for Mashhad.

Over the past five hours, the funeral procession carrying the blessed coffin of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Husayni Khamenei (r) passed through Abu Mahdi al Muhandis Street and Shatt al Abbas Street before entering Bayn al Haramain, where millions of mourners had gathered throughout every corner of the sacred precinct to receive their Imam and bid him a final farewell. The funeral procession in Karbala began more than four hours behind schedule due to the unprecedented crowds and continued for nearly twelve hours. Hours before the ceremonies began, every road leading to the Holy Shrines of Imam Husayn (A) and Hazrat Abul Fadl al Abbas (A) had already been filled with mourners. Before entering Karbala, millions of pilgrims participating in the Arbaeen walking route from Najaf accompanied the sacred coffin to the entrance of the city before joining the procession.

The procession then entered the Holy Shrine of Imam Husayn (A), where the blessed coffin was placed before the sacred grave of Imam Husayn (A). The fourth funeral prayer was led by the Chief Custodian of the shrine, Hujjat al Islam wal Muslimeen Sheikh Abdul Mahdi al Karbalai. Following the prayer, the sacred body was carried in circumambulation around the holy shrine of Imam Husayn (A).

The blessed coffin was then carried on the shoulders of mourners through Bayn al Haramain to the Holy Shrine of Hazrat Abul Fadl al Abbas (A), where millions of pilgrims and mourners were waiting. Another funeral prayer was led by Sayyid Ahmad al Safi. The shrine’s custodians then placed the sacred body before the holy shrine of Qamar Bani Hashim, Hazrat Abul Fadl al Abbas (A). The Ziyarat was recited, another funeral prayer was offered under the leadership of Hujjat al Islam Sayyid Ahmad al Safi, and a mourning gathering was held during which condolences were presented to Imam al Mahdi (AJ).

The ceremonies concluded at approximately 5:00 a.m. Tehran time, several hours later than originally planned. The sacred coffins had initially been expected to arrive in Mashhad at around 6:00 a.m., but the extraordinary crowds of millions gathered along the entire route caused significant delays throughout the journey.

According to preliminary estimates, more than 25 million mourners participated across Najaf and Karbala, with the flow of mourners continuing until the final moments of the ceremonies. Public holidays were declared across both holy cities to facilitate participation. Iraq’s entire leadership, including the President, Prime Minister, cabinet ministers, provincial governors, senior government officials, representatives of the Maraji’, tribal leaders, prominent social figures, and representatives of other religious communities attended the ceremonies. More than 3,000 international journalists covered the funeral in Karbala, while the ceremonies at the Holy Shrines of Imam Husayn (A) and Hazrat Abul Fadl al Abbas (A) were described as among the largest funeral gatherings in history.

The sacred coffins of Martyr Imam Sayyid Ali Khamenei (r) and his martyred family members have now departed from Najaf International Airport for Mashhad, where the final funeral procession is scheduled to begin at approximately 2:00 p.m. today, with millions more mourners expected to participate.
