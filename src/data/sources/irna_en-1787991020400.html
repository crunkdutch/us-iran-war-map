📌 Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Parliament Speaker Ali Nikzad has dismissed the US claim of an economic blockade on Iran as a “psychological operation,” stressing that any decision to end the war must be based on national security and interests, not pressure or war fatigue.


In an exclusive interview with IRNA on Friday, Nikzad described the US claim that Iran is under an economic blockade as part of a “psychological operation,” stressing that Iran cannot easily be isolated given its 15 neighboring countries, access to international waters and strategic position in the Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman.
​
He said that Iran’s regional, economic and geopolitical capacities, as well as its ties with neighboring countries and Asian powers, provide significant opportunities to neutralize external pressure.
​
He added that the country’s ability to maintain its institutions and continue decision-making and governance under such conditions demonstrated Iran’s social and political capacity to preserve cohesion and rebuild its strength. External pressure, he added, does not necessarily lead to Iran’s collapse or inaction.
​
Addressing the possibility of continuing the war and the president’s remarks on ending it, Nikzad said that a distinction must be made between the “ability to continue the war” and the “wisdom of continuing it.” He stressed that the war is not an end in itself; otherwise, the ultimate objective is to safeguard national security and interests.
​
He said that the criterion for peace should not be war fatigue or economic pressure, but the outcome achieved by ending the war. If ending the war helps consolidate security, reduce economic pressure, protect national interests and secure a clear outcome from the resistance, it should be welcomed, he added.
​
Nikzad warned, however, that Iran should not be naive if the adversary seeks to use negotiations to rebuild its capabilities or impose new conditions. Brigadier General Talaei-Nik spoke to IRNA’s political correspondent during a visit to the Islamic Republic News Agency (IRNA) on the occasion of Government Week, where he also met with IRNA Managing Director Hossein Jaberi Ansari.
​
He said that the Pezeshkian administration faced highly sensitive circumstances over the past two years, including two wars, security threats and rapid regional developments.
​
The Defense Ministry spokesperson said that despite the geographic scope of the attacks and the enemy’s heavy firepower, the government supported the Army, IRGC and the Central Khatam al-Anbiya Headquarters in meeting the needs of the Armed Forces, preventing the enemy from achieving its objectives.
​
Brigadier General Talaei-Nik said that the recent wars, despite their losses, had also created opportunities for political, social, scientific, industrial and defense-related progress. He added that the defense industry had built strategic stocks of weapons, equipment and ammunition before the war, while production continued during the war through a network of about 9,300 knowledge-based and private companies. According to Talaei-Nik, these companies support two-thirds of the defense industry, and production of some equipment increased several-fold.
​
He said that Iran’s arms production has more than doubled since the 12-day war, with several-fold increases targeted for some products. He also added that Iran is moving toward a leading global position in areas such as long-range and cruise missiles and drones, while arms exports will continue based on available surplus.
​
The Defense Ministry spokesperson stressed that Iran produces more than 1,000 types of weapons, equipment and ammunition, with defense self-sufficiency exceeding 90 percent. He said that the defense industry has moved beyond reverse engineering and achieved indigenous technologies and designs in some areas.
​
He described Iran’s resilience as the main “surprise” of the war, saying the enemy had expected the country’s military, defense, economic and social capabilities to collapse in the early days, but the opposite occurred.
​
Talaei-Nik said that Iran’s defense strategy remains combined and asymmetric, combining the preservation and strengthening of deterrence with offensive approaches within a defensive framework to prevent the enemy from seizing the initiative. He added that strengthened counter-drone defenses and lessons learned after the 12-day war significantly reduced Defense Ministry casualties in the subsequent war.

🌍 Iran’s security chief: Leader’s message is clear, beyond interpretation

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council Major General Mohsen Rezaei says the message of Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei on Government Week, is clear and beyond interpretation, serving as a guiding principle for all Iranian officials.


Rezaei wrote in a post on X on Friday that the Leader’s call to avoid “imaginary dualisms” and “Any action that harms social cohesion is nationally and religiously forbidden for all of us” provides a clear strategy for everyone.
​
Rezaei stressed that national unity and social cohesion are among the most fundamental elements of power and national security.
