⚡️ It seems that minor changes have been made in the design of underground structures in recent years. One of these changes could be the construction of a greater number of underground entrances. NEHSA engineering forces have been able to construct more than a sufficient number of entrances in some sites that meet the necessary conditions in recent years. This feature will make suppressing the base more difficult during wartime and naturally increases its operational capability.

To better understand the issue, these facilities can be compared with the newest base in the Khorramabad missile complex (first image), whose construction began around 2016. Despite extensive excavations, the said base initially had only two entrances at the beginning of its construction process, which eventually increased to three underground entrances with the completion of excavations. On the other hand, the construction of the new base in Alborz Province (second image) began around 2024, and according to the images provided, five underground entrances have been constructed there so far, while based on the excavated soil piles, the extent of its excavations is estimated to be much less than the new Khorramabad base.
