He went on to say that according to Islamic teachings, superiority is determined neither by speaking Arabic nor by geographical proximity to the birthplace of Islam, but by piety, citing a verse from the Qur'an stating that “Indeed the noblest of you in the sight of Allah is the most Godwary among you.” (49:13)
​
Baqaei further noted that no ruler has the right to claim representation of the “Prophetic mission of Muhammad (PBUH)” solely based on being Arab or Arabic-speaking, saying that such claims cannot substitute for adherence to the principles and teachings of Islam.

​
Referring to Quranic verses and sayings attributed to Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), Baqaei pointed out that Muslims should neither support nor align themselves with oppressors.
​
He questioned whether cooperating with forces that have carried out military attacks against a Muslim country or hosting and supporting “enemies of Muslims” was compatible with Islamic teachings on justice, neighborly relations and solidarity among Muslim nations.

“The answers to these questions are clearly stated in the Holy Qur'an, affirmed by the tradition of the Prophet, and testified to by the conscience of the Islamic Ummah,” he wrote.

⚡️ Iran’s population surpasses 87 million as urban growth continues

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s population has surpassed 87 million people, according to estimates by the Statistical Center of Iran, with the country’s population reaching 87,006,037 people.


The figure comes as the Statistical Center had previously estimated Iran’s population at around 86.56 million and projected it would reach 87.13 million by the end of the Iranian calendar year 1405 (March 2027).
​
Under the projection, more than 67 million people will live in urban areas, while the rural population will stand at around 20 million.
​

​
According to official statistics, Iran registered 892,227 births in the last year (March 21, 2025–March 20, 2026), while the number of registered deaths stood at approximately 459,984 during the same period.
​
The highest number of births was recorded in the summer of last year, while the largest number of deaths occurred in spring.

🖥 Iran’s top rights: US strike on Qeshm home grave violation of int’l humanitarian law

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s High Council for Human Rights has strongly condemned the latest US attacks on residential areas in Iran, which left civilian casualties, calling the aggression “a clear example of American human rights” and a grave violation of international humanitarian law.


In a statement released on Sunday, the council particularly condemned the attacks on Qeshm Island early Thursday, including a strike on a house that killed a child and his parents, and injured two other children. The attacks were part of a series of airstrikes the US military conducted across Iran in recent weeks.
​
The statement said deliberate or indiscriminate attacks on residential areas and civilian objects violate the core principles of international humanitarian law, including the principle of distinction reflected in the 1977 Additional Protocol I to the Geneva Conventions. It stressed that parties to an armed conflict are obligated to distinguish between military targets and civilians and direct attacks only against legitimate military objectives.
​
It added that attacks against civilians and civilian property, as well as indiscriminate or disproportionate strikes causing civilian casualties, are prohibited under international law and constitute serious violations of humanitarian norms.
​
Referring to Article 3 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and Article 6 of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights, the council said the right to life obliges states to prevent the arbitrary deprivation of life. It said the killing of members of a family inside their home represented a clear breach of that right and a disregard for human dignity.
​
The statement also criticized the silence, inaction, and double standards of many United Nations human rights mechanisms, arguing that such responses undermine the credibility of the international human rights system and contribute to impunity.
​
The council called on the UN Human Rights Council, the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights, special rapporteurs, and other relevant international mechanisms to explicitly condemn the attack and take “practical, effective, and tangible” measures to hold the “aggressor state” accountable, end impunity for those responsible, provide redress for victims, and prevent similar attacks in the future.
