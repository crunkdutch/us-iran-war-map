🔊 Exclusive | Recent war strengthened national unity, geopolitical awareness: Iran

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Spokesperson of the Army of the Islamic Republic of Iran, Brigadier General Mohammad Akraminia, has described strong government support for the Armed Forces during the country’s recent conflicts with the US and the Israeli regime as a key factor in maintaining military capabilities, while saying the wars have strengthened national unity and created greater geopolitical awareness among Iranians.


In an exclusive interview with IRNA on Sunday, Akraminia said the government had adequately met the Armed Forces’ budgetary and financial requirements despite Iran’s economic difficulties.
​
He added that cooperation between the government and Armed Forces remains strong, emphasizing that continued support is essential under the country’s current security conditions.
​
Akraminia identified strengthened national cohesion as one of the major outcomes of the recent war, saying Iranians had rallied around the country, its national interests and the leadership. He also highlighted a new geopolitical awakening, particularly greater public awareness of the strategic importance of the Strait of Hormuz and its potential influence on Iran’s foreign policy and regional position.
​
The Army spokesperson said the recent conflicts had also provided Iran’s Armed Forces with valuable operational experience and increased their confidence. He argued that the wars had exposed the aggressive nature of the United States and the Israeli regime to a wider international audience, while noting that Washington’s credibility as a major international power had declined in regional and global affairs.
​
Akraminia said Iran’s military doctrine had gradually shifted from a predominantly defensive posture toward a more proactive approach during the two recent wars. He stressed that this did not necessarily mean initiating conflicts, but rather maintaining the capability to respond rapidly and forcefully to aggression. He said recent operations had demonstrated Iran’s ability to conduct broader responses and, in certain circumstances, preemptive actions.
​
The spokesperson also pointed to recent appointments of senior military commanders as evidence of Iran’s determination to strengthen national defense. He said the new commanders’ extensive military backgrounds were intended to ensure effective leadership and send a clear message to both domestic and foreign audiences that the Islamic Republic remained committed to defending its territory and national interests.
​
He said the Army had used the current ceasefire period to rebuild damaged systems, introduce new capabilities and update training based on lessons learned from the recent conflicts. According to Akraminia, Iran is relying increasingly on domestic defense industries and military technologies to enhance its readiness and deterrence capabilities.
​
Akraminia also said the recent merger of the Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters with the General Staff of the Armed Forces was aimed at streamlining command structures, accelerating decision-making and improving coordination. He described unified command and faster responses to emerging threats as important advantages of the restructuring.
​
Regarding three Iranian Sukhoi-24 pilots whose whereabouts remain unclear, he said Iran is pursuing the matter through legal and diplomatic channels with Qatar and international counterparts. He called for greater responsibility and transparency from Qatar in clarifying their status in accordance with international conventions.

💬 President Pezeshkian: Iran’s unity surprised world, foiled adversaries’ plans

📌 Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has praised the Iranian people for maintaining national unity amid an unequal military, economic and security confrontation, saying the country’s resilience had surprised the world and frustrated its enemies’ plans.


Speaking at the beginning of Government Week on Sunday, the president said Iran had faced continuous war, sanctions, pressure, and assassinations since his administration took office in July 2024. He said adversaries had expected Iran to collapse under pressure, but the country instead demonstrated greater strength, solidarity and resilience.
​
He pointed to statements by US President Donald Trump regarding the imposition of the most severe sanctions on Iran, saying Tehran had remained committed to serving its people and overcoming the economic difficulties.
​
Pressure has failed to break national resolve
​
Pezeshkian said the recent memorandum of understanding reached after lengthy discussions reflected an expert consensus among military personnel, security officials and government authorities. He said the framework was based on the principles of expediency, dignity and wisdom, emphasizing that Iran’s resistance had been made possible by cooperation among institutions and society.
​
He also said Iran’s adversaries are pursuing an economic war alongside military and security pressure. According to President Pezeshkian, foreign pressure has included efforts to persuade Muslim governments not to conduct economic transactions with Iran in an attempt to force Tehran into submission.
​
He stressed that the confrontation should not be viewed solely as an exchange of missiles, arguing that economic pressure and attempts to undermine Iran’s internal cohesion were also components of the broader conflict. He praised Iranian citizens, including those critical of the government, for standing together during the crisis.
​
He called for national unity and coordinated decision-making, saying Iran’s strength depends on solidarity, consultation, expertise, and adherence to national leadership. He argued that once a decision is reached through proper discussion, all sides should support and implement it consistently.
​
The president also warned that Iran’s adversaries were seeking to exploit economic and social difficulties to create divisions inside the country. He pledged that the government would work to prevent polarization and address inflation and livelihood concerns despite external pressure.
​
He called for a nationwide effort to overcome the country’s challenges, saying Iran needs practical action, not advice. President Pezeshkian thanked the Armed Forces and the Iranian people for their resilience and vowed to continue serving the country while defending national dignity and unity.

🖥 Pezeshkian administration pursues ‘smart defense doctrine’ to safeguard security, sustainable development

📌 Tehran, IRNA – The administration of President Masoud Pezeshkian has placed national security and the strengthening of Iran’s defense capabilities at the heart of its approach to sustainable development on the grounds that security is a prerequisite for economic stability, investment, and national power.


Since taking office in late July 2024, the administration has faced a series of security challenges and has increasingly emphasized “active deterrence” in response to regional tensions and Israeli actions. Against this backdrop, it has viewed defense spending not merely as a current expense but as a strategic investment aimed at safeguarding political and economic stability.
​
The administration has pursued a combination of strategic realism and reliance on indigenous military technology, maintaining that stronger defense capabilities can provide a secure environment for economic growth. President Pezeshkian has repeatedly stressed the need to maintain the readiness of Iran’s Armed Forces and pledged support for defense modernization, logistical assistance and the development of operational infrastructure.
​
In one such instance, the president said his administration would support programs to strengthen the country’s deterrence capabilities. His executive deputy, Mohammad-Jafar Qaem-Panah, also said security was a prerequisite for people’s livelihoods, arguing that preserving defense capabilities was essential to protecting the country’s economic and social stability.
​
The administration’s support for the Armed Forces has also been reflected in its budget priorities. According to official figures, military and security sectors accounted for around 16 percent of the budget for the current Iranian calendar year that started on March 21, amid heightened security challenges and two recent rounds of wars waged by Israel and the United States.
​
Strengthening defense capabilities a strategic investment in national security
​
Iran’s ability to withstand military pressure cannot be measured solely by comparing defense budgets with those of major military powers. Despite the vast disparity in resources, Iran has relied on indigenous technologies and asymmetric capabilities to maintain deterrence and withstand pressure from adversaries.
​
An example is the US, where public debt is rising in the wake of the imposed war on Iran, creating challenges for the Trump administration, whose inability to achieve political objectives through military pressure on Iran, including over the Strait of Hormuz, has caused public backlash and also highlighted the limits of military power when confronted with determined resistance.
​
Against this backdrop, the Pezeshkian administration’s defense policy is presented as a “smart defense doctrine” centered on three priorities: safeguarding national security, advancing indigenous defense technologies and creating synergy between military power and diplomacy. Strengthening defense capabilities is not aimed at military expansion, but is viewed as a strategic investment in national security, stability and sustainable development.

📡 Pezeshkian: Islamabad MoU contains no sign of concession

📌 Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has described the Islamabad memorandum of understanding between Iran and the United States as “dignified” and free of any concession, saying Iran must use logic and maintain its strength to move beyond a state of “neither war nor peace.”


Speaking at a ceremony marking National Doctors’ Day in Tehran on Saturday, Pezeshkian said the agreement’s commitments were directed at the other side.
​
“I believe that one cannot continue with war forever. One day, a decision must be made to save the country from the state of ‘neither war nor peace,’” he said.
​
Pezeshkian said members of the Supreme National Security Council who had been involved in the matter considered the memorandum “the best agreement based on dignity, wisdom, and expediency.”
​
“Not even a single clause can be found in it that indicates capitulation, and all the commitments concern the other side,” he said.
​
“The Leader determines the policies, and we will follow the same path,” the president added.
​
Pezeshkian said Iran could address its problems through implementation of the memorandum “with logic and dignity,” while stressing that the country would not yield to its enemies.
​
He also argued that investment could not be attracted under conditions of war or “neither war nor peace,” saying, “Capital and money are the most delicate non-human beings that flee from any danger.”
​
The president also praised Iranians who stood by the country despite their grievances, saying that when the homeland was at stake, “we all work together and in one voice for Iran.”
​
The Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding was signed on June 18, 2026 and ended the US–Israeli war of aggression against Iran that began on February 28.

📄 Iran to inaugurate 122 fisheries projects this week: Official

📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Fisheries Organization has announced that 122 fisheries projects will be inaugurated across 23 provinces during Government Week, which starts on August 24.


Hamzeh Rostampour, head of the Fisheries Organization, made the announcement on Saturday, saying the new projects will increase national fisheries production by more than 59,000 tons and create over 27,000 direct and indirect jobs.
​
According to the official, the projects cover a range of areas, including fishing vessel operations, warm-water, cold-water and sturgeon farming, cage fish farming, shrimp farming, jetty construction, feed production, seafood packaging and processing, ornamental fish farming, and leech farming.
​
He said Hormozgan Province in southern Iran ranks first in terms of investment despite wartime conditions. The projects to be inaugurated in the province include fleet development, vessel equipment, shrimp farming and the restructuring of aquaculture farms, he added.
