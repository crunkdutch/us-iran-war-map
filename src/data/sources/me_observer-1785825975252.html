⚡️ ▪️ For some time, the Director-General of the Agricultural Scientific Research Department at the Ministry of Agriculture, Jad Shaaya, has refrained from returning large samples of meat, chicken, and food items to their owners after their examination is completed, which has exacerbated the dispute between the Ministry and importers and traders.
According to concerned sources, Shaaya relies on texts that oblige traders to subject their goods to laboratory tests before offering them in the markets. However, he does not return the samples after the examination is completed; instead, he keeps them in the possession of the department before distributing them to other parties, in a move that opponents say is carried out with the cover of Minister of Agriculture Nizar Hani.
This matter provoked an official objection from food sector unions, which demanded the return of the samples, noting that they are not symbolic quantities but rather constitute a part of the imported shipments and incur significant financial costs. However, according to the sources, the Minister did not respond to these demands, which prompted importers to pass these losses on to commodity prices, a matter that directly affects consumers.
