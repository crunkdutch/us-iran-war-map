🇾🇪 | Latest updates from the Yemen war:

1. Missile attacks on southern Saudi Arabia continue

Missile attacks from Yemen are currently ongoing, with another wave of missiles striking Khamis Mushait and Abha in southern Saudi Arabia. Saudi media reported that air raid warnings in Abha and Khamis Mushait remained active for nearly one hour after the alarm was triggered for the third time, indicating continued missile activity over southern Saudi Arabia. Hours later, fresh explosions were reported in Abha, followed by the renewal of air raid sirens as additional missile attacks continued.

2. Saudi friendly-fire strike kills allied forces

Saudi-backed forces in Yemen reported that the Royal Saudi Air Force accidentally bombed its own allies, the Southern Giants Brigades, on the Bab al-Mandab coast. The mistaken airstrike reportedly caused dozens of casualties among the UAE-backed forces operating in the area.

3. Damage to Saudi oil infrastructure

Satellite imagery reportedly revealed extensive damage to Saudi Arabia’s strategic East-West oil pipeline following the recent attacks. Reports state that Saudi authorities did not disclose the extent of the damage to the pipeline leading to the Red Sea. Oil buyers and traders reportedly believe the kingdom may be forced to draw down oil stocks designated for export because of the disruption of one of its main export routes. According to the reports, prolonged disruption of the pipeline could affect up to 4% of global oil supplies, while satellite images showed a pumping station appearing heavily burned and severely damaged. <Cite refs={[“turn0news10”,“turn0news5”]}/>

4. Saudi missile strikes on Yemen

Overnight, Saudi Arabia reportedly launched several Chinese-made DF-15 short-range ballistic missiles toward AnsarAllah-controlled areas in Yemen, with multiple missiles striking their intended targets. One missile reportedly malfunctioned, with its booster landing in a populated area of western Marib under Saudi-backed PLC control. The number of civilian casualties remains unknown. Separate claims that the missile belonged to AnsarAllah were described as false, with reports stating there is no evidence that AnsarAllah possesses Chinese DF-15 missile systems.

5. UAE acknowledges military casualties

The UAE Ministry of Defense officially acknowledged the deaths of two Emirati soldiers, identifying them as Abdullah Shams Al-Menhali and Saeed Abdullah Al-Shamsi. The ministry stated that both were killed while carrying out a military mission.

6. Withdrawal of Saudi-backed PLC forces

Reports indicate a coordinated withdrawal by Yemeni government forces from parts of the frontline. Images circulating from the withdrawal reportedly showed M-ATV armored vehicles, each valued at approximately $410,000, being moved as Saudi-backed forces repositioned.

7. Intensified Saudi aerial activity

Saudi warplanes continued intense aerial activity over Taif as the Yemen war entered another phase of sustained missile exchanges and air operations between Saudi Arabia and AnsarAllah.
